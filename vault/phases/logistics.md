# Phase: logistics

She said yes in principle. Now: when, where, how. Goal: lock a real
meet without losing momentum to scheduling theatre.

## You're in this phase when

- She agreed to meet (explicitly or near-explicitly).
- The conversation is now about coordinating, not connecting.

## What works

- Two-option format: "Thursday or Friday?" beats open-ended.
- Suggesting a specific place that fits both your locations.
- Confirming the day-of with one short message.

## What doesn't

- Volleying scheduling indefinitely.
- Vague "let me know when you're free".
- Over-detailing the plan before she's confirmed.

## Strategies in this phase

- `logistics-two-option-close` — "Thursday or Friday?". Default when
  the day isn't locked.
- `logistics-specific-place` — propose one place + one time. Use when
  she said "you pick" or she's a planner.
- `logistics-day-of-confirm` — short matter-of-fact day-of message.
  Use 4-8h before, not earlier or later.

## Goal-type interactions

- All meeting-oriented goals share this phase.
- `decline-gracefully` should never reach this phase.

## Phase exit

→ `confirm` — specific time AND place are agreed.
→ `recovery` — scheduling stalled or she went quiet.
→ `dead` — she pulled out without a reschedule.
