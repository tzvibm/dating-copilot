# Phase: logistics

## Psychological objective

Logistics is where **autonomy-supportive choice architecture** (A16)
and **commitment-consistency** (A3) replace tension as the dominant
dynamics. The misattribution work is over; the meet is
psychologically agreed. The job now is to convert agreed-in-principle
into a specific calendared event without losing the warmth that got
her to yes.

The dominant subconscious dynamic is **Voss's "no as safety"** (A17).
Counterintuitively, the meet rate is higher when she can decline
without social cost — *"totally fine if thursday's a no"* outperforms
*"you should come."* Letting her say no makes her feel in control,
which paradoxically increases yes-rate. The mechanism is autonomy
(A16): forced compliance reads as coercion, freely-chosen compliance
reads as her decision and ratchets self-perception (A11) — *I'm doing
this because I want to*.

The text-only constraint shifts here. Through escalation, ambiguity
served the seduction (Greene's mixed signals, the Coquette pendulum,
the future-pace). At logistics, ambiguity inverts. Every coded
phrasing, every "let me know" hedge, every "if you want" softener
reads as scared. Greene's principle that clarity becomes the
seduction at the close (G "moving in for the kill") is operationally
true: the man who has been playful for ten turns and then drops a
specific, clean, frame-confident logistics message reads as decisive
precisely because the contrast.

**Voss labels** (A17) and **mirrors** are the highest-leverage
toolkit at this phase. *"Sounds like tuesday is brutal"* validates and
defuses without entering an argument; *"the noisy one"* (echo of her
last words) elicits elaboration without pressure. **I7 frame
control** matters disproportionately — scheduling theatre is one of
the most common places frame collapses, with him bending around her
calendar across multiple messages until the asymmetry has eroded the
prior status work.

The constraint: this phase is **brief by design**. Long logistics
threads kill momentum. Match-decay (the longer between agreement
and meet, the lower the meet probability) operates whether or not
you can feel it. Two messages to lock the meet is healthy. Five-plus
is a signal something else is going on — usually that escalation
wasn't actually 60.

**Objective.** Lock specific time + specific place in two-to-three
messages. **A16 forks-with-options** for choice architecture, **A17
labels** for any friction that surfaces, **A7 real scarcity** to
keep the window felt, **A10 sociometer-positive** baseline (he's
indifferent to whether *this specific time* works — there'll be
others). Frame stays his throughout: he's proposing the shape of the
meet, she's confirming or adjusting within it.

## Phase-progress rubric (this dimension)

| Score | What it looks like | Psychological reading |
|---|---|---|
| **0-30** | No time, no place. The "yes" hasn't been operationalised. Conversation drifted back to chat after the agreement. | Commitment-consistency (A3) is at risk — every turn that doesn't move toward specifics weakens her self-perception (A11) of "I'm doing this." Soft yes is decaying. |
| **30-60** | Day floated but not pinned. "Thursday-ish works" / "later this week probably". One axis (time *or* place) firmed; the other vague. | Half-locked. Autonomy frame (A16) holding — she's co-authoring. Risk: drifting indefinitely on the unpinned axis. Time to converge with a Voss-labeled close. |
| **60-100** | Specific time AND place agreed. Day, time, venue named. Both parties have signed off. | Full commitment-consistency lock. Self-perception (A11) cemented — she's now publicly (to herself) the kind of woman going on this date. Phase exits to confirm. |

**Hard prerequisite gate:** confirm requires logistics ≥ 60. Without
both axes locked, "see you thursday" doesn't have an anchor — the
day-of confirmation has nothing to confirm. Trying to confirm
half-locked logistics is a signal that escalation→logistics rushed
through the gate.

## What advances this dimension (mechanisms)

- **A16 autonomy via two-option close.** "Thursday or friday?" beats
  open-ended. Two specific options frame her as co-author (autonomy-
  supportive) without paralysing choice. Forces convergence on time
  axis in one turn.
- **A17 Voss labels.** "Sounds like tuesdays are brutal." "Seems like
  thursday's the move." Labels validate and partially defuse her
  emotional state; affect-labeling fMRI work shows amygdala dampening.
  Lowers friction without entering argument.
- **A17 mirrors.** Echo her last 1-3 words. "The noisy one?" elicits
  elaboration with minimal pressure. Highest-leverage low-effort
  move when she's hedging.
- **A17 calibrated questions.** "What would make this actually fun?"
  shifts cognitive load to her while preserving autonomy (A16).
  Better than "is this place okay?" — the latter is yes/no theatre,
  the former produces real signal.
- **A17 "no" as safety.** "Totally fine if thursday's a no" beats
  "you should come." Removing pressure increases yes-rate. The
  mechanism is autonomy (A16) — freely-chosen compliance ratchets
  self-perception (A11), forced compliance erodes it.
- **A3 commitment-consistency.** Once she's said yes-in-principle,
  every specific micro-confirmation (day, place, time) compounds.
  Self-perception (A11) does the rest invisibly. The structure of
  the messages is "we're doing this — these are the details", not
  "are we doing this?"
- **A7 real scarcity.** Real time-bounded windows ("I'm in town
  through saturday") keep the felt urgency without manufactured
  pressure. Authority by leak (A5) when the scarcity comes from
  real life — work, travel, plans — never from theatre.
- **I7 frame control.** Most common collapse point. She delays a
  reply to scheduling, he sends a follow-up softener that hands
  her the frame ("just checking in!"). Holds: send nothing, or send
  one labeled close ("seems like the week's hectic — let's lock
  thursday and confirm day-of?"). The frame stays where it was.
- **H7 art of insinuation, residual.** Logistics borrows tone from
  escalation when she's wavering. "Wine bar with the chaos crowd
  or the actually-quiet one — your call, but one of those is wrong."
  Light play, but the shape is logistics.
- **Specific over open.** "Thursday 8 at this cafe" converts
  materially better than "wanna hang sometime this week." Cognitive
  ease (A18 hyperpersonal compression) — short, specific, yes/no.

## What stalls or regresses it (failure modes)

- **Vague "let me know when you're free" (A16 inversion + A3
  collapse).** Puts planning burden entirely on her, offers no
  specific ladder rung, and inverts the autonomy frame from co-
  authoring to her doing all the work. Sometime is when nothing
  happens.
- **Volleying scheduling indefinitely (A14 otherness collapse + A7
  scarcity collapse).** Three+ rounds of "what about tuesday?
  actually wednesday? hm friday?" erodes scarcity and reads as
  endless availability. Every additional round drops the meet
  probability. Converge in two messages or accept this isn't going
  to lock.
- **Permission-framed ask ("would you maybe want to...?") (A16
  violation).** Frames consent as binary grant/deny, her in
  evaluator-frame, him in supplicant-frame. Most common at
  logistics because the asker thinks softening = polite. Mechanism
  inversion.
- **Pressure ("has to be thursday or never") (A16 violation + A17
  no-safety violation).** Forced-choice asks. Reads as anxious-
  coercive. The meet rate is higher when she can decline without
  social cost.
- **Over-detailing the plan before she's confirmed (A2 inversion +
  A18 length penalty).** Sending a paragraph about which cafe and
  why and how to park collapses the reciprocity gradient and
  signals she's the centre of his planning. Lock first, detail later.
- **Adding new requests ("can we also do dinner after") (A3
  ladder violation + scope creep).** Lock the meet, don't expand
  it. Each added ask is an unagreed rung on the ladder; the brain
  refuses moves that don't match prior consistency.
- **Sociometer leak via re-confirmation ("just want to make sure
  you're still in!") (A10 leak).** Asking for the agreement to be
  re-stated drops her read on his gauge. The agreement was the
  agreement; treat it as fact.
- **Folding under scheduling delay (I7 frame collapse).** She
  takes 14h to reply to "thursday or friday?", he sends "no
  pressure either way!! :)" — handed her the frame. Hold: wait,
  or send one labeled close, but don't apologise for the ask.
- **Manufactured-scarcity theatre (A7 inversion).** "I'm super
  booked, only have a 30-minute window thursday at 7:15." Reads
  as fake-busy theatre and pattern-matches as game-playing.
  Real scarcity quietly named, never headlined.

## Strategies in this phase

- **`logistics-two-option-close`** — *A16 autonomy + A3 convergence*.
  "Thursday or friday?" Default when day isn't locked. Forces
  convergence on the time axis in one turn while preserving co-
  authoring frame.
- **`logistics-specific-place`** — *A5 authority by leak + A16
  competence-supportive*. Propose one place + one time. Use when
  she said "you pick" or she's a planner. Demonstrates taste
  without claiming it.
- **`logistics-day-of-confirm`** — *A10 sociometer-positive + A7
  real scarcity*. Short matter-of-fact day-of message, 4-8h
  before. The only proactive message between lock and arrival.
  Crosses into the confirm phase.

## Goal-type weighting

- **`quick-meet-window`** — logistics is short and decisive. Direct
  ask with specific time and place; no soft-time-anchor at this
  point. **A7 real scarcity** is loud (you're literally leaving in
  three days). Two messages to lock; if it doesn't lock in two,
  it isn't going to.
- **`flexible-short-window`** — default cadence. Two-option close,
  then specifics. 2-3 messages to lock.
- **`extended-window`** — logistics still has to be specific even
  when timeline is loose. The meet has to actually happen or the
  thread metabolises. The ask anchors on a real plan-on-the-horizon
  ("when I'm in your city in three weeks, blocking thursday").
- **`reconnect-later`** — logistics doesn't activate until escalation
  has been rebuilt post-reopener. Don't push specifics on a
  reconnected thread until rapport+escalation are re-established.
- **`decline-gracefully`** / **`platonic-curiosity`** — phase doesn't
  apply. There's no meet to logistic.

## Phase exit / next-phase prerequisite

→ **`confirm`** activates when **logistics ≥ 60**: specific time AND
specific place are agreed and acknowledged by both parties. The
ladder (A3) has reached the meet-rung; commitment-consistency is
locked. From here, the phase shifts to maintenance — *don't drop the
ball between agreement and arrival* — and the active toolkit shrinks
to one move: the day-of confirm.

→ **`recovery`** if scheduling stalled or she went quiet mid-
logistics. Most common cause: the underlying escalation wasn't
actually 60 and the ask was premature. Don't push harder; reset to
recovery and re-establish before re-asking.

→ **`dead`** if she pulled out without offering a reschedule. The
absence of a reschedule offer is the signal — *with* a reschedule
("thursday's not gonna work, what about next tuesday?"), the phase
holds; *without* one, it's a soft decline. Per **decline-gracefully**:
"all good — take care." No re-pitching.
