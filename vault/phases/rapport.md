# Phase: rapport

## Psychological objective

Rapport is where the **reciprocity gradient** (A2) and **self-perception
ladder** (A11) start firing. The opener installed a frame; rapport's
job is to convert frame into investment. Each turn should elicit a
slightly larger micro-action from her than the last — a follow-up
question, a callback to an earlier detail, an unprompted share — so
that her own behaviour becomes the evidence (to herself) that she
must be interested. Bem's mechanism does the work the words can't.

The dominant subconscious dynamic is **liking via selective
expression** (A6) plus *just enough* **misattribution-flavoured
tension** (A12) to keep the thread from flatlining into pleasant
agreement. Pure pleasantness habituates fast and produces zero pull;
playful pushback, mock-disagreement, and light reactance moves
(A9) are what generate the arousal her cortex labels as *attraction
to this guy* rather than *nice text exchange*. Greene's principle of
**mixing pleasure with pain** (H4) is the operational shape of this
phase — warmth + tease, agreement + edge, never one without the
other.

The substrate constraint: **otherness** (A14) must not collapse. Rapport
that closes the desire-gap by trying to be a friend kills the thread
in 48 hours. The goal is not "she likes me as a person"; it's "she's
curious about me as a separate gravitational center." Lead from your
own life. Bio remains calibration, never subject (A16 autonomy
violation if it becomes subject). Channel-jumps (voice note) compress
multiple text-rungs of the **commitment ladder** (A3) into one move
and are the most underused rapport tool.

**Objective.** Convert frame ceiling into elicited investment. Build
texture through the alternation of warmth and light push (H4, I2).
Preserve otherness — be the protagonist of your own story, not a
chorus to hers. Default registers: **F4 Natural** for guarded matches,
**F6 Charmer** for verbal/narrative archetypes, **I1 cocky-funny**
across the board.

## Phase-progress rubric (this dimension)

| Score | What it looks like | Psychological reading |
|---|---|---|
| **0-30** | Reciprocal pleasantries. She replies but doesn't ask back. Topics surface-level. | Reciprocity loop one-sided — she's evaluating, not investing. Self-perception (A11) hasn't engaged because she hasn't taken any consistent-with-interest action yet. Liking signal (A6) reading neutral. |
| **30-60** | One specific topic exchanged. She's volunteered something past surface, occasionally asks back. Some texture. | Investment beginning to compound. The Bem mechanism (A11) is engaging — she's inferring her own interest from her own follow-up. Liking selective enough to register (A6). Ladder rung climbed. |
| **60-100** | She volunteers and asks back unprompted. Texture is clear — callbacks, name use, voice note exchange, jokes that depend on prior turns. | Reciprocity gradient is now slightly tilted in her direction (A2 working *for* him). Self-perception (A11) locked in. The thread has its own momentum and its own private register. **F5 Coquette** moves and **A9 reactance** can now operate — there's a freedom (his attention) she's invested in keeping. |

**Hard prerequisite gate:** escalation strategies require rapport
≥ 60. Below 60, the meet ask reads as transactional because there's
no felt familiarity to anchor it. Reactance (A9) and qualifying-style
moves also need rapport substrate — landing a neg or push-pull on
30-rapport reads as arrogance, not flirtation, because her brain has
no warmth-context to misattribute the tension into.

## What advances this dimension (mechanisms)

- **A2 reciprocity gradient.** Per-message investment slightly under
  hers compounds; investment significantly above hers triggers
  obligation alarm. Match her length, then trim 10%. Specific noticing
  invites disclosure at manageable size.
- **A11 self-perception ladder.** Every elicited follow-up, every
  callback she makes, every voice note she sends shifts her felt
  interest upward. The mechanism is internal — she labels her own
  action as "I must be into this" because Bem.
- **A12 misattribution via light tension.** Pleasant agreement
  generates no arousal to label. Tease, mock-disagreement, surprise
  pivots, and pattern interrupts produce arousal her cortex maps onto
  the proximate cause: him. Tension *with embedded warmth* — never
  without.
- **A6 liking via selective expression.** "Ok that line was actually
  funny, you've been upgraded" lands; "you seem cool" doesn't. The
  selectivity is the signal. Express liking earned, not free.
- **I2 push-pull.** One push-pull per turn maximum. "Ok that was
  actually clever, I'm revising my opinion of you upward — don't let
  it go to your head." Pull on the compliment, push on the takeaway.
  Variable-ratio (A13) at the message level.
- **I3 the neg.** Backhanded observation aimed at *chosen categories*
  (her takes, her self-described type, her stated preferences). "The
  spotify-playlist girls always end up being the most chaotic." Signals
  he's not auditioning. Never lands on body, appearance, or innate
  trait — that's injury, and the brain doesn't misattribute pain into
  attraction.
- **I10 storytelling.** Replace facts with narratives. "Second night
  in mexico city, ended up arguing with a tequila distiller about
  whether mezcal was a fad. Lost. Earned a free shot." Beginning,
  tension, resolution, take. Authority leaks (A5), social proof embeds
  in the cast (A4), Zeigarnik fires within the narrative (A15). Voice
  notes amplify the mechanism — narrative is what voice format is *for*.
- **A8 unity / co-conspirator frame.** Light "we" framing where
  plausibly deniable. "We'd be the worst influence on each other."
  Pre-figures relational identity before one exists; self-perception
  (A11) does the rest.
- **H6 enter their spirit.** Adopt her register, vocabulary, cadence
  — then nudge it toward yours. Greene's principle: meet them where
  they are before leading them where you want them to be. Compatible
  with Voss labels/mirrors (A17).
- **I8 the phantom.** Implied-but-never-explicit other-options
  texture. "We're doing thursday at the wine bar" with no specification
  of who "we" is. Continuous low-level social proof (A4), scarcity
  (A7), otherness (A14) without ever being headline.
- **F4 Natural / F6 Charmer registers.** F4 disarms guarded matches
  by refusing to seem to be performing — she relaxes guard because
  nothing is being extracted (A2 in her favour). F6 makes her feel
  like the centre of attention; sociometer-positive because his own
  needs no validation.
- **Channel-jump (voice note).** Single most underused rapport move.
  One voice note compresses several text-rungs of the commitment
  ladder (A3) into one upgrade. Voice carries paralinguistic warmth
  text strips out (A18 inversion in your favour).

## What stalls or regresses it (failure modes)

- **Statement-statement-statement (A15 dead-air).** No hook, no
  future-pace, no thread for her to pull on. Each message must leave
  one open loop or there's nothing for her to do but acknowledge.
- **Excessive agreement (A12 collapse).** Pure pleasantness produces
  zero arousal to misattribute. Yes-and-yes-and-yes feels nice in
  the moment and goes cold within 48 hours because there's no
  tension generating pull. The thread plateaus into "fun chat" and
  dies of comfort.
- **Premature deep disclosure (A14 otherness collapse).** Multi-
  paragraph emotional honesty on day two reads as warmth in the
  moment and closes the desire-gap permanently. The imaginative
  space that generated pull has been filled in. She enjoys the
  message and goes cold within 48 hours.
- **Interview mode (A2 inversion + cognitive load A18).** Stacked
  questions — "how was your weekend? what are you up to this week?
  you been to that place yet?" — read as interrogation, dilute the
  move, and put her in performance-frame (A16 autonomy violation).
  One move per message.
- **Long polished paragraphs (A2 inversion + I6 DLV).** Investment
  significantly above hers triggers obligation alarm. Length should
  match signal. Polish reads as optimisation-for-impressing, which
  inverts the status-via-incidence frame.
- **Bio-quoting on round two (A16 autonomy violation).** "You
  mentioned reading — what's your favorite book?" puts her back in
  performance-frame and signals he's still mining the profile. Bio
  was for opener calibration; rapport runs on what's actually
  happening in the thread.
- **Indiscriminate flattery (A6 inversion + A10 leak).** "You're
  amazing", "you're so cool", "I love your vibe" — generates zero
  reciprocal-liking signal because she correctly infers he'd say it
  to anyone. Lowers her read on his sociometer.
- **Neg landing on innate trait (A9 miscalibration).** Targeting
  body, appearance, or trait she didn't choose crosses from playful
  frame-establishment into actual injury. The brain doesn't
  misattribute pain into attraction — pain just registers as a
  reason to leave.
- **Folding under shit-test (I7 frame loss).** "You say that to all
  the girls?" / "are you always this confident?" — defending or
  explaining enters her frame. "Depends on the day" plus return-to-
  topic holds the frame; "haha no, just thought…" loses it.
- **Ghost-protest after slow reply (recovery-coded leak).** She slow-
  replied; he goes cold-and-punishing. Reads as avoidant-coercive.
  Meet her at the temperature she's at — no scorekeeping.
- **Pattern-matched intermittency (A13 inversion).** Calculated
  delays read as game-playing and collapse trust. Natural variance
  from a real life is the only sustainable variable schedule.

## Strategies in this phase

- **`rapport-mirror-then-pivot`** — *H6 enter their spirit + A6
  liking via similarity*. Match her register, then introduce one
  adjacent angle. Best when conversation is warm but circling.
- **`rapport-share-then-volley`** — *A2 reciprocity gradient + A11
  self-perception*. Share one concrete thing, then ask. Best when
  thread has gone Q&A-shaped and needs reciprocity rebalanced.
- **`rapport-callback`** — *A11 self-perception + A15 closing one
  loop while opening another*. Reference an earlier specific detail
  and push the thread forward. Top move for proving attention without
  performing it.
- **`rapport-playful-tease`** — *A12 misattribution + I3 neg + H4
  pleasure-with-pain*. Tease *chosen categories* — her takes, her
  vibe, her hot opinions. Never body, never innate trait. Raises
  temperature without grinding.
- **`rapport-frame-control`** — *I7 frame control + I4 AMOG handling*.
  She tested or curveballed; brief acknowledge + redirect to your
  move. Don't fold, don't fight, don't enter the frame she invited.
- **`rapport-social-proof`** — *A4 incidental social proof + I8 the
  phantom*. Drop a real, offhand reference to your life as context.
  Never headline — the moment it's pointed at, it inverts.
- **`rapport-disqualifier`** — *A9 reactance + A16 autonomy support*.
  Flip the audition: "you'd have to convince me you're not just
  another planner-type." Use when rapport is warm and you want to
  raise temperature ahead of escalation.

## Goal-type weighting

- **`quick-meet-window`** — keep rapport short. Target rapport ~50-60,
  not 80. Compress with **I9 time distortion** (one high-density 20-
  minute exchange does the work of three days) and channel-jump to
  voice early. Move to escalation as soon as she reciprocates with
  warmth — don't over-rapport.
- **`flexible-short-window`** — default. Build real rapport (60-80)
  before pushing. Let it breathe. Escalate on her cue, not arbitrarily.
- **`extended-window`** — natural home; rapport runs high (80+) for
  many turns. **A13 variable-ratio** does outsized work without the
  meet-as-natural-close. Channel-jumps (voice note, call) compound.
  **F5 Coquette** and **F8 Star** registers fit. Open loops (A15)
  carry tension across days.
- **`reconnect-later`** — rapport reset to low after a successful
  reopener. Don't try to resume mid-thread; build fresh.
- **`decline-gracefully`** / **`just-being-polite`** — drop pursuit
  moves. Reply rate matches hers, no escalation, no temperature
  raises. The phase isn't operating.

## Phase exit / next-phase prerequisite

→ **`qualifying`** activates when **rapport ≥ 60**: she's volunteered
something unprompted, the thread has texture, mutual reciprocation is
evident. There's enough warmth substrate for **A9 reactance** moves
(neg, mock-disqualification, push-pull) to fire without injuring,
because there's now a freedom (his attention, the thread's existence)
she's mildly invested in keeping.

→ **`escalation`** (skipping qualifying) is allowed when rapport is
loud (≥ 70), goal_type is `quick-meet-window`, and qualifying would
just delay an obvious meet. The commitment ladder (A3) can compress
when frame and reciprocity are already strong.

→ **`recovery`** if she goes silent 48-96h on a substantive turn.
Off-axis transition; rapport score doesn't reset, but the dominant
phase changes.

If rapport stalls at 30-60 across 3-4 turns: the substrate is one-
sided. Either escalate temperature (push-pull, neg, voice note) to
force the misattribution mechanism (A12) into firing, or accept that
her engagement ceiling is capped and the thread is cooling regardless.
Three IODs (I5) in a turn: phase has regressed; treat as recovery.
