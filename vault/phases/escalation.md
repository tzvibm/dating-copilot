# Phase: escalation

## Psychological objective

Escalation is the phase where **misattribution of arousal** (A12),
the **commitment ladder** (A3), and **otherness-as-desire** (A14)
all peak simultaneously. The thread has accumulated reciprocity and
fit-signal; the mechanism that converts those into a meet is not
"asking nicely" — it's tension that her cortex maps as *I want this
to happen in person*. Pleasant rapport plateaued at 70 stays at 70
forever; the meet only materialises when something pushes the
emotional charge past where text can hold it.

Greene's **deepening effect** and **moving in for the kill** both
live here. The deepening is mid-escalation: stir up the
transgressive (H3), implicate intimacy through insinuation (H7),
warm-then-withdraw (F5 Coquette), keep her wanting (H5). The kill is
late-escalation: drop the indirection. Greene's principle inverts at
the close — *clarity becomes the seduction*. The man who's been
playful, slightly absent, hard-to-pin-down for ten turns and then
drops a specific clear ask reads as decisive precisely because the
prior register set the contrast. **F1 Rake** (hungry, irreverent,
not-safe-and-not-sorry) and **F7 Charismatic** (vision pulling her
in) are the registers that make this contrast land.

The dominant text-only constraint: **time-bridging / future-pacing**
(A15 + H7) does most of the work. By the time the formal ask appears,
the meet should already be psychologically pre-figured — "when we
do this in person", "I'll show you the actually-good cafe", "we're
getting drinks at hideout thursday." The reader does the inferential
work that *the meet is happening*, and because the inference is hers
(self-perception A11), it sticks. Once future-paced, the explicit
ask becomes logistics, not negotiation.

The **commitment ladder** (A3) constraint: each turn requests a
slightly larger micro-investment than the last. Voice note → callback
in her own voice note → phone call → meet. Skipping rungs (asking
for the meet from cold rapport) violates the gradient and the brain
refuses moves that don't match prior consistency. The ladder is also
where channel-jumps compound — voice and phone are several text-rungs
in one move.

**Objective.** Convert accumulated mutual investment into a specific
near-future meet. Push temperature ~10% above her last message every
turn until the ask lands as continuation. **F1 Rake** for warm
flirty matches, **F7 Charismatic** for ambition-coded ones, **F9
Sensualist** for fully escalated late-thread energy. The ask itself,
when it lands, is **direct and specific** — Greene's clarity-becomes-
seduction principle, not coded indirection.

## Phase-progress rubric (this dimension)

| Score | What it looks like | Psychological reading |
|---|---|---|
| **0-30** | No meet signal. Conversation stays in the abstract. No future-pacing, no playful pre-figuration. | Misattribution (A12) hasn't crossed threshold. **A14 otherness** intact but no proximity-pressure. The ladder (A3) is stuck at the rung where it sat. Pleasant rapport, no pull toward the close. |
| **30-60** | She hinted at meeting — picked up on a future-pace, asked what you're up to this week, made a "we should" gesture. | A12 firing. Self-perception (A11) registering — she's now stating, however lightly, the meet-trajectory. Time-bridging (H7) compounding. **F5 Coquette** moves are landing. |
| **60-100** | Explicit "we should" or near-yes. She's named a day, picked up the bridge with detail, or said "yes" before the formal ask. Channel may have jumped (voice note received). | A3 ladder in late-stage. **A14 otherness** held; **A12 misattribution** at peak. The ask, when made, is logistics. The work has been done; the close is closing what's already psychologically agreed. |

**Hard prerequisite gate:** logistics requires escalation ≥ 60.
Below 60, "thursday at 8?" lands as transactional because the meet
hasn't been pre-figured — she's being asked to convert *text* into
*meeting* in one move, which the commitment ladder (A3) refuses.
The fix when escalation is stuck is more bridging and more
temperature, not a harder ask.

## What advances this dimension (mechanisms)

- **A15 future-pacing / H7 art of insinuation.** "When you show me the
  actually-good cafe in this city" / "we're getting drinks at that
  place when I'm back from bogotá." Drop offhand references to
  future hangs *before* any explicit ask. The reader does the
  inferring (A11 self-perception), and the inference is hers, so it
  sticks. Most leveraged escalation move that exists.
- **A3 commitment ladder.** Each turn slightly larger micro-investment.
  Substantive reply → asks a follow-up → uses your name → voice note
  → phone call → meet. Channel-jumps compress several text-rungs in
  one move.
- **A12 misattribution via temperature raise.** Calibrated escalation
  — match her temperature on the last message, then nudge ~10%.
  Polite mirror → playful. Playful → flirty. Flirty → invitation.
  Don't skip rungs (creep) and don't stay flat (death by comfort).
- **A7 scarcity (real options).** "I'm in town through saturday."
  "Flying to tokyo thursday." Real time-bounded windows. Manufactured
  scarcity (engineered delays, fake-busy theatre) inverts the moment
  pattern-matched. Real scarcity built on a full life isn't.
- **H3 stir up the transgressive.** Mild taboo, slightly off-limits
  implication. **A12** via transgression — arousal her cortex labels
  as *I want this guy*. Implication of transgression, not the
  transgression itself.
- **H5 create temptation, leave them wanting.** Voice note that ends
  mid-thought. Story whose ending she has to ask for. Show enough to
  be tantalising, never enough to satisfy. **A15 Zeigarnik** in
  active deployment.
- **F1 Rake register.** Hungry, irreverent, makes her feel she
  awakens something in him. Surgical, surprising compliments.
  Pleasure-coded, slightly transgressive. Default for warm flirty
  threads with reciprocating temperature.
- **F5 Coquette / variable-ratio (A13).** Hot-cold, attainable-then-
  distant. Pull back exactly when she leans in. Powerful at the
  deepening stage; stops the moment it tips into anxious-attachment-
  induction.
- **F7 Charismatic.** Vision-pulling. *I'm going somewhere; come with
  me.* Authority leaks (A5), pre-suasive frame (A1), mate-preference
  cues for status/agency (A19). Powerful for ambition-coded matches.
- **I11 statement of intent.** "You're cute. Let's get a drink this
  week." Bypasses indirect rapport on the bet that frame confidence
  substitutes for warmth investment. **High-risk, high-reward**;
  works for `quick-meet-window` when timeline doesn't permit gradual
  escalation. Fails without genuine warmth substrate.
- **Channel-jump (voice note → phone).** Each upgrade is a ladder rung
  worth several text-rungs. Voice carries paralinguistic warmth text
  strips out (A18 inverted in his favour). Single most underused
  escalation move.
- **Specific invitation > open invitation.** "Thursday 8 at this
  cafe?" converts orders of magnitude better than "wanna hang
  sometime." When you ask, ask with one specific time, one specific
  place. Yes/no is easier cognitively than negotiating (A18
  hyperpersonal compression — short asks land better than long ones).
- **A8 unity / co-conspirator frame.** "Alright we're doing this."
  "This is going to be a problem." Pre-figures relational identity;
  self-perception (A11) does the rest.

## What stalls or regresses it (failure modes)

- **Sudden temperature jump (A12 inversion → creep).** Going from
  polite-friendly to "you're so hot, when can I see you" is the
  textbook creep failure. The brain's pattern-match is *this jumped
  rungs in the ladder*, which signals predator, not pull. The fix is
  always +10% from where she just was, never +50%.
- **Flat plateau in safe rapport (A12 collapse → death by comfort).**
  Pleasant agreement that never raises temperature — the desire-gap
  closes by attrition. Past about 12 turns of warmth-without-
  escalation, the thread metabolises into pen-pal-zone. The meet
  becomes psychologically harder, not easier, the longer this drags.
- **Vague ask ("we should hang sometime") (A3 + A16 failure).**
  Puts the planning burden entirely on her and offers no specific
  ladder rung. Sometime is when nothing happens. Either propose a
  specific day/place, or hold off until you can.
- **Permission-framed ask ("can I take you out?") (A16 violation).**
  Frames consent as binary grant/deny — her into evaluator-frame,
  him into supplicant-frame. Same intent, mechanism inverted. "Drinks
  thursday — wine bar on smith or the noisy one, your call" frames
  her as co-author.
- **Subliminal ask ("feels criminal not to share these spots")
  (A18 + clarity collapse).** Coded indirection at escalation reads
  as scared, not clever. Greene's principle inverts at the close —
  clarity *is* the seduction. Direct invite > poetic insinuation
  about wanting an invite.
- **Endless availability ("whenever works", "I'm free all week") (A7
  collapse).** Manufactured unavailability is brittle, but real
  scarcity is the most leveraged signal in escalation. Naming a real
  window ("around tuesday-thursday this week") beats both extremes.
- **Pressure ("has to be thursday or never") (A16 violation + A17
  no-as-safety violation).** Forced-choice asks read as anxious-
  coercive. The meet rate is higher when she can say no without
  consequence — paradoxically, "totally fine if thursday's a no"
  beats "you should come."
- **Sociometer leak ("did I read this wrong?", "still on for...?")
  (A10 leak).** Anything whose function is to harvest reassurance
  drops her read on his gauge. One anxious-coded message undoes
  three good ones; the asymmetry is steep.
- **I11 statement-of-intent without warmth substrate.** Direct game
  from a man who can't back the frame collapses fast. The bet
  requires real frame-confidence; if the rapport hasn't generated
  enough heat, the "let's get a drink" reads as random instead of
  decisive.
- **Stacked-message ask (long pitch + invitation).** Make the ask
  its own short line. Long messages dilute (A18 hyperpersonal
  compression — length should match signal); the ask should be the
  single most legible thing in the message.

## Strategies in this phase

- **`escalation-specific-invite`** — *I11 statement of intent + A3
  ladder rung + clarity*. Yes/no with one time and one place. Top-
  converter when rapport is warm. Reference card for direct meet
  asks.
- **`escalation-soft-time-anchor`** — *A15 future-pacing + A7 real
  scarcity*. "I'm around thursday-ish." Default when `days_remaining
  > 4` and she's not a planner. Lets her co-author the specifics.
- **`escalation-direct-ask`** — *I11 + A7 scarcity compressed*.
  "Drinks thursday at 8?" Use when window is short or she's a
  planner. Frame-confident; high-risk for cold threads.
- **`escalation-activity-anchor`** — *A11 self-perception + A8 unity
  frame*. Propose an activity she already mentioned wanting to do.
  Her stated preference becomes self-evidence she should accept.
- **`escalation-time-bridge`** — *H7 art of insinuation + A15
  future-pacing*. Drop offhand "when we get drinks" before any
  explicit ask. The pre-figuration that turns the eventual ask into
  logistics. Use during late rapport / qualifying.
- **`escalation-phone-bridge`** — *A3 channel-jump rung + A18
  paralinguistic warmth*. Pivot to a 5-minute call before the meet.
  Best when bio explicitly says phone-caller, or when text is
  plateauing despite warm signals.

## Goal-type weighting

- **`quick-meet-window`** — primary phase, compressed. **I11 statement
  of intent** is on the table. **A7 real scarcity** does extra work
  (you're literally in town for a few days — natural urgency, no
  manufactured pressure needed). Push the ask at messages 5-7 of a
  warm thread, not 9-12. **I9 time distortion** via voice note can
  compress a day's worth of text in one upgrade.
- **`flexible-short-window`** — escalate on her cue; ask around
  messages 7-11 in a warm thread. Default cadence. Soft-time-anchor
  > direct-ask unless signals are loud.
- **`extended-window`** — escalate when there's a *reason* (a specific
  upcoming thing, a real plan-anchor on the horizon), not arbitrarily.
  **F5 Coquette** and **F8 Star** registers sustain tension across
  many turns; a forced ask without anchor inverts.
- **`reconnect-later`** — escalation activates only after the
  reopener has rebuilt rapport. Don't carry escalation state across
  the gap.
- **`decline-gracefully`** — phase is structurally wrong; the goal is
  exit, not progression. Skip.

## Phase exit / next-phase prerequisite

→ **`logistics`** activates when **escalation ≥ 60**: she's named a
day, said an explicit "we should", or returned a specific yes to a
soft anchor. The misattribution work is done; the meet is
psychologically agreed and now needs coordination. **A17 Voss
calibrated questions** and **no-as-safety** become the dominant
toolkit; **A16 autonomy** via choice architecture takes over from
**A12 tension**.

→ **`recovery`** if she went silent or cooled noticeably *after* the
ask. The ask was either premature (escalation wasn't actually 60)
or landed wrong (creep, vague, permission-framed). Don't double
down; treat as recovery and reset.

→ **`dead`** on explicit decline. Per **decline-gracefully**: "all
good — appreciated the honesty, take care." Sociometer-positive
disengagement (A10 + A16) keeps the door open if anything later
changes; re-pitching closes it permanently.
