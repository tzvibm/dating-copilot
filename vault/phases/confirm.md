# Phase: confirm

## Psychological objective

Confirm is the phase where **silence is a strategy**. The meet is on
the calendar. Every additional message between lock and arrival is
either neutral or — far more often — corrosive. The dominant
subconscious dynamic is the **sociometer** (A10) reading every
message in this window with extra weight: anything that registers as
*needing reassurance* drops her gauge sharply, and the asymmetry is
brutal at this stage because there's no further rapport runway to
recover.

**Scarcity** (A7) operates on a different axis here. The meet itself
is now scarce relative to the rest of his life, not the other way
around. Daily "can't wait" messages chip at this scarcity by
signalling that *this meet is the centre of his week* — which
inverts the signal that got her to yes. He's amused, busy, has
options; the meet is a piece of his thursday, not its peak.

Greene's principle that **calculated absence makes presence felt**
(H2) operates as the default. The withdrawal between lock and
arrival is the move. She notices the silence — not as gap, but as
texture; the man whose ten turns established frame-confidence
doesn't need to keep auditioning. **A14 otherness** is at its purest
here: don't close the gap; let her wonder slightly what he's up to
between thursday's lock and thursday's drink.

The **hyperpersonal effect** (A18) inverts dangerously in this
window. Strong prior frame → her imagination reads short messages
warmly; weak signal → her imagination fills the gap *cold*. So if
the prior frame is solid, the right move is *fewer* and *shorter*
messages. If the prior frame was fragile, the urge is to send more
to "shore it up" — and that urge is precisely the move that
collapses the frame.

The exception that proves the rule: a small genuine logistics
adjustment (running 10 late, place changed because of a closure)
is fine because it's *operationally* required and reads as
competence (A5 authority leak — *he runs his life, even when adjusting*).
Anything else is sociometer leak.

**Objective.** Hold the frame across the gap between lock and
arrival. **Silence by default.** One short matter-of-fact day-of
confirm 4-8h before. Real-detail-change messages are fine. Anything
emotive (excitement, anticipation, "can't wait", "looking forward
so much") is leak. The meet is happening; the work is to not undo it.

## Phase-progress rubric (this dimension)

| Score | What it looks like | Psychological reading |
|---|---|---|
| **0-30** | Not day-of yet. Meet is locked but >24h out. | Maintenance window. Silence is signal — frame holding because not being defended. **H2 calculated absence** doing its work passively. |
| **30-60** | Day-of approaching, 4-12h before. Window for the day-of confirm. | Active confirmation window. One short message to convert "agreed" into "this is happening today" — commitment-consistency (A3) at the final rung. |
| **60-100** | Day-of confirmed. Both parties have acknowledged the meet today. | Full lock. From here, only operational adjustments. The next state-change is the meet itself. |

**Note.** Confirm has no "next phase" gate the way earlier phases do
— the phase exits to terminal states (the meet happens, or it
doesn't). Score functions less as a gate, more as a where-are-we-in-
the-window indicator.

## What advances this dimension (mechanisms)

- **H2 calculated absence.** The withdrawal between lock and arrival
  is the move. Real, never explained. She notices the texture of
  *no message because his life is full*, not *no message because he
  played it cool*.
- **A10 sociometer-positive baseline.** The implicit posture: he's
  indifferent to confirming. The meet is happening because they
  agreed. No re-confirmation needed.
- **A7 real scarcity, residual.** The meet is one of several things
  on his thursday. This isn't said; it's the texture of how the
  short day-of message reads.
- **A18 short message wins.** The single best confirm message in
  history is one line. *"7 at the wine bar — see you there."* The
  brevity is the signal. Long warm messages collapse hyperpersonal
  in the wrong direction.
- **A17 Voss-style label for friction.** If she surfaces hesitation
  ("I might be running late"), one label closes it: *"sounds like
  the day's been a lot — no rush, I'll grab a seat."* Defuses
  without entering negotiation.
- **A5 authority by leak on operational adjustments.** "Place
  closed today, moving us to <X>, same time." Clean, no apology,
  no "is that okay??". Reads as competence — he runs his life,
  even when adjusting.
- **I7 frame control.** Especially if she sends an ambiguous mid-
  day text. Brief acknowledge + return to plan. Don't over-engage
  pre-meet small talk; the meet is where that happens now.

## What stalls or regresses it (failure modes)

- **Daily "can't wait" messages (A10 leak + A7 inversion).** Chips
  at anticipation, signals the meet is the centre of his week,
  inverts the scarcity that got her to yes. Most common confirm
  failure mode.
- **Re-confirmation ("we still on?", "just making sure thursday
  works") (A10 leak + A3 violation).** Asks the agreement to be
  re-granted, treats the lock as not-locked. Drops her gauge
  sharply. The agreement was the agreement.
- **Apologetic check-in ("hey, hope I'm not bothering you, just
  wanted to confirm...") (A10 leak + A16 inversion).** Stacks
  multiple sociometer leaks: hedging, asking permission to text,
  asking for re-confirmation. Worst-of-all-worlds in this window.
- **Adding new requests pre-meet ("can we also do dinner after?")
  (A3 ladder violation).** Lock the meet, don't expand it.
  Unagreed rungs on the ladder; the brain refuses moves that
  don't match prior consistency. Save expansion for in-person.
- **Long pre-meet warming ("so excited, you're going to love this
  place, btw I was thinking...") (A2 inversion + A14 otherness
  collapse).** Closes the desire-gap before proximity. The
  imaginative space that the meet was about to fill gets pre-
  filled by text, and the in-person experience reads as flat.
- **Ghosting day-of (A10 leak via absence).** Even a one-liner
  "running 10 late" is required. Showing up unannounced after
  silence reads as low-conscientiousness, drops her gauge during
  the wait, and starts the meet in a hole.
- **Over-confirming details ("you remember it's at the wine bar,
  right? thursday at 8?") (A10 leak via redundancy).** Treats
  her as forgetful. Read as anxious management.
- **Ghost-protest if she's slow on a day-of reply (recovery-coded
  leak).** She replies 90 minutes later than usual to the day-of
  confirm; he goes cold, sends a curt "ok" or doesn't respond.
  Reads as avoidant-coercive. Meet her at the temperature she's
  at; treat the meet as on until told otherwise.

## Strategies in this phase

- **`logistics-day-of-confirm`** — *A10 sociometer-positive + A7
  real scarcity + A18 short-message-wins*. Short matter-of-fact
  day-of message, 4-8h before. The only proactive message in this
  phase. Format: time + place + see-you-there, one line.

## Goal-type weighting

- All meeting-oriented goals share this phase identically. Confirm
  is uniform across `quick-meet-window`, `flexible-short-window`,
  and `extended-window` because the dynamics (sociometer, scarcity,
  calculated absence) are not timeline-dependent. The day-of message
  is the same shape regardless of how long the prior thread ran.
- **`decline-gracefully`** / **`platonic-curiosity`** — phase
  doesn't activate.

## Phase exit / next-phase prerequisite

→ **`meet_set`** (terminal) — the meet day arrives and the meet
happens. Label after with `meet_happened`. The conversational arc
the vault tracks completes here; whatever happens at the meet is
out-of-scope for the copilot's text-only domain.

→ **`recovery`** if she rescheduled or went quiet on confirmation.
Reschedule with a real new day offered = soft positive (re-enter
logistics on the new day). Reschedule without a new day, or
silence on the day-of confirm = recovery; the meet has cooled
between lock and arrival, and the right move is *not* to push.

→ **`dead`** if she pulled out without rescheduling, or no-showed.
Per **decline-gracefully**: clean acknowledgement, no re-pitching.
A no-show specifically: one short message ("got it, hope you're
okay") closes the loop; further pursuit reads as anxious. The bridge
stays minimally open in case the no-show was real-life chaos, not
intent — but no follow-up.
