# Phase: confirm

A specific time AND place are agreed. The meet is on the calendar.
Goal: don't drop the ball between agreement and arrival.

## You're in this phase when

- A specific day, time, and place are confirmed.
- The meet is in the future — usually 0-72h out.

## What works

- Silence by default. The plan is set; don't keep selling it.
- One short day-of confirmation, 4-8h before.
- A small surface-area message if a real detail changes (ETA, place
  swap because of a closure).

## What doesn't

- Daily "can't wait" messages — chips at the anticipation, smells
  needy.
- Adding new requests ("can we also do X after"). Lock the meet,
  don't expand it.
- Ghosting on the day-of. Even a one-liner is required.

## Strategies in this phase

- `logistics-day-of-confirm` — short matter-of-fact message, 4-8h
  before. The only proactive message in this phase.

## Goal-type interactions

- All meeting goals share this phase.
- The phase is uniform across goals — confirm = confirm.

## Phase exit

→ `meet_set` (terminal-ish) — the day arrives. Label after.
→ `recovery` — she rescheduled or went quiet on confirmation.
→ `dead` — she pulled out without rescheduling.
