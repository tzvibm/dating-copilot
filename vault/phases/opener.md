# Phase: opener

You have a match and zero conversation history. Goal: get a real
reply that opens up an actual exchange.

## You're in this phase when

- New match, no messages exchanged.
- Or: stalled match where neither side has said anything substantive.

## What works

- **Lead with your own energy** — a take, a plan, a frame, a move.
  The message says something about *you*, not about *her profile*.
- **Use her bio to calibrate, not as subject matter.** Her vibe
  shapes which take you pick (a planner gets a logistics tease, an
  artsy type gets a culture take), but the message itself isn't a
  callback to her profile.
- **One clear move per message.** A specific take, a specific plan,
  a specific invite — not a question buffet.
- **Implied confidence over performed interest.** "Trying to figure
  out if friday night needs a plan" beats "what do you do for fun".

## What doesn't

- **Don't quote her bio back at her.** "I see you have a bird, what's
  its name?" / "you mentioned reading — last great book?" That's the
  reactive trap. She knows what's in her bio.
- "Hey", "How's your day", "How are you", "What do you do" — bland.
- Compliments on appearance — burns the opener as a gambit.
- LLM-generic openers ("That's a great prompt!", "Love that energy").
- Stacking 2 questions in one opener — interrogation.
- Trying-too-hard wit. If the line takes a paragraph to land, scrap it.

## Strategies in this phase

- `opener-flirty-edge` — flirty, slightly edgy, sets the room with
  temperature. One sentence with confidence. Use when voice notes
  allow flirty register (most defaults).
- `opener-lead-with-take` — open with a confident take or frame from
  your world; her bio sets the topic register, not the subject.
- `opener-callback-to-photo` — riff on a non-obvious detail in a
  photo. Use sparingly; only when the photo has a *unique* hook AND
  the user voice notes haven't explicitly opted out of profile-
  reactive openers.
- `opener-prompt-flip` — flip her bio/prompt back at her with a take,
  not a question. Risky — easy to read as "I read your profile."
- `opener-low-stakes-question` — last resort when there's nothing
  unique to grab. Make sure the question implies you have a take.

## Goal-type interactions

- `quick-meet-window` — opener can hint tempo ("trying to figure out
  if friday needs a plan") without making it the headline.
- `flexible-short-window` — open like you have time but a point of view.
- `extended-window` — same; no rush.
- `decline-gracefully` / `just-being-polite` — don't open at all.

## Phase exit

→ `rapport` — she replied with substance (not emoji or one-word ack).
