# Phase: opener

You have a match and zero conversation history. Goal: get a real
reply that opens up an actual exchange.

## You're in this phase when

- New match, no messages exchanged.
- Or: stalled match where neither side has said anything substantive.

## What works

- Specific reference to her profile (a photo, a prompt) that shows
  you read it and have a take on it.
- Light teasing only when it's clearly playful.
- Asking a question that isn't googleable.

## What doesn't

- "Hey", "How's your day", "How are you".
- Compliments on appearance — burns the gambit.
- LLM-generic openers ("That's a great prompt!").
- Stacking 2 questions in one opener — interrogation.

## Strategies in this phase

- `opener-callback-to-photo` — riff on a non-obvious detail in a
  photo. Best when photos have texture.
- `opener-prompt-flip` — flip her bio/prompt back at her with a take,
  not a question. Best when prompts have a position to flip.
- `opener-low-stakes-question` — one specific, low-effort question
  with implied take. Fallback when there's nothing to callback or
  flip.

## Goal-type interactions

- `quick-meet-window` — opener can hint at being on a clock without
  making it the headline.
- `flexible-short-window` — open like you have time. Read intent later.
- `extended-window` — same; no rush.
- `decline-gracefully` — don't open at all. Unmatch or leave it.
- `just-being-polite` — same; if you're not pursuing, opening
  contradicts the goal.

## Phase exit

→ `rapport` — she replied with substance (not emoji or one-word ack).
