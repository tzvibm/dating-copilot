# Phase: opener

## Psychological objective

The opener does **pre-suasion** work (A1). The first message installs
the interpretive frame that anchors how every subsequent message is
read. Her brain decides in seconds what category this thread belongs
to — interview, supplicant, peer-banter, intriguing-stranger,
sexual-undercurrent — and that category sets a *ceiling* on the
warmth, wit, and intimacy the rest of the thread can reach.

Because text strips paralinguistic cues, the **hyperpersonal effect**
(A18) compounds the frame: ambiguous later messages read warm if the
opener was strong, cold if it was weak. Frame quality compounds
asymmetrically — every downstream mechanism (reactance, reciprocity,
escalation) operates from the ceiling the opener set.

The opener is also where the **sociometer** (A10) first reads.
Anything signaling he's seeking confirmation from her — long
messages, multiple questions, generic compliments — registers as
low-mate-value evidence before any rapport exists to compensate.

**Objective.** Install a frame that's neither interrogating nor
supplicating. Signal competence/humour through what he *does*, not
what he claims (A5). Leave one open loop (A15) for her to pull on.
Cocky-funny register (I1) is the default unless `voice.md` overrides.

## Phase-progress rubric (this dimension)

| Score | What it looks like | Psychological reading |
|---|---|---|
| **0-30** | No reply, or one-word ack ("hey", "haha") | Frame too low or too high, sociometer leak detected, or no hook offered. Imagination filling negative direction (A18). |
| **30-60** | Substantive single response, no question back | Frame held but didn't compound — she's evaluating, not invested yet. Reciprocity loop one-sided. |
| **60-100** | Substantive reply, asks back, volunteers detail, matches/raises energy | Frame stuck. Ceiling lifted. Hyperpersonal effect (A18) now compounding *for* him. Reciprocity loop two-sided. Self-perception ladder (A11) primed. |

**Hard prerequisite gate:** rapport strategies require opener ≥ 60.
Until the opener compounds, there is no substrate for the rapport
mechanisms (A2 reciprocity gradient, A12 tension, A11 ladder) to
operate on. Re-fire opener moves until the frame catches.

## What advances this dimension (mechanisms)

- **A1 pre-suasive frame.** Choose openers that anchor the frame you
  want to live in. Playful-judgment-frame ("you're either a planner
  or chaos") caps high; interview-frame ("what do you do for fun")
  caps low. Pick the ceiling deliberately.
- **A14 otherness installation.** Lead with your own energy — a
  take, a plan, a small piece of your day. Bio is *calibration* (it
  shapes the register), not *subject* (it never becomes the topic).
  This preserves the desire-gap from message one.
- **A2 reciprocity gradient via specific noticing.** Pick *one* non-
  obvious detail from a photo or her bio's stated preference, and
  hand her a 5-second-answerable hook. This is reciprocity at
  manageable size — not a paragraph of "I read your profile."
- **A10 sociometer-positive signal.** "Trying to figure out if friday
  needs a plan" carries a life that's running. "What do you do for
  fun?" asks her to fill his message with content. The first signals
  high MV without claiming it; the second signals the inverse.
- **I1 cocky-funny.** Default register. Combines A5 authority leak,
  A9 light reactance, and humour-as-competence-signal (A19) in one
  move. Self-aware enough that the arrogance reads as play.
- **A15 one open loop.** Most openers should leave exactly one thread
  for her to pull on — not zero (dead-air), not three (interrogation).
- **H1 mixed signals (Greene).** Warmth + slight distance, attention
  + mild absence. The contradiction is the hook her mind tries to
  resolve.

## What stalls or regresses it (failure modes)

- **Bio-quoting → A16 autonomy violation + A2 inversion.** "I see
  you have a bird, what's its name?" puts her in performance-frame
  ("did he correctly process my profile?"), and signals he made her
  profile the subject. Bio calibrates register; bio never becomes
  topic.
- **"Hey" / "How's your day" / "What do you do" → A1 frame
  collapse.** Anchors the thread as small-talk interview. Pre-
  suasive ceiling set low; nothing later compensates.
- **Long opener → A2 reciprocity inversion.** Investment significantly
  above hers triggers obligation alarm. Multi-paragraph openers
  collapse the gradient before any exchange has happened.
- **Compliments on appearance → A6 inversion + A10 leak.** She's
  been told this hundreds of times — zero-information, registers as
  supplication.
- **LLM-generic warmth ("Love that energy", "Such a cool prompt")
  → A10 sociometer leak.** Generic enthusiasm reads as low
  discrimination, which downstream-encodes as low mate-value.
- **Stacked questions → cognitive load (A18).** Two+ questions in
  one message reads as interrogation and dilutes the move. Pick one.
- **Trying-too-hard wit → authenticity collapse + DLV (I6).** A line
  that takes a paragraph to land signals optimisation-for-impressing,
  which inverts the status-via-incidence frame. Cleverness that
  needs a runway is a leak.

## Strategies in this phase

- **`opener-lead-with-take`** — *A14 otherness installation + I1
  cocky-funny*. Open with a confident take from your world; bio is
  calibration only. Use when you want to skip profile-anchoring
  entirely.
- **`opener-callback-to-photo`** — *A2 reciprocity via specific
  noticing*. One concrete detail in a photo, 5-second-answerable.
  Top-converter when a unique detail exists.
- **`opener-flirty-edge`** — *A12 misattribution priming + H3 light
  transgressive*. Slightly edgy, sets temperature. Best when voice
  permits flirty register and there's no strong photo hook.
- **`opener-prompt-flip`** — *A1 frame inversion + A9 light
  reactance*. Flip her prompt as a take, not a question. Risky on
  generic prompts.
- **`opener-low-stakes-question`** — *cognitive ease fallback*. Use
  when nothing unique grabs; question must imply you have a take.
- **`opener-scarcity-direct-ask`** — *I11 statement of intent + A7
  scarcity*. Direct invite from cold, anchored on real time-bounded
  scarcity. Quick-meet-window only; high-risk, high-reward.

## Goal-type weighting

- **`quick-meet-window`** — opener does extra work; less recovery
  time. Tempo can leak into the frame ("trying to figure out if
  friday needs a plan") but timeline shouldn't headline. I11
  statement-of-intent is on the table when scarcity is real.
- **`flexible-short-window`** — open like you have time but a point
  of view. Default register.
- **`extended-window`** — same; no rush. F8 Star register can apply
  if ambient mystery suits her archetype. A15 open loops do more
  work than scarcity here.
- **`reconnect-later`** — A1 pre-suasive reset. The reopener is a
  fresh first impression; do not reference the old thread's
  stuckness. Anchor on present-tense observation.
- **`decline-gracefully`** / **`just-being-polite`** — don't open at
  all. There's no frame work to do.

## Phase exit / next-phase prerequisite

→ **`rapport`** activates when **opener ≥ 60**: she replied with
substance, ideally asks back, matches or raises energy. The frame
stuck. Now there's substrate for A2 reciprocity gradient, A12 light
tension, and A11 self-perception ladder to operate on.

If opener stalls at 30-60 across 2-3 turns: the frame held but
didn't compound. Either escalate temperature (move to flirty-edge,
add I2 push-pull element) or accept that her engagement ceiling on
this thread is capped and goal_type may need re-evaluation.
