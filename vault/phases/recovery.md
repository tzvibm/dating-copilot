# Phase: recovery

## Psychological objective

Recovery is off-axis. It isn't a rung on the linear progression chain
— it's the phase the thread enters when the **attachment system**
(Bowlby; Hazan & Shaver) has been activated in either direction:
silence after a substantive turn (her avoidant withdrawal, or his
move that misfired), a noticeably colder reply, a misread he can
name, a meet that deflated. The dominant dynamics are the hardest in
the catalogue because the failure modes aren't *less rapport* — they
are **anxious-attachment-coded protest behaviour**, which is
mechanism-inverted relative to everything that worked through opener
through escalation.

The dominant subconscious dynamic is **secure-base posture**: he's
fine. The thread cooled; he's not flinching. **A10 sociometer** reads
this signature with absolute clarity — a man who can be ignored
without protest is a high-MV signal; a man who triple-texts after a
silence is signalling that *his* gauge is reading low, which is
downstream evidence to *her* gauge that his actual mate-value is
low. The asymmetry is brutal: one anxious-coded message can erase
the entire prior thread's frame-work, because the attachment-system
read overwrites the prior cognitive read.

**Greene's calculated absence** (H2) is the operational shape of
recovery, but with a critical inversion. Through escalation, H2 was
*his* withdrawal generating *her* presence-felt-as-weight. In
recovery, the withdrawal is *hers* — and the move is to mirror it.
The man who matches her absence with his own absence holds the
frame; the man who fills it with messages collapses it. **A9
reactance** does the work: she has the freedom (his attention) and
has paused exercising it; reasserting the freedom requires that the
freedom feel un-pressured, which means his side has to be calm
enough that she can return without it costing her anything.

The **A14 otherness** mechanism, which has been operative throughout,
peaks in importance here. The man who has a full life is the man
who can be slow-replied without going into protest mode, because
the silence isn't existential — there are other things happening.
Otherness *is* what makes calmness real rather than performed.

The reopener, when it comes, is **A1 pre-suasive reset**. Not a
continuation. Not "hey stranger". Not "sorry I disappeared". A fresh
first impression — present-tense, lead with energy, zero reference
to the gap. The gap is information she already has; commenting on
it is the leak.

**Objective.** Match her absence with calm. If she returns, meet her
at the temperature she returns at — no warmer, no colder, no
scorekeeping. If you reopen, do it from your own world: present-
tense observation, fresh hook, no acknowledgement of the silence.
The phase is won by what's *not* sent at least as often as by what is.

## Phase-progress rubric (re-engagement stages, not 0-100)

Recovery is off-axis — it doesn't have a 0-100 progression because
it isn't a ladder rung. It has *stages of re-engagement* after the
silence or cooling. The score in `phase_progress.recovery` (when
tracked) reads roughly:

| Stage | What it looks like | Psychological reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Cooling** (just entered) | 48-96h silence after substantive turn, or her last reply was a noticeable temperature drop. No reopener attempted yet. | Attachment system activated. The right move is often *no move*. Send protest behaviour now and burn the bridge; let the silence sit. **H2** in mirror mode — match her absence with yours. |
| **Reopener sent, no response** | Clean reopener attempted, no reply yet (typically <72h since reopener). | The reopener is being read or not. The mechanism the reopener has to fire is **A1 pre-suasive reset** — fresh first impression. Triple-texting now (chasing the reopener) is the canonical failure. One reopener, then patience. |
| **Reopener sent, cold reply** | Short ack, no question back, energy clearly below prior thread. | She's testing the temperature. Match her one-step-up — a slight temperature raise without overshooting. Don't reference the gap. Don't reference the prior thread's collapse. **A9 reactance** + **A14 otherness** carry the frame: he has his own thing going. |
| **Reopener sent, warm reply** | Substantive reply, asks back, energy at or near prior thread peak. | Recovery succeeded. The thread re-enters whatever phase her warmth is in (usually rapport or escalation). Treat as continuation, not as Stage Two of recovery. |
| **Failed recovery** | Two reopener attempts, no warm reply. | Phase exits to `dead` or `just-being-polite`. Further attempts trip the anxious-attachment-induction line and burn the bridge. |

**Recovery has no hard prerequisite gate** because it isn't on the
ladder. The trigger condition is the silence/cooling itself. The
exit condition is either re-warmth (back to active phase) or repeat
failure (`dead`).

## What advances this dimension (mechanisms)

- **H2 calculated absence (mirrored).** Match her absence with
  yours. The withdrawal that makes presence felt — but in recovery,
  the presence she feels is yours *not chasing*. Single most
  leveraged recovery move that exists.
- **A9 reactance.** Her freedom to ignore is the freedom; reasserting
  her exercise of his attention requires that returning to him cost
  her nothing. Calm space is what makes return un-pressured. Anxious
  pursuit makes return cost her social capital.
- **A10 secure-base sociometer signal.** Calm posture in his messages
  reads as *his gauge is fine*, which is downstream evidence her
  gauge can re-warm. Anxious posture reads as *his gauge is panicking*,
  which collapses her gauge.
- **A1 pre-suasive reset.** The reopener installs a *new* frame —
  not a continuation. Anchored on present-tense observation: "just
  walked past that bar you mentioned, can confirm it's still
  hostile to humans." The gap is information; commenting on the
  gap leaks; ignoring the gap holds frame.
- **A14 otherness via real life.** The reopener *is* a piece of
  his life leaking through. He's not "reaching out"; he's mentioning
  something present-tense from his world that happens to involve her
  context. The structure: *I noticed something — thought of you —
  here's the texture*, never *I noticed you weren't responding —
  here's me trying again*.
- **A15 Zeigarnik via callback.** Bring back a specific earlier
  detail and pivot to a new angle. Use when the present-tense hook
  is thin but a specific earlier thread had genuine pull. The
  callback proves attention without performing it.
- **A18 brevity, sharpened.** Recovery messages are short. One line.
  The brevity *is* the calm signal. Long recovery messages stack
  multiple leaks: explanation, length, anxiety. Short messages
  contain none.
- **A17 Voss labels (rare).** When she returns with a label-able
  state ("yeah it's been hectic"), one mirror-label closes it and
  redirects: "sounds like a week — what's actually been good?"
  Validates without being a therapy session.
- **F8 Star register, residual.** Slightly distant, slightly luminous,
  letting her project. The reopener that anchors on his world and
  leaves space for her imagination is operating in this register
  whether or not it's named.

## What stalls or regresses it (failure modes)

- **"Did I say something wrong?" (A10 sociometer leak — terminal).**
  The textbook anxious-attachment-coded message. Signals his gauge
  is panicking, which collapses hers. Single most damaging recovery
  move; in many cases, ends the thread by itself.
- **"Hey stranger" / "long time no talk" / "sorry I disappeared"
  (A10 leak + A1 reset failure).** Each variant references the gap,
  which puts her into evaluator-frame ("what does he want me to
  say to that?") and resets her gauge low. The reopener has to be
  a fresh first impression, not a comment on the previous one.
- **Triple-texting after silence (anxious-attachment activation).**
  Each successive message after no-reply lowers his gauge further
  and activates her avoidant withdrawal more strongly. The asymmetry
  is steep — one triple-text after silence often kills threads that
  one calm reopener would have saved.
- **Apologising for being slow (A10 leak + A16 violation).** When
  *he's* the one who took time to reply: "sorry, work's been
  insane!" Inverts the frame — he's apologising for having a life,
  which inverts the **A14 otherness** that was carrying him through
  the gap. Don't apologise for the silence in either direction.
- **Ghost-protest / cold-and-punishing (avoidant-coercive signal —
  worst attachment read).** She slow-replied; he goes cold and
  curt to "make a point". Reads as the worst of all attachment
  signatures. Meet her at the temperature she's at; no scorekeeping.
- **Over-explaining the gap ("yeah I went silent because work blew
  up and then I was travelling and...") (A2 inversion + A18 length
  penalty + A10 leak).** Long explanation collapses the gradient,
  signals defensiveness, and treats the gap as something requiring
  account. The gap doesn't require account.
- **Doubling down on whatever didn't land (A12 miscalibration
  retry).** A neg landed wrong, the thread cooled, the reopener
  doubles the same neg harder. The first miscalibration was data
  about her temperature; doubling it ignores the data and reads as
  not-listening.
- **Over-warm reopener (over-correction → A12 inversion).** "Hey,
  I've really missed talking to you" — overshoots in the opposite
  direction of cold-and-punishing, lands as needy/lovebombing,
  closes the desire-gap (A14 collapse) by force. The reopener is
  cool-and-fresh, not warm-and-confessional.
- **Multiple reopener attempts (anxious-attachment compound).** Two
  attempts is the upper bound for `extended-window`; one for
  everything shorter. Three+ reopeners is functionally a triple-
  text spread across days and reads identically.

## Strategies in this phase

- **`recovery-clean-reopen`** — *A1 pre-suasive reset + A14 otherness*.
  Fresh thread, zero reference to the gap. Anchored on present-tense
  observation from his world. Default when there's a real new hook.
- **`recovery-bold-reset`** — *A1 reset + I11 statement of intent +
  A7 real scarcity*. New energy from his world that points
  immediately at a meet. "Back in town this weekend. Need a drink
  partner thursday." Use when prior thread had escalation-warmth
  and the silence is short enough that the meet is still on the
  table.
- **`recovery-callback-pivot`** — *A15 Zeigarnik + A11 self-
  perception*. Bring back a specific earlier detail and pivot to
  a new angle. Use when the new-hook is thin but a specific
  earlier thread had genuine pull.

## Goal-type weighting

- **`quick-meet-window`** — recover once, then move on. Timeline
  doesn't permit multiple attempts. **`recovery-bold-reset`** is the
  natural strategy — the silence and the meet-window are competing
  for the same days, so the reopener has to point directly at the
  meet to be worth sending at all.
- **`flexible-short-window`** — one shot, then archive. The reopener
  is calmer, less direct than `quick-meet-window`. **clean-reopen**
  default; bold-reset only if signals were hot pre-silence.
- **`extended-window`** — can attempt twice spaced out (~5-7 days
  apart). **`recovery-callback-pivot`** is native here — long arcs
  generate specific earlier details worth callback. **F8 Star** and
  **A13 variable-ratio** dynamics cushion the gap; absences are
  expected texture in this archetype.
- **`reconnect-later`** — recovery and reconnect-later overlap. The
  distinction: recovery is recovering a *current* thread; reconnect-
  later is reopening after a thread has formally cooled (weeks-to-
  months gap, often after a stated leaving-town). Same toolkit, but
  reconnect-later operates from a clean slate; recovery operates
  with prior-thread context her brain still remembers.
- **`decline-gracefully`** — recovery is the wrong move. The goal
  was clean exit; pursuit after silence inverts it. Let it go.
- **`just-being-polite`** — recovery doesn't activate; the holding-
  pattern reply rate already accounts for silence.

## Phase exit / next-phase prerequisite

→ **back to active phase** (usually rapport or escalation) — when
the reopener generates a warm reply. The thread resumes wherever her
warmth indicates; don't force it back to where it was pre-silence,
read where it actually is now.

→ **`dead`** — second recovery attempt failed, or single attempt at
shorter goal-types failed. The phase has tried; further attempts
cross from recovery into anxious-attachment-induction. The bridge
stays open passively (she can always re-engage on her own), but no
further pursuit. Per **decline-gracefully** dynamics if she eventually
surfaces with explicit closure.

→ **`just-being-polite`** — middle ground. Reopener got a polite-
but-cold reply; thread isn't dead but isn't recoverable to active
pursuit either. Match her cadence, no escalation moves, drift.
