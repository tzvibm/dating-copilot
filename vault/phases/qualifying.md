# Phase: qualifying

## Psychological objective

Qualifying inverts the frame. Up through rapport, the implicit question
running in the background is *will she like me?*; in qualifying, the
running question becomes *will I like her?* — and her brain registers
the inversion. This is **reactance** (A9) and **autonomy** (A16) doing
their highest-leverage work. When a freedom (his approval, his
continued interest) is positioned as not-yet-granted, motivation to
claim it rises; and when she's choosing to demonstrate fit rather
than being asked to perform, the **self-perception ladder** (A11)
locks her stated interest in as her own conclusion.

The dominant subconscious dynamic is **scarcity** (A7) of *him* —
real scarcity, signalled by having standards rather than by claiming
to. Every move audits whether she fits, never apologetically, never
performatively. Greene's **deepening effect** lives here: stir up the
mildly transgressive (H3), mix pleasure with pain (H4), introduce
the disqualifier that she now wants to clear. The **F5 Coquette**
register is structurally native to qualifying — almost-attainable,
almost-distant, the pendulum is the move. Tyler Durden's **statement
of intent** logic (I11) shows up in compressed form: he names what
he's looking for, what would disqualify her, what would close the
deal. She does the work of demonstrating she clears the bar — and
because the action is hers, the conclusion (Bem A11) is hers.

The constraint: qualifying requires warm rapport substrate. Reactance
without warmth lands as arrogance, because her brain has no positive
context to misattribute the tension into. The mechanism only fires
on freedoms she's already mildly invested in.

**Objective.** Install a frame in which she's clearing a bar. Use
forks-with-takes (A16 competence-supportive), playful disqualifiers
(A9), and selective liking (A6) to elicit qualifying moves from her.
Each demonstrated fit-signal she volunteers ratchets self-perception
(A11) further. Read her response shape as much as content: does she
play, defend, or fold under light tension (I5 IOI/IOD)? That's the
qualifying signal.

## Phase-progress rubric (this dimension)

| Score | What it looks like | Psychological reading |
|---|---|---|
| **0-30** | Bio-only inference. Surface preferences (genre, cuisine). She's answering, not revealing. | No fit-demonstration has occurred. Reactance frame hasn't installed — she doesn't yet sense there's a bar to clear. **F5 Coquette** and **A9** moves don't have substrate. |
| **30-60** | One real preference or value shared. She's responded to a take with a take of her own. Some texture on what she actually wants. | Frame inversion engaging. Bem mechanism (A11) starting — her stated take is now self-evidence she's the kind of person who has takes. Light disqualifier (I3) lands without injury. |
| **60-100** | Mutual texture, taste signals exchanged, light disagreement honest. She's qualified herself unprompted ("I'm not the planner type", "I'd hate that bar") — knowing the categorisation matters. | Reactance fully fired. She's now invested in his read. Self-perception (A11) locked: she's been audibly the-kind-of-woman-who-passes. **A12 misattribution** working at full strength because tension and warmth are alternating cleanly. **F5 Coquette** territory. |

**Hard prerequisite gate:** escalation requires qualifying ≥ 60 in
any goal_type that runs through this phase. Skipping qualifying when
the substrate isn't ready means the meet ask lands without felt fit
— she says yes from politeness, not pull, and meets evaporate or
flake. (`quick-meet-window` is the documented exception: qualifying
folds into rapport on compressed timelines.)

## What advances this dimension (mechanisms)

- **A9 reactance / playful disqualifier.** "You'd have to convince me
  you're not just another spotify-list foodie." The standard is game-
  able — she chose to be a foodie, she can choose how to defend it.
  Targets opinions and self-categories, never traits. Installs a
  freedom she now wants to claim.
- **A16 autonomy / forks-with-takes.** "Wine bar with the chaos crowd
  or the actually-quiet one — your call, but one of those is wrong."
  Frames her as co-author with a take of her own to bring. Competence-
  supportive (A16) — she gets to be smart. The take she returns is
  qualifying signal.
- **A11 self-perception ratchet.** Every preference she volunteers
  becomes self-evidence. Bem does the work invisibly: she's labelled
  herself, to herself, and the label sticks because she said it.
- **A6 liking via selective expression.** Selective approval, earned.
  "Ok that's the right answer" lands; "you seem great" doesn't. The
  approval has to be discriminating to register as signal.
- **I3 the neg.** Mid-rapport-into-qualifying staple. Backhanded
  observation aimed at chosen categories. Sociometer signal (A10) —
  registers that he's not auditioning for her approval. Use sparingly;
  one neg per several turns, not stacked.
- **I12 five-questions / qualifying spike.** "Ok five questions, fast,
  no thinking." Compresses self-perception (A11), reciprocity (A2),
  and commitment-ladder (A3) into one structured exchange. Risk:
  feels gamey if deployed cold — best when energy is already high
  but topics have stayed surface.
- **H3 stir up the transgressive.** Mild taboo, edge, the slightly
  off-limits implication. Misattribution (A12) via transgression —
  the arousal her cortex labels as attraction. Implication of
  transgression, not the transgression itself.
- **H4 mix pleasure with pain.** Compliment + tease alternation.
  Variable-ratio (A13) at the message level. The texture is what
  creates pull — pure pleasure habituates, pure pain extinguishes,
  alternation locks attention.
- **F5 Coquette register.** Almost-attainable, almost-distant. Pull
  back exactly when she leans in. Among the most operationally
  powerful Greene types in qualifying — and the easiest to overdo.
  Stops the moment it tips into anxious-attachment-induction.
- **A19 incidental status / agency cues.** Frame-stability under her
  testing. Humour as competence-signal. Real takes about real things.
  Mate-preference asymmetries (A19) reward agency cues *when
  incidental* — claiming inverts (see A5 failure mode).

## What stalls or regresses it (failure modes)

- **Interview mode (A2 inversion + A16 violation).** Three "what
  about you" questions in a row turns qualifying into HR-screen.
  She's evaluating his ability to hold a frame, not his curiosity
  about her. One move per turn.
- **Sycophancy (A6 inversion + A12 collapse).** Agreeing with
  everything kills the read on both sides. Pure pleasantness
  generates no arousal to misattribute (A12). Light honest
  disagreement is the move; "totally, same!" is the leak.
- **Performing values to seem compatible (A14 otherness collapse +
  authenticity-detector).** Pretending to share a take you don't
  hold reads as performance the moment her bullshit-detector pings.
  Once detected, the entire frame collapses — she stops believing
  any prior signal.
- **Disqualifier landing on real flaw (A9 miscalibration).** Cruelty,
  not flirtation. The brain doesn't misattribute pain into
  attraction; pain registers as a reason to leave. Target chosen
  categories, never the woman herself.
- **Disqualifier without warm substrate (rapport gate violation).**
  Reactance needs a freedom she's already mildly invested in. A neg
  fired at 30-rapport reads as arrogance, not flirtation. The
  mechanism literally doesn't fire — there's no warmth context for
  the cortex to label tension as.
- **Stretching qualifying into pen-pal-zone (A14 + A3 stall).** Past
  ~15 messages of texture-without-escalation, the desire-gap closes
  by attrition. The thread metabolises into intellectual
  acquaintanceship. Move to escalation; this phase has a ceiling.
- **Stacked negs (A9 saturation + A10 leak).** More than one push
  per turn dilutes and starts reading as hostility, not play. One
  push-pull, one neg, then warmth.
- **Earnest-mode collapse (F5 inversion).** Dropping the playful
  register to "really though, I want to know what you're looking for"
  — collapses the Coquette pendulum, reads as anxious-attachment
  pre-screening. Save the directness for logistics (where it works);
  in qualifying it kills tension.
- **Five-questions deployed cold (I12 miscalibration).** Without
  high-energy substrate, structured exchange feels gamey and signals
  optimisation. Only deploy when she's already volunteering
  substantively.

## Strategies in this phase

- **`qualifying-ask-take`** — *A16 fork-with-take + A11 self-
  perception ratchet*. Ask her opinion on something with a fork in
  it. Reveals real texture and gives the next move material.
- **`rapport-share-then-volley`** — *A2 reciprocity + A11 ladder*.
  Your share + her response is among the highest-signal qualifying
  reads. Reusable across the rapport/qualifying boundary.
- **`rapport-mirror-then-pivot`** — *H6 enter their spirit + A14
  preserved*. Extends rapport by introducing one new angle that
  tests fit without interrogating.
- **`rapport-callback`** — *A11 self-perception + A15 loop closure*.
  Her response to a callback tells you what she actually cared about
  earlier. High-signal read.
- **`rapport-playful-tease`** — *A12 misattribution + I3 neg + H4
  pleasure-pain*. Her response is the qualifying signal: does she
  play, defend, or fold? Three different reads, three different
  next moves.
- **`rapport-frame-control`** — *I7 frame control + I4 AMOG*. When
  she tests, brief acknowledge + redirect. Tells you whether she
  escalates the test (high signal — fight or flirt energy) or rolls
  (warm acquiescence).
- **`rapport-social-proof`** — *A4 incidental + I8 phantom*. Drop
  offhand life-context. Tells you whether your life intrigues her
  or not — high-signal read on whether she's a fit for the kind of
  match this would actually be.
- **`rapport-disqualifier`** — *A9 reactance + F5 Coquette*. Playful
  "you'd have to convince me…". Use when rapport is warm and you
  want to raise temperature ahead of escalation. Native to qualifying.

## Goal-type weighting

- **`quick-meet-window`** — qualifying compresses or folds into
  rapport. Target ~30-50, not 60+. The pre-meet read happens in
  person; over-qualifying mid-text burns the timeline. **I11 statement
  of intent** can collapse qualifying-into-logistics in one move when
  energy is already loud.
- **`flexible-short-window`** — qualify until confident; don't rush.
  60+ before the meet ask. Single most reliable phase for predicting
  whether the meet will click.
- **`extended-window`** — natural home alongside rapport. Can run a
  week+ here. **F5 Coquette** and **A13 variable-ratio** do
  disproportionate work — qualifying tension sustains the thread when
  the meet isn't imminent.
- **`reconnect-later`** — qualifying resets when the thread reopens;
  don't resume mid-fit-read.
- **`decline-gracefully`** — qualifying is structurally wrong. The
  phase generates pull; the goal is graceful exit. Skip.

## Phase exit / next-phase prerequisite

→ **`escalation`** activates when **qualifying ≥ 60**: she's
volunteered taste signals, light honest disagreement has happened,
mutual texture is real. The fit-read has produced enough mutual
investment that an explicit meet ask reads as continuation, not
transaction. Reactance and self-perception have done their work — her
felt interest is now her own (Bem A11), which means the meet ask
isn't asking her for something; it's giving her access to something
she's already invested in.

→ **`recovery`** if energy drops or she goes quiet on a qualifying
push. Common signal: a neg or disqualifier landed wrong; the response
is silence or a cold one-liner. Treat as recovery, don't double down.

→ **`dead`** if a clear incompatibility surfaces (different cities
without overlap, different goals stated explicitly, value-level
mismatch). Qualifying did its job — the phase exists *to* surface
this. Disengage cleanly per **decline-gracefully** dynamics.
