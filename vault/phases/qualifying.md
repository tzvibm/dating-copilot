# Phase: qualifying

Conversation has texture. Now both sides are reading the other for
fit before committing to meeting. Goal: find the read on whether you
two would actually click in person.

## You're in this phase when

- 8-15 messages in.
- Topic has gone past surface; she's volunteered something unprompted.
- Neither side has proposed a meet yet, but the door is open.

## What works

- One real opinion or position from you per turn.
- Asking the kind of question that tells you something — not "what
  do you do" but "what does your perfect day look like that isn't
  work".
- Light disagreement when honest. Sycophancy kills the read.

## What doesn't

- Treating qualifying like an interview ("3 questions in a row").
- Performing values you don't hold to seem compatible.
- Stretching qualifying past 15 messages — it becomes pen-pals.

## Strategies in this phase

- `qualifying-ask-take` — ask her opinion on something with a fork
  in it. Reveals something real and gives you texture for the next
  move.
- `rapport-share-then-volley` — your share + her response is a key
  qualifying signal.
- `rapport-mirror-then-pivot` — extends rapport by introducing a new
  angle that tests fit.
- `rapport-callback` — proves attention; her response to a callback
  tells you what she actually cared about earlier.
- `rapport-playful-tease` — tease her takes / vibe. Her response is
  qualifying signal — does she play, defend, or fold?
- `rapport-frame-control` — when she tests, brief acknowledge +
  redirect. Tells you whether she escalates the test or rolls.
- `rapport-social-proof` — drop offhand life-context. Tells you
  whether your life intrigues her or not.
- `rapport-disqualifier` — playful "you'd have to convince me…"
  Use when rapport is warm and you want to raise temperature
  before the meet ask.

## Goal-type interactions

- `quick-meet-window` — short qualifying. 2-3 turns max, then escalate.
- `flexible-short-window` — qualify until you're confident. Don't rush.
- `extended-window` — natural home; can spend a week+ here.
- `decline-gracefully` — minimal engagement; don't qualify, drift.

## Phase exit

→ `escalation` — mutual reciprocation across at least one Q/A pair,
both sides warm, ready to move toward meeting.
→ `recovery` — energy drops or she goes quiet.
→ `dead` — clear incompatibility surfaced.
