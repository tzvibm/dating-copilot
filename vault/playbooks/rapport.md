# Playbook: rapport

She replied. There's a thread going. Goal: build enough of a real read
on each other that proposing a meet doesn't feel transactional.

## Signals you're in this phase

- 4–10 messages in.
- She's asking questions back, not just answering.
- Topic has gone past "where you from / what do you do".

## What works

- Following the thread she opened, not pivoting.
- Sharing one concrete thing about yourself per reply, not a résumé.
- Calibrated callbacks to earlier in the chat.

## What doesn't

- Interview mode (4 questions in a row).
- Jumping to logistics on the first sign of warmth.
- Performing — long, polished paragraphs.

## Goal-type interactions

- `quick-meet-window` — keep rapport short (3–5 of your turns). Use
  `escalation-soft-time-anchor` once she reciprocates a question with
  warmth.
- `flexible-short-window` — let it breathe; escalate when interesting.
- `extended-window` — fine to spend a week here.
