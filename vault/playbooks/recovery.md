# Playbook: recovery

A previous turn went wrong (no reply for too long, a flat reaction, or
a misread). Goal: re-open without explaining or apologising your way out.

## Signals you're in this phase

- 48–96 hours of silence after a substantive turn from you.
- A reply that's noticeably colder than the prior cadence.
- A misread you can name (e.g. wrong tone, wrong reference).

## What works

- A clean re-open that doesn't reference the silence.
- A genuine callback to something earlier she said.
- Brevity. One line.

## What doesn't

- "Sorry for the delay" / "Did I say something wrong?" — frames you
  as anxious.
- Stacking three messages in a row.
- Doubling down on whatever didn't land the first time.

## Goal-type interactions

- `quick-meet-window` — recover once, then move on if it doesn't take.
- `flexible-short-window` — one shot, then archive.
- `decline-gracefully` — recovery is the wrong move; let it go.
