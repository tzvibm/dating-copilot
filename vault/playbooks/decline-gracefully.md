# Playbook: decline-gracefully

Goals diverged. You're not pursuing this match anymore but a hard
ghost is unkind. Goal: wind down honestly without leading her on.

## Premise

You've decided this isn't going anywhere — wrong fit, wrong timing,
your situation changed. The win is closing the loop without leaving
her wondering or feeling discarded.

## Pacing target

- Slower replies are fine and expected on this playbook.
- One or two short, friendly turns to land softly. Then mutual
  silence.
- No new threads, no callbacks to keep things alive.

## Phase-by-phase

### Whatever phase you're in
The goal change is the move. The phase becomes secondary.

- If you're in **opener** or **rapport**: don't open. Or one short
  closing line if she opened first ("life got hectic, sorry I
  flaked, good luck out there").
- If you're in **qualifying** or later: name it gently. "honestly
  I don't think the timing is right for me — appreciate the chats."

### What works

- Short. Two sentences max.
- Honest framing without being clinical. "Timing isn't right for me"
  is fine. "I've decided to focus on myself right now" is not.
- Don't leave a door cracked. "Maybe later" gives false hope.

### What doesn't

- Listing reasons. She didn't ask for a memo.
- Explaining how you got here. Closure is for you, not her.
- "It's not you, it's me." Save the cliché.

## Failure modes

- **Soft-ghosting forever**: you neither close nor reply. She's left
  to figure it out. This playbook exists specifically to avoid that.
- **Over-explaining**: a paragraph of context. She'll feel
  performed at.
- **Reopening regret**: 3 weeks later you reach out "to check in".
  Don't. The goal type was real when you set it.

## What success looks like

A short, kind close. She knows where she stands. Neither of you
spends another week wondering.
