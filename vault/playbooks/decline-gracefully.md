# Playbook: decline-gracefully

The arc when goals have diverged and continuing would mislead. Wind
down without leaving her wondering. The exit itself is the move.

## Psychological frame

This goal_type runs on **A16 autonomy** + **A10 sociometer** with
all persuasion off the table. The core read: a clean decline is the
move that **preserves option-value**. Every outcome later — overlap
in a new city, mutual context shifting, archetype-fit rotation, her
friend introducing him to someone else — is gated by how her
**sociometer** read of him stands at the moment of disengagement.
A man who can be told no without flinching, who doesn't argue or
sulk or re-pitch, registers on her gauge as **frame-stable under
loss**. That reading is one of the most durable mate-value cues in
the catalog (A19 mate-preference for frame-stability), and it's the
specific reading that keeps the door from being nailed shut.

The frame to install at exit: *I have a life that's running, this
isn't going to fit, and I'm fine.* No protest behavior, no harvested
reassurance, no last-chance reframe. **A14 otherness** stays intact
on the way out — she sees a man with his own gravitational center
who isn't reorganising around her response. **A1 pre-suasion** in
reverse: the closing message anchors the *final* impression, which
is the one she'll carry forward into any future overlap. Make it
clean, brief, and frame-confident.

This is the inverse of the quick-meet **I11 statement-of-intent**
move: same mechanism family (frame confidence carrying the move),
opposite vector. Direct game says *I want this and I'm clear about
it.* Direct decline says *this isn't it and I'm clear about it.*
Both rely on **A10 sociometer-positive** signal — neither asks for
permission, neither harvests reassurance, neither argues the frame.

## Phase-progress allocation

No axis is being raised. The exit itself is the move; phase becomes
secondary. The implicit allocation:

- **opener / rapport / qualifying / escalation / logistics / confirm
  → frozen.** No phase-progress moves on this goal_type. Strategy
  cards filter out via `incompatible_goals: [decline-gracefully]`.
- **What's actually being protected:** her sociometer read of him at
  the moment of disengagement, which is the only thing that keeps
  any future overlap available.

## Timeline

- **Slower replies are fine and expected.** The variable-ratio
  schedule (A13) running at low frequency naturally tapers without
  ceremony.
- **One or two short turns to land softly,** then mutual silence.
- **No callbacks, no new threads, no re-pings to keep things alive.**
  Each post-decline contact would be re-pursuing under cover of
  closure — the mechanism collapses immediately on detection.

If you're already deep in a thread (qualifying or later), one short
honest line. If you're at opener/rapport stage, often the right move
is no message at all — there's nothing built that needs a closing
ceremony.

## Strategy priors

**No active strategies fire on this goal_type.** Every strategy in
the library has `decline-gracefully` in its `incompatible_goals` (or
should). The agent should refuse to generate suggestions and surface
to user: *"goal is `decline-gracefully` — no strategy fires. Want me
to draft a one-line wind-down?"*

If the user wants the line drafted, the move follows two principles:

- **Short, two sentences max.** Length signals investment; the
  investment shouldn't be there.
- **Honest framing without being clinical.** "Timing isn't right for
  me" is fine. "I've decided to focus on myself right now" is
  performance-coded and reads as a script.

What's already been said by phase position:

- **At opener / rapport** — usually no message at all. If she opened
  first and a non-reply would be conspicuous, one short line: *"life
  got hectic, sorry I flaked, good luck out there."*
- **At qualifying or later** — name it gently: *"honestly I don't
  think the timing is right for me. appreciated the chats."*

What never appears:

- Lists of reasons. Memo-shaped messages **A2-invert** (over-
  investment at exit) and signal he needs her to understand, which
  is sociometer leak.
- "It's not you, it's me" — script-detection, frame collapse.
- Cracked doors. "Maybe later" / "if anything changes" gives false
  hope and re-introduces evaluation-frame she now has to maintain.
  **A14 otherness** + **A16 autonomy** both inverted — she's left
  holding a future she didn't agree to.

## Failure modes (goal-specific)

- **Re-pitching after the close → A10 sociometer leak + frame
  collapse.** "But what if we just got coffee once?" inverts the
  whole exit. The close registered frame-stability; the re-pitch
  retracts it and replays the move at lower mate-value than before.
  The sociometer reading drops further than if he'd never declined
  at all.
- **Sulky one-word replies → A10 leak + protest-behavior signal.**
  Going cold-but-still-engaged reads as avoidant-coercive
  (Bowlby/Hazan-Shaver attachment dynamics). Worse than the silence
  it's pretending to be, because it signals injury without
  declaring it.
- **Apologetic withdrawal that re-pursues → A2 inversion + A14
  collapse.** "I'm so sorry, I just feel like maybe we should talk
  about whether…" — over-investment at exit, harvesting reassurance
  under cover of closure. The mechanism the playbook exists to
  prevent.
- **Re-opening 3 weeks later → A15 + A1 frame violation.** "Hey,
  just thought I'd check in" 3 weeks after a clean close inverts the
  preserved option-value. The earlier exit was the move; reopening
  unprompted reads as the close having been performance, which
  retroactively collapses its frame-stability signal.
- **Memo-style explanation → A2 over-investment at exit.** Long
  message detailing the reasoning. The length itself signals he
  needs her to validate the exit, which is the exact sociometer leak
  the clean close exists to avoid.
- **Soft-ghosting forever → A14 + A16 dual-failure.** Neither close
  nor reply, leaves her to figure it out. Reads as low-agency
  avoidance (failure to hold a frame at all), which lands worse than
  a clean close on her gauge — and burns the option-value the
  playbook is supposed to preserve.
- **Cracked-door framing → A14 + A16 inversion.** "Maybe down the
  road" leaves her holding evaluator-frame indefinitely. The close
  must actually close to preserve the option of a *clean* future
  reopen on different terms.

## Strategy selection priors

This goal_type explicitly **suppresses every persuasion mechanism**.
None of the standard rapport/escalation/logistics moves apply.

What's left as live signal:

- **A10 sociometer-positive disengagement** — frame-stable, brief,
  no harvested reassurance. The single mechanism the playbook is
  organised around.
- **A14 otherness** preserved through the exit — life that's running,
  no reorganising around her response.
- **A16 autonomy** preserved on her side — no obligation to respond,
  no evaluation she has to perform, no future she has to manage.
- **A1 pre-suasion in reverse** — the closing message anchors the
  *final* read, which is the one that determines whether anything
  is recoverable later.
- **A19 mate-preference for frame-stability** — frame-confidence
  *under loss* is among the most durable signals; the clean close
  is one of the few moments to display it cleanly.

What's explicitly off:

- **A3 commitment ladder** — no rungs being climbed; reverse motion
  isn't on the ladder at all.
- **A9 reactance / takeaway** — using the close as a takeaway move
  to provoke pursuit is the **A10 leak** mechanism in costume.
- **A15 open loops** — closing must close. Loops left open here
  metabolise into ambiguity she's responsible for resolving, which
  is the autonomy violation the playbook exits to avoid.
- **All Greene F-types** — registers are for engagement; no
  engagement to register.
- **All PUA tactics (I-series)** — same.
