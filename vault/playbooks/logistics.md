# Playbook: logistics

She said yes in principle. Now: when, where, how. Goal: lock a real
meet without losing momentum to scheduling theatre.

## Signals you're in this phase

- She agreed to meet (explicitly or near-explicitly).
- The conversation is now about coordinating, not connecting.

## What works

- Two-option format: "Thursday or Friday?" beats open-ended.
- Suggesting a specific place that fits both your locations.
- Confirming the day-of with one short message.

## What doesn't

- Volleying scheduling indefinitely.
- Vague "let me know when you're free".
- Over-detailing the plan before she's confirmed.
