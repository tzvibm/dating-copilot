# Playbook: flexible-short-window

The default arc. Same physical overlap as `quick-meet-window` (~5–14
days), no push. If it clicks, you'll meet; if not, no pressure either
way.

## Psychological frame

This goal_type runs on the standard mechanism stack at default
weights: **A6 liking via selective expression** + **A3 commitment
ladder** + light **A15 Zeigarnik / open loops**. The rapport-into-
logistics arc unfolds at natural pace — no compression from scarcity,
no extension into pen-pal territory. **A2 reciprocity gradient** is
the texture you watch most closely; per-turn investment slightly under
hers is the rule that compounds across the window.

The frame to install: *I have time and a point of view; I'm
interested in the read more than the close*. **A14 otherness** still
matters — leading with your own energy preserves the desire-gap — but
without scarcity doing extra ceiling work, **A18 hyperpersonal
asymmetry** is the lever that lets a strong early frame keep
compounding through ambiguous later messages. The single biggest
risk on this goal_type is the timeline lying to itself: the meet
window is real even when the urgency isn't. **Match-decay is real**
— the longer between match and meet, the lower the meet probability,
regardless of how warm the thread feels in the moment. The thread
that feels good at day 12 is statistically going cold at day 14.

## Phase-progress allocation

- **opener** — target 60+. Standard ceiling work via **A1**; no
  scarcity bonus, frame has to carry on its own.
- **rapport** — target 60–80. **A2 reciprocity**, **A6 liking**, and
  **A12 misattribution** all get full weight. Real texture is the
  point — this is where the read happens.
- **qualifying** — target 50–70. **A11 self-perception** and **A9
  reactance** get standard weight. One or two real fork-questions per
  thread, not every turn.
- **escalation** — target 60+ before logistics fires. Triggered on
  her cue (volunteered place, asked when you're free, hinted at
  meeting), not on the clock. **A3 ladder** runs at default rung
  spacing.
- **logistics** — target 80+. Two-option close, real plan, easy out
  (A16 + A17).
- **confirm** — short, warm, day-of only.

## Timeline

Field-tested:

- **Days 0–1** — opener compounds; rapport begins.
- **Days 1–4** — rapport. 5–8 of your turns is fine if conversation
  is alive. Don't compress; let the read happen.
- **Days 3–6** — qualifying threaded through rapport. Real fit-tests,
  not procedural ones.
- **Days 5–9** — escalation when she signals. Field-tested ask
  window: **messages 7–11 of a warm thread**, sometimes **9–12** if
  signals are tepid and temperature is still climbing.
- **Days 7–12** — logistics + meet.

**The 10-day cliff.** After ~10 days the thread is decaying regardless
of how it feels. **A15 open loops** stop compounding once she's
context-shifted away from "this is a live possibility" — by day 14
the thread is asleep even if neither of you has said anything cold.
If you're past day 8 with no escalation cue, the move is to surface
the question — escalate or accept that the read is "no spark."
Drifting past 14 days isn't patience; it's avoiding the read.

## Strategy priors

**opener** — favored: `opener-callback-to-photo` (A2 specific
noticing, top-converter when available), `opener-lead-with-take` (A14
+ I1 cocky-funny), `opener-flirty-edge` when voice permits.
Inappropriate: `opener-scarcity-direct-ask` — no real scarcity to
lean on, would manufacture urgency the timeline doesn't support.

**rapport** — favored: full toolkit, rotated so you're not running
the same play twice. `rapport-mirror-then-pivot`,
`rapport-share-then-volley`, `rapport-callback` (after a real detail
has dropped — A11 self-perception lock-in), `rapport-playful-tease`
(A12 light tension), `rapport-frame-control` (I7) on shit-tests,
`rapport-social-proof` in passing (A4 incidental, A8 phantom).
Re-weight up **F6 Charmer** (return-attention reads as time-luxury
the timeline allows) and **F2 Ideal Lover** if her bio gives a clear
unmet-need read. **I2 push-pull** at one-per-turn cadence.

**qualifying** — favored: `qualifying-ask-take` (A11 + A16
competence), `rapport-share-then-volley` for testing fit while
keeping warmth, `rapport-disqualifier` (A9 reactance) if rapport is
warm. **I12 five-questions** when energy is high but topics have
been surface.

**escalation** — favored: `escalation-time-bridge` first (H7
insinuation, A15 future-pace) — drop "when we get drinks" to
normalise the meet without asking; `escalation-soft-time-anchor`
once she's signaled; `escalation-activity-anchor` if she's named
something specific; `escalation-specific-invite` after at least one
warm reciprocation toward meeting. Less appropriate:
`escalation-direct-ask` unless she's clearly a planner — direct game
without scarcity lacks its mechanism substrate (I11 needs A7 to
carry).

**logistics** — favored: `logistics-two-option-close` (A16 + A17),
`logistics-specific-place` when there's a real city default. If she
defers once, defer gracefully — don't scramble.

## Failure modes (goal-specific)

- **Pen-pal drift past 14 days → A15 open-loop decay + A14 collapse.**
  Open loops require imminent payoff to keep firing; without a meet on
  the horizon the loops metabolise into "this is a chat, not a
  date" and the desire-gap closes. The most expensive failure mode
  on this playbook because it feels fine while it kills the thread.
- **Forced escalation against signal → A16 autonomy violation.**
  Pushing for a meet because the calendar says day 7, not because
  she gave a cue. Reads transactional, the move that worked under
  quick-meet-window inverts here.
- **Mismatched goal-type read between you → A1 frame conflict.**
  She's reading this as quick-meet-window and you're not (or vice
  versa). Surfaces as her escalating faster than you, or you both
  drifting at different cadences. Surface and ratify, don't paper
  over.
- **Comfort-plateau in rapport → A12 stalls + A14 collapse.** Pure
  agreement, no tension, no push, no risk. The thread is "nice" and
  going nowhere — pure pleasure habituates fast (H4 pleasure-with-
  pain failure mode). One light push or H1 mixed-signal move
  re-injects tension.
- **Over-confirmation when warm → A10 sociometer leak.** When she's
  reciprocating well, the temptation is to celebrate it ("you're
  great to talk to"). Indiscriminate liking inverts A6 and reads as
  low discrimination. Express liking selectively and earned, not as
  ambient warmth.

## Strategy selection priors

Mechanism categories that **dominate** under flexible-short-window:

- **A6 liking via selective expression** — the central rapport
  mechanism. Earned, specific, never indiscriminate.
- **A2 reciprocity gradient** — investment slightly under hers,
  watched closely.
- **A3 commitment ladder** at default rung spacing.
- **A15 open loops** with payoff inside the window.
- **A11 self-perception** via micro-investment.
- **F6 Charmer** and **F2 Ideal Lover** as primary registers.
- **H6 enter-their-spirit** (calibrated mirroring) — meet her register
  before nudging it.

Mechanism categories at **standard weight** (no special boost or
suppression):

- **A7 scarcity** — exists in background (you have a life), not
  amplified.
- **A12 misattribution** — used sparingly via tease and push-pull.
- **F5 Coquette** — usable mid-rapport but not characteristic of
  this goal_type's tempo.

Mechanism categories to **suppress**:

- **I11 statement-of-intent** — needs scarcity substrate this
  goal_type doesn't supply.
- **F8 Star** — too ambient for the tight-but-not-urgent timeline.
- **F1 Rake** at high intensity — better matched to extended-window
  or quick-meet contexts.
