# Playbook: opener

Phase where there is no conversation history yet. Goal: get a real reply
that opens up an actual exchange.

## When the match is in this phase

- New match, no messages exchanged.
- Or: stalled match where neither side has said anything substantive.

## What works

- Specific reference to her profile (a photo, a prompt) that shows you
  read it and have a take on it.
- Light teasing only when it's clearly playful.
- Asking a question that isn't googleable.

## What doesn't

- "Hey", "How's your day", "How are you".
- Compliments on appearance — burns the gambit.
- LLM-generic openers ("That's a great prompt!").

## Goal-type interactions

- `quick-meet-window` — opener should already hint at being on a clock
  without making it the headline.
- `flexible-short-window` — open like you have time. Read intent later.
- `decline-gracefully` — don't open at all. Unmatch or leave it.
