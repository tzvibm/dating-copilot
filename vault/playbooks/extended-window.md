# Playbook: extended-window

You both live in the same place (or have a long overlap), and
you're willing to invest more time in messages before meeting.
Still casual; not LTR-shaped.

## Premise

Time pressure is low. Quality of connection is the optimisation
target, not speed. Comfortable spending a week+ in rapport /
qualifying.

## Pacing target

- Days, not hours. Reply windows of 1-12h are fine.
- Rapport can run 7-12 messages.
- Escalate when there's a *reason* (a specific upcoming thing, a
  natural opening), not just because the timer ran out.

## Phase-by-phase

### opener
- Same opener strategies. No travel-window pressure.

### rapport
- Real rapport. 5-10 of your turns. Don't rush.
- All three rapport strategies work; rotate them as the
  conversation shapes.

### qualifying
- This is where most of the value lives.
- Real opinions, real questions, mild disagreement when honest.
- Spend a week here if it's interesting.

### escalation
- Trigger should be a specific thing — a place she mentioned, a
  weekend coming up, a shared interest with a calendar.
- Avoid `escalation-direct-ask` unless rapport is *very* clear.
- `escalation-activity-anchor` is the cleanest move on this playbook.
- `escalation-soft-time-anchor` works when nothing concrete has
  emerged but the warmth has.

### logistics
- Don't sprint. Two-option close is fine; `logistics-specific-place`
  when there's a real venue tied to the conversation.

### confirm
- Day-of confirmation only.

### recovery
- Up to two attempts spaced out (1 week+ apart). If both fail,
  archive without ceremony.

## Failure modes

- **Becoming pen-pals**: rapport extends past 3 weeks with no
  escalation cue. You're now friends-of-the-app. Surface and
  consider transitioning to `decline-gracefully` or proposing a
  no-pressure meet.
- **Premature escalation**: pushing for a meet because of impatience,
  not because of a real signal. Cools fast on this playbook.
- **Underclocking the recovery**: not noticing a 96h+ gap because
  the playbook is "slow." Recovery still applies.

## What success looks like

A meet that happened because both of you wanted it after enough
back-and-forth to actually want it. Higher floor on quality than
`quick-meet-window`; lower hit rate on actually meeting.
