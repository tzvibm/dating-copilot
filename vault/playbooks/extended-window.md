# Playbook: extended-window

The arc when both parties are local (or have a long real overlap) and
nobody's on a hard timer. Casual; not LTR-shaped. Quality of read
takes priority over speed of meet.

## Psychological frame

This goal_type runs on **A15 Zeigarnik / open loops** + **A13
variable-ratio reinforcement** + **A14 otherness / desire-distance**.
Without the meet acting as natural close, the thread has to sustain
its own tension — and the only mechanisms that sustain tension across
days are unresolved narratives, intermittent unpredictable warmth,
and preserved imaginative space. **A18 hyperpersonal** does heavy
lifting here: text strips paralinguistic cues and replaces them with
timing, length, punctuation as the entire signal vocabulary, which
means imagination fills the gaps — and imagination, given a strong
prior frame, fills warm. Channel-jumps (voice note, phone call,
photo from your actual day) do work text alone can't, because they
re-anchor the relational texture without collapsing the desire-gap.

The frame to install: *I'm a real person with a life she can't fully
see, and the unfolding is part of the pleasure*. **F8 The Star**
becomes a live register for the first time — partly absent, partly
luminous, lets her project. **F5 The Coquette** earns its keep here
because the variable schedule has time to operate. The risk profile
inverts compared to quick-meet: there's no clock to fail against, but
there's a worse failure — the thread metabolises into pen-pal-zone,
the desire-gap closes via accumulated daily contact, and what was a
match becomes a parasocial chat. Closing the desire-gap before
proximity is the modal failure on this playbook.

The orienting fact: **extended-window is not infinite-window**. The
meet still has to happen or the thread dies of comfort. Without one
concrete plan-anchor on the horizon — "I'm out there in three weeks,"
"there's a thing I want to drag someone to next month" — the thread
has no telos and **A15 loops** stop being unresolved (which compounds
attention) and become unresolvable (which extinguishes it).

## Phase-progress allocation

- **opener** — target 60+. Standard ceiling work via **A1**;
  slightly more **F8 Star** flavour permissible from message one if
  archetype suits.
- **rapport** — can run 80+ for many turns. **A6 liking**, **A2
  reciprocity**, and **A12 misattribution** all get extended runway.
  Most of this playbook's value lives in rapport.
- **qualifying** — target 70+. The depth phase. Real opinions, real
  fork-questions, mild disagreement. **A11 self-perception** and **A9
  reactance** get extended weight.
- **escalation** — target 60+ before logistics fires. Triggered on a
  *specific reason* (a place, a weekend, a shared interest with a
  calendar), not impatience. **F8 Star → F1 Rake** transition is a
  natural escalation arc here.
- **logistics** — target 80+. Don't sprint; specific venue tied to
  the conversation > generic two-option close.
- **confirm** — short, warm, day-of only.

## Timeline

Days, not hours. Field-tested:

- **Reply windows of 1–12h are fine.** **A13 variable-ratio**
  produces the schedule organically when both parties have lives;
  uniform fast-response actually *underperforms* here because it
  flattens the schedule and her engagement habituates downward.
- **Rapport can run 7–12 messages**, sometimes more.
- **Qualifying:** spend a week here if it's interesting. This is
  where most of the value lives on this playbook.
- **Escalation:** field-tested ask window stretches later — **messages
  11–15+** of a warm thread, sometimes deeper. The trigger is a
  *reason*, not a turn count.
- **Channel-jumps** matter more here than anywhere else. A voice note
  at week two does what twenty texts can't (A18 amplification +
  reduced ambiguity in tone). A phone call before the meet collapses
  multiple ladder rungs at once (A3) and re-anchors otherness (A14).

The decay curve is slower but real: rapport that hasn't moved toward
a plan-anchor in 3+ weeks has metabolised into friend-of-the-app
texture. The signature: warm replies, mutual ease, no escalation
signal from either side. That's pen-pal-zone. Surface it; either
propose a no-pressure meet or transition to `decline-gracefully`.

## Strategy priors

**opener** — favored: `opener-callback-to-photo`,
`opener-lead-with-take`, `opener-flirty-edge` when voice permits.
**F8 Star**-flavored openers (slightly mysterious, ambient) come
online here. Inappropriate: `opener-scarcity-direct-ask` — no
scarcity substrate.

**rapport** — favored: full toolkit, rotate aggressively to avoid
running the same play twice. `rapport-share-then-volley`,
`rapport-mirror-then-pivot`, `rapport-callback` (A11 + A15),
`rapport-playful-tease`, `rapport-social-proof` (A4 + A8 phantom),
`rapport-frame-control`. `rapport-disqualifier` (A9) earns its keep
here once warmth is established — extended runway means the
disqualifier has time to be felt rather than read as judgmental.
**F6 Charmer**, **F2 Ideal Lover**, **F8 Star** all live registers.

**qualifying** — favored: `qualifying-ask-take` is the workhorse.
Multiple fork-questions over time, one per turn. **I12 five-questions**
once or twice across the thread for compressed bursts of mutual
disclosure. Mild disagreement when honest — pure agreement collapses
**A12** and the thread feels nice but generates no pull.

**escalation** — favored: `escalation-time-bridge` is the friend on
a long thread (H7 insinuation, A15 future-pace) — drop offhand
future-pacing 2–3 times before the formal ask, so the meet feels
assumed; `escalation-activity-anchor` is the cleanest formal ask
here (specific activity tied to the conversation = reason that
isn't impatience); `escalation-soft-time-anchor` when nothing
concrete has emerged but warmth has; `escalation-specific-invite`
when she's clearly receptive; `escalation-phone-bridge` if text
plateaus and she's a phone person — channel-jump compresses ladder
rungs. Less appropriate: `escalation-direct-ask` unless rapport is
*very* clear.

**logistics** — favored: `logistics-specific-place` (real venue
tied to the conversation > generic two-option close). Don't sprint;
two-option-close still works as fallback.

## Failure modes (goal-specific)

- **Daily good-morning texts → A14 otherness collapse.** The most
  expensive failure on this playbook. Daily contact closes the
  desire-gap by accumulation — she enjoys it in the moment and goes
  cold within weeks because the imaginative space that generated
  pull has been closed. Symptoms: warm replies, mutual ease, zero
  meet-signal.
- **Premature emotional disclosure → A14 + A18 inversion.** Multi-
  paragraph emotional disclosure on a long thread feels organic
  ("we've been talking for weeks") but collapses the desire-gap and
  inverts hyperpersonal — she enjoys it and then goes cold within
  48h. Same mechanism as quick-meet's pen-pal failure, slower
  trigger.
- **Pretending it's a phase it's not → A1 frame drift.** Talking
  like she's a girlfriend before any proximity. Each turn that
  operates above the actual rung re-anchors the thread one step away
  from where it actually is.
- **No plan-anchor on the horizon → A15 telos failure.** Open loops
  compound only when payoff is plausible. Without "I'm out there in
  three weeks" or equivalent, every loop becomes unresolvable and
  the mechanism extinguishes.
- **Underclocking recovery → A13 + Bowlby misread.** "The playbook
  is slow" gets weaponised as "I shouldn't notice the 96h gap." But
  recovery still applies; ignoring a long silence reads as avoidant-
  cold to her attachment system.
- **Engineered intermittency → A13 inversion + trust collapse.**
  Calculated delays read as game-playing the moment she pattern-
  matches, which collapses trust faster than uniform availability
  collapses pull. **A13** only works when life produces the variance
  organically. Variance from a stopwatch is its own failure mode.
- **Treating extended-window as infinite-window → A15 + match-decay.**
  The meet still has to happen. The thread that's been going great
  for six weeks with no concrete proximity-anchor has decayed; it
  just hasn't gotten the news yet.

## Strategy selection priors

Mechanism categories that **dominate** under extended-window:

- **A15 Zeigarnik / open loops** with extended payoff windows.
- **A13 variable-ratio reinforcement** at organic life-cadence.
- **A14 otherness / desire-distance** preserved through partial
  visibility.
- **A18 hyperpersonal** asymmetry — strong frame + ambiguity reads
  warm.
- **A6 liking via selective expression** at full strength.
- **F8 The Star**, **F5 The Coquette**, **F2 The Ideal Lover** all
  live registers.
- **H5 create-temptation-leave-wanting** as recurring move.
- **H1 mixed signals** — warmth + slight distance over time creates
  the cognitive hook her mind keeps returning to.
- **I10 storytelling structure** with delayed payoffs (especially
  via voice note).
- **Channel-jumps** as the highest-leverage move available — voice
  notes do what text can't.

Mechanism categories to **suppress**:

- **I11 statement-of-intent / direct game** — no scarcity to ride
  on; reads as transactional in a context that explicitly isn't.
- **A7 manufactured scarcity** — pretending to be busier than you
  are pattern-matches and burns trust on a long thread.
- **F7 Charismatic vision-frame** at high intensity — works in
  quick-meet but reads as overselling in extended-window where the
  read is the point.
- **High-density compression moves (I9 time distortion)** at
  consistent cadence — the texture is variation, not density.
