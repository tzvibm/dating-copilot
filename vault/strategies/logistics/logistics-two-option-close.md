## What it is

Two-option binary close on the day. "thursday or friday?" Beats
open-ended scheduling because it gives her a small choice, not a
calendar invitation.

## When it works

- She's said yes in principle and you need to lock the day.
- Both your options are real for you (don't bait-and-switch).
- The two options are adjacent (same week, similar window).

## When it fails

- Three or more options — paralysis, conversation drags.
- She has a third constraint you didn't account for (work trip, etc.)
  — be ready to flex.
- She's a planner type who wants the full proposal at once
  (use logistics-specific-place instead).
