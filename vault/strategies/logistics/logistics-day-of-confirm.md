## What it is

Short day-of message confirming the plan and surfacing one tiny
detail. "still good for 8? grabbing the corner spot if it's free."
Not "are you still coming" (which reads as anxious), just a
confirmation tone.

## When it works

- A specific time and place are already locked.
- Sent ~4-8 hours before, not the morning of (too early) or 30 min
  before (too late).
- Tone is matter-of-fact, not seeking reassurance.

## When it fails

- "Are we still on for tonight?" — frames you as nervous.
- Sent too early ("can't wait for tomorrow" 24h out).
- Stacking with new info ("by the way I made a reservation at X
  instead") — destabilises the plan.
