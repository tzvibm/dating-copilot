## What it is

**Targets:** confirm 0 → 80+ via brief, secure-base check-in.
**Hard prerequisite gate:** logistics ≥ 80 — a specific time and
place are already locked. Without a locked plan there's nothing
to confirm; the message reverts to a logistics ask in disguise
and reads as anxious.

**Mechanisms:** A10 sociometer maintenance + A7 real scarcity +
A18 hyperpersonal compression + H2 calculated absence (the
*absence* of further check-ins).

The move is a short day-of message that surfaces one tiny
forward-pacing detail and signals the plan is on without asking
whether the plan is on. "still good for 8 — grabbing the corner
spot if it's free." The frame: *this is a piece of my day, not
the whole of it*. The implicit scarcity (A7) is real — he has
other things happening between now and 8, the meet is a slot in
a populated calendar, not the gravitational centre of his
afternoon.

The mechanism stack: A10 sociometer is the dominant lever. The
gauge reads behaviour diagnostically — a man asking "are we still
on?" or "you still good for tonight??" signals *his* sociometer
reads low, which is downstream evidence to *her* sociometer that
his actual mate value is low. High-MV men don't seek confirmation;
they signal continuity and trust the plan. The day-of message
must read as continuity-signalling, not as confirmation-seeking.
The forward-pacing detail ("grabbing the corner spot") does the
work — it implies arrival, presupposes the meet, and gives her
something concrete to react to without demanding she affirm
anything. A18 hyperpersonal compression: text strips paralinguistic
cues, so a single short line carries the entire signal — length
and tone are content. The follow-on rule (only one check-in, then
nothing until showing up) operationalises H2 calculated absence
without the manipulation register; it's just real-life behaviour
of someone whose day is already in motion.

The asymmetry is steep: one anxious-coded message can undo the
sociometer reading of three good ones. Day-of is the highest-
sensitivity sociometer window of the entire arc — she's deciding
whether to actually leave the house, and the felt mate-value at
that moment compounds into the in-person frame.

Example illustrations:

- "still good for 8. grabbing the corner spot if it's free."
- "8 at lardo. running ten minutes off the gym so might be a
  hair late, no rush on your end."
- "see you at 7:30. the bartender there does a weird mezcal sour,
  worth a try."

Each one carries the time, a forward-pacing detail, and a
texture-of-day-already-in-motion. None of them ask whether
the plan is on.

## When it works

- **Logistics ≥ 80.** A specific time and place are locked from a
  prior turn. The day-of message confirms continuity, not the
  plan itself.
- **Sent ~2-4 hours before**, not the morning of (over-eager,
  reads as the meet being the day's main event) and not 30
  minutes before (logistically late, signals disorganisation).
- **One forward-pacing detail.** Implies arrival, gives her
  something concrete to picture or react to, fires A15 mild open
  loop (the corner spot, the mezcal sour) into the live meet.
- **Tone reads matter-of-fact.** No urgency markers, no
  emoji-stacking, no question marks at the end of every sentence.
  Compression is signal (A18).

## When it fails

- **Confirmation-seeking phrasing → A10 sociometer leak.** "Are
  we still on for tonight?" / "You still good?" / "Just making
  sure" — every one of these flags his sociometer reading low
  in real-time, and her downstream read on his MV drops
  accordingly. The asymmetry is brutal: this is the single
  highest-leverage anxious-attachment trigger in the entire
  logistics arc.
- **Sent too early → A14 otherness collapse.** "Can't wait for
  tomorrow" 24h out frames the meet as the gravitational centre
  of his week. The implicit signal becomes "this is the whole of
  my day," which collapses scarcity (A7) and otherness (A14)
  simultaneously.
- **Multiple check-ins → anxious-attachment activation
  (Bowlby/Hazan-Shaver) + A10 leak compounding.** Two messages
  in the day-of window unmistakably activate her avoidant
  withdrawal (or anxious mirroring). The system is binary —
  one check-in, then nothing. Two is qualitatively different
  from one.
- **Stacking new info or plan changes → A1 pre-suasive frame
  destabilisation.** "By the way I made a reservation at X
  instead" on day-of breaks the plan she's already committed
  to mentally and forces her to re-evaluate. If a real change
  is needed, frame as light correction, not as new ask. The
  day-of window is for continuity, not negotiation.
- **Over-warm tone → A2 reciprocity inversion.** "So excited!!"
  / "Been looking forward to this all day" — investment
  significantly above hers triggers obligation alarm right
  before the meet. Better to under-invest at the message and
  over-deliver in person.
- **Asking for confirmation in disguise → A17 misuse.** "Just
  let me know if anything changed?" reads as Voss-style
  no-as-safety on the surface but actually invites cancellation
  by foregrounding it. The real no-as-safety move is to make
  no message at all easy to respond to or ignore — not to
  invite the no.
