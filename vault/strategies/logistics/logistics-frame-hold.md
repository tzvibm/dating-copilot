## What it is

When she introduces a pre-meet demand or friction (pickup request, venue pushback, timing adjustment) after logistics are already in motion, hold the existing frame briefly and playfully — redirect back to the confirmed venue and time without negotiating or apologizing.

The move: one line that rejects the ask (playfully or matter-of-fact) + restates the logistics. No explanation, no counter-offer that hands her control. The implicit signal is that this is happening on his terms, and that security is more attractive than compliance.

Examples in voice:
- "I'll be at Clandestino at 8. Come find me."
- "Walk over, I'll buy the first round. Clandestino at 8."
- "That's not how it works 😈 Clandestino at 8."

## Prerequisites

- Escalation ≥ 60 (she's already committed to meeting — this move assumes a yes is in place)
- Logistics ≥ 40 (venue or time already established)
- STATE: she's introduced a specific pre-meet ask or playful friction, not genuine hesitation or a rescheduling

## When it works

- She's testing whether he'll chase or hold; the ask is slightly performative
- The logistical barrier is trivial (she's 5 minutes away; the venue is an easy walk)
- The thread is warm and she's clearly still planning to come
- His register can carry a light tease without it reading as cold or dismissive

## When it fails

- She has a genuine logistical barrier (no transport, far away, late at night in an unsafe area) — then the hold reads as tone-deaf
- The ask is her first signal of real hesitation, not playful friction — then holding reads as dismissive and risks losing her
- Rapport is low (< 50) — not enough warmth substrate for a tease to land; a straight hold without warmth can kill the thread
