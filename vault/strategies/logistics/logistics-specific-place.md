## What it is

**Targets:** logistics 0 → 60+ via concrete commitment-rung. **Hard
prerequisite gate:** escalation ≥ 60 — without forward-leaning
mutual interest, a specific proposal lands as presumptuous rather
than confident. Until escalation has compounded, there's no warrant
for the move.

**Mechanisms:** A3 commitment ladder (specific rung) + A16 SDT
autonomy-as-co-author + I11 statement of intent + A1 pre-suasive
frame.

The move is one specific place + one specific time, fully formed:
"lardo at 8 on thursday." Pulled from `cities/<slug>.md`'s default
first-meet venue or one she mentioned in the thread. The
orchestrator's standing doctrine — *specific invitation > open
invitation* — operationalises here: the difference between "we
should hang sometime" and "the wine bar on Smith at 8 thursday" is
the difference between *no commitment-rung* and *a concrete rung
she can step onto*.

The mechanism stack: a vague ask requires her to do the planning
work (pick the place, pick the day, propose a time), which is
investment-above-her-current-rung (A2 inversion) and ambient
cognitive load (A18). She declines or stalls because the move
*feels like work*. A specific proposal collapses that load — the
only decision she has to make is yes-or-not-quite, and the not-
quite version usually returns a counter-proposal rather than a
deflection. A3 fires because the specific rung *exists* for her to
step onto; vague asks have no rung. A16 autonomy is *not* violated
here despite the apparent lack of choice — paradoxically, having a
concrete plan to push against (or accept) preserves more agency
than being asked to author it from scratch. Frame stability (I11
statement of intent) signals A19-grade agency: the man who has the
plan reads as having his life in motion, not as someone fishing for
permission.

The hand-off rule: she either accepts, counter-proposes a swap on
day or place (high signal — she's now co-authoring), or surfaces a
real constraint. All three are productive. The only failure mode
is "let me think about it," which usually means the prerequisite
gate (escalation ≥ 60) wasn't actually met.

Example illustrations:

- "lardo at 8 thursday. corner table if it's free."
- "the wine bar on smith, 7:30 friday. quiet enough to hear yourself
  think."
- "saturday morning, that bakery you mentioned. before the line
  forms."

Each one carries a place, a time, and one tiny texturing detail
that signals the man has *been there* (not just googled it). The
detail is incidental authority leak (A5).

## When it works

- **Escalation ≥ 60.** The forward-leaning interest is mutual; the
  thread has tension pointing at a meet. Without that substrate,
  this move skips a rung and triggers reactance against being
  pushed (A9 misfire).
- **She's said "you pick" or signals planner-decision-fatigue.**
  Some matches explicitly hand the choice over; the move executes
  on that hand-off with zero friction.
- **You have a real default in `cities/<slug>.md`** — a venue
  you've been to, not a Google Maps screenshot. The detail in the
  proposal is the proof.
- **The place fits both your locations** (you've checked
  `distance_km`). Asking her to cross town to your bar is autonomy-
  violating (A16) regardless of how specific the rest is.

## When it fails

- **Skipped escalation prerequisite → A3 ladder violation.** The
  proposal arrives before forward-leaning mutual interest exists.
  She hasn't been asked for the smaller rungs (substantive reply,
  callback, voice note, future-pacing in her own messages), so the
  ladder breaks at the meet-rung. Brain refuses moves that don't
  match prior consistency. Reads as presumptuous, not confident.
- **Strong-opinion-on-venues match got no co-authorship → A16
  autonomy violation.** Some matches read venue-choice as an
  expression of taste they want to participate in. Single-shot
  proposal in that case removes the move's autonomy-supportive
  ambiguity. For these matches, `logistics-two-option-close` opens
  the same A3 rung while preserving co-authorship.
- **Wrong venue for first meet → A1 pre-suasive frame collapse.**
  Loud, intimate, expensive, or far. The frame she's been forming
  ("this guy reads me well") collapses at the venue mismatch. The
  venue *is* a frame statement.
- **Stale `cities/<slug>.md` entry → I6 DLV.** Place has closed,
  changed character, or is wrong-night-of-the-week. The signal
  inverts from authority-by-leak to "googled it badly."
- **Permission-framed wrapper → A16 autonomy violation.** "Would it
  be okay if we did lardo at 8 thursday?" reverts the entire move
  to interview-frame. The whole point is statement, not request.
