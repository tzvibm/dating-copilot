## What it is

Propose one specific place + one specific time. "lardo at 8 on
thursday." Pulled from `cities/<slug>.md`'s default first-meet venue
or one she mentioned. Removes all coordination overhead by doing
the work yourself.

## When it works

- She's a planner type, or she said "you pick".
- You have a real default in `cities/<slug>.md` to pull from.
- The place fits both your locations (you've checked).

## When it fails

- She has a strong opinion about venues and didn't get to weigh in —
  feels presumptuous.
- The place is wrong for a first meet (loud, private, far).
- You picked a place from a city file that's stale.
