## What it is

**Targets:** logistics 60 → 100 by piggybacking a venue close onto a
social-handle handoff she initiated.

For situations where she asks for your IG/snap/number *after* already
agreeing to the meet. The meet is confirmed; the only open variable
is venue. She's given you a natural hook — her social request — to
simultaneously hand over the handle and flip the venue choice to her
in one tight message.

**Mechanisms:** A3 commitment ladder (she's already on the meet-rung;
venue is just the next micro-rung) + A16 SDT autonomy-as-co-author
(she picks the spot, so she owns the choice) + A5 incidental authority
leak (you've got enough of a life to let her pick; you're not
auditioning venues).

The flip-to-her works especially well when there's a running thread
joke or callback available — it lets the venue ask feel like
continuation rather than logistics pressure. Without a callback,
"you pick" or "you're the local" still works fine; it implies security
and social proof (you don't need to impress her with your venue
knowledge).

State conditions that apply: She asked for a social handle AFTER
already confirming a meet time. Time is set; place is not.

Example illustrations:

- "[handle]. You're the local, pick the spot."
- "[handle] — figure out where from there."
- "[handle]. You know this city better than I do. Pick somewhere good."

Each: handle delivery + flip venue to her in two short beats. Nothing
more.

## Prerequisites

phase_progress required:
- opener ≥ 60
- rapport ≥ 60
- escalation ≥ 80 (she must have explicitly confirmed the meet, not
  just future-paced — the venue flip only lands as confident if the
  meet itself is certain)

State conditions:
- She asked for a social handle AFTER already agreeing to a time/day.
- Venue is the only remaining open logistics variable.
- A callback or tease is available from the thread (optional, but
  preferred — it reframes logistics as continuation of the riff).

## When it works

- **Escalation ≥ 80 / explicit meet confirmation.** She said yes to
  tonight, not "maybe." The venue flip reads as "we're doing this,
  just figure out where" — not "do we even want to hang?"
- **A thread callback is available.** "You're the local" lands better
  if she made a claim about the city or her local knowledge earlier.
  The callback proves you were listening and makes logistics feel
  personal.
- **She's a "phone caller" / low-text type.** Bundling the handle
  with the venue ask keeps it to one message instead of a
  back-and-forth that drains momentum.
- **She proposed the social request unprompted.** That's an
  investment signal — she wants to see more of you before meeting.
  Answering cleanly and immediately (vs. teasing / withholding) reads
  as secure.

## When it fails

- **Escalation < 80 / meet not yet confirmed.** The venue flip reads
  as presumptuous rather than confident. She hasn't actually agreed
  to meet; the "you pick the spot" implies a meet that hasn't been
  established.
- **You name a venue instead of flipping.** Both are valid, but the
  card encodes the flip. If you'd rather name a specific venue, use
  `logistics-specific-place` instead. Don't contort this card into
  a venue proposal — pick the right card.
- **The flip reads as indecision.** If the tone is "I dunno, you
  decide?" rather than "you're the expert, I trust you," it inverts.
  The frame must be playful / secure, not helpless.
- **Long multi-part message.** Handle + venue ask + explanation +
  riff. Kill the explanation. Handle, one tease, venue flip. Done.
- **Withholding the handle as a tease.** "You want my IG? Depends..."
  — this works occasionally in very high-rapport threads, but mostly
  reads as petty when logistics are this close. Give the handle.
