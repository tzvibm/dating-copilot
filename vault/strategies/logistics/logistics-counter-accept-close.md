## What it is

**Targets:** logistics 10 → 60 via one-beat acknowledgment of her
counter-proposal + specific time anchor.

For situations where she redirected the meet to a different day
(real reason: schedule, work, tiredness) and counter-proposed
spontaneously. She's said yes-in-principle; the day is already
decided. The only open parameter is time. The move: one brief
acknowledgment of the redirect (optional — omit if it reads flat),
then name a specific time tonight/tomorrow. Done.

**Mechanisms:** A3 commitment ladder (she stepped onto the day-rung
herself by counter-proposing; naming the time closes the next rung)
+ A7 real scarcity (today-window; not manufactured) + A16 SDT
autonomy (accepting her counter vs. ignoring it preserves her
agency read).

The redirect acknowledgment is optional and brief — one word or one
short clause max. Its function is to prevent the time anchor from
reading like you ignored her counter-proposal. If omitting the ack
doesn't feel cold, omit it.

State conditions that apply: She counter-proposed a specific day
(not "sometime soon" — an actual day). She has a plausible reason
(not an evasion). Her message had warmth signals (emoji, tone,
counter rather than silence).

Example illustrations:

- "Tonight works. 8?"
- "Makes sense. 8 tonight, drinks?"
- "Tonight it is. 8?"
- "Night shift survivor. 8 tonight?"

Each: brief ack (optional) + accepted day + specific time. Nothing
more.

## Prerequisites

phase_progress required:
- opener ≥ 60 (thread is alive)
- rapport ≥ 60 (enough warmth that accepting her redirect reads
  as secure, not resigned)
- escalation ≥ 60 (she's already expressed intent to meet;
  counter-proposal is the clearest form of this)

State conditions:
- She counter-proposed a real day (not a vague "sometime").
- Her redirect had a genuine reason, not a hard no.
- Her reply had warmth signals (emoji, energy match, or a question
  mark inviting your confirmation).

## When it works

- **She proposed a real day.** The move converts her
  counter-proposal into a logistics rung without renegotiating
  the premise.
- **escalation ≥ 60.** She's leaning toward the meet; accepting
  her counter + naming a time is completion, not a new ask.
- **Her reason is real.** Work, schedule, tiredness — plausible
  constraints. The one-beat ack (if used) names the constraint
  without dwelling on it.
- **She had warmth in the message.** Emoji, energy, playfulness —
  signals the redirect is tactical (timing), not avoidance
  (disinterest).
- **One time only.** One specific time closes the loop with
  minimum friction. Offering multiple times reopens the
  scheduling question she already decided by naming a day.

## When it fails

- **She gave a vague redirect** ("sometime this week") — this
  card assumes a specific day was named. A vague redirect needs
  a `logistics-two-option-close` instead to narrow the day.
- **escalation < 60.** No forward-leaning mutual interest; the
  time anchor reads as pressing rather than completing.
- **Her redirect was a polite no.** "I'm actually pretty busy
  this week" / "maybe next time" — genuine deferral, not a
  counter-proposal. Hard to distinguish from a real reason;
  err toward accepting it once and reading her response.
- **The ack becomes a riff.** One beat on her reason — not a
  tangent. "Night shift? That sounds exhausting, what do you
  do?" derails into rapport and burns the window.
- **Multiple time options.** "8 or 9?" after she's already
  proposed the day feels like re-opening the negotiation she
  tried to close. Pick one.
