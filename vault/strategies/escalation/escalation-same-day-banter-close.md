## What it is

**Targets:** escalation 0 → 60 / logistics 0 → 30 in one message.

For casual-sex / today-only situations where she replied warmly but
sidestepped the meet ask with humor or banter rather than a hard yes
or no. The move: one brief acknowledgment of her deflection (one word
or short clause — not a riff), then a specific TONIGHT ask with no
further softening.

**Mechanisms:** A7 scarcity (tonight or nothing — the constraint is
real) + I11 statement of intent (names the thing directly without
asking permission) + A11 self-perception (she signaled "of course"
or similar positive lean; treating that as partial-yes and naming
tonight activates A3 commitment consistency) + A1 frame (one beat
of warmth keeps the close from reading cold; the brevity signals
he is not pressing).

The banter-match is a deliberate single beat — one word or one short
clause. Then the close follows immediately, no tangent, no rapport
detour. Extended riffs burn the constraint window.

Example illustrations:

- "Neither do I. Drinks tonight?"
- "Even better. Tonight?"
- "Perfect 😈 Tonight?"
- "Same. Free tonight?"

Each: one acknowledgment beat + one today ask. Nothing more.

## Prerequisites

phase_progress required:
- opener ≥ 60 (thread is alive, she's engaging)
- rapport ≥ 30 (minimum warmth so the close doesn't land cold)

State conditions:
- She replied warmly but deflected or sidestepped the meet ask
  (humor, tangent, banter). Her deflection is NOT a hard no.
- goal_type is casual-sex with a same-day or ≤1 day constraint.
- Her reply contained a positive signal (🤭, laughter, "of course",
  playing along) — she is enjoying the thread.
- The user's voice permits short declarative asks.

## When it works

- Thread is warm but she deflected without rejecting — humor and
  premise-plays count as deflections, not rejections.
- goal_type is casual-sex and the constraint is today: real A7
  scarcity backs the directness.
- opener ≥ 60 + rapport ≥ 30: enough warmth that the close reads
  confident rather than cold.
- One beat match + immediate close: the brevity signals security,
  not pressure.

## When it fails

- She explicitly declined the meet ask — this card is for sidesteps,
  not rejections. A hard no is a hard no.
- rapport < 30: no warmth substrate; the one-beat match reads
  robotic or transactional.
- Extended banter riff instead of one beat: burns the constraint
  window, devolves into rapport-building mode.
- The "deflection" actually signals unavailability for today (e.g.
  "I'm actually swamped today" — not just a premise-sidestep).
- goal_type is not today-constrained — use escalation-soft-time-
  anchor or escalation-specific-invite instead.
