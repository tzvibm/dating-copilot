## What it is

Tie the meet to an activity she already mentioned wanting to do or
liking. "you said you've been wanting to try that natural wine bar
in roma — let's do that thursday." Removes the awkwardness of a
generic "let's get drinks" because it's tied to her own thread.

## When it works

- She's already volunteered a place / activity / curiosity.
- The activity is plausible for a first meet (drinks > dinner > hike).
- You can name a specific place or time, not "we should sometime".

## When it fails

- The activity she mentioned is wrong for a first meet (a 4-hour
  hike, a private event you can't get into).
- You misremember what she said and propose something she didn't.
- Activity-anchor turns into open-ended planning instead of an ask.
