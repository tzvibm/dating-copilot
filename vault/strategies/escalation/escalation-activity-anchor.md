## What it is

**Targets:** escalation 40 → 80. Bridges to specific-invite using her
own thread as the anchor. Requires rapport ≥ 60 and at least one
volunteered activity / venue / curiosity from her side.

**Mechanisms:** H7 art of insinuation (the activity is hers; he's
just naming what she opened) + A15 Zeigarnik via the activity as
open loop (the unfinished scene she half-opened by mentioning it) +
A11 self-perception (she said she wanted to do X; agreeing to do X
with him is internally consistent with her own prior statement) +
A16 autonomy support (the plan came from her territory) + A6 liking
via specific-noticing (he registered exactly what she said).

The move ties the meet to a specific activity she already mentioned
wanting to do or liking. "You said you've been meaning to try that
natural wine bar in roma — let's do that thursday." The mechanism is
that the activity is *hers* — she opened the loop by mentioning it,
and the unresolved scene of actually going has been quietly running
in her head ever since. Naming the activity closes that A15 loop in
a way that is *also* the meet.

A11 self-perception does the heavy lifting. She volunteered the
activity (probably casually, days ago); accepting now is internally
consistent with her own prior statement of interest, and consistency
with one's prior statements is one of the brain's strongest pulls.
She isn't being asked to decide whether to go on a date with this
guy — she's being asked whether she still wants the thing she
already said she wanted, with him as the now-natural co-conspirator.
The A8 unity frame leaks through quietly.

H7 insinuation makes this the most autonomy-supportive escalation
move in the catalog: the plan, the activity, the framing all come
from her territory. He is not imposing; he is converting an open
loop she already had into a coordinate that includes him. A6 liking
also fires at low intensity — the noticing is specific and earned
("you said X" beats generic "let's get drinks"), which is exactly
the kind of selective expression that lands.

The activity is the substrate, not the headline. The move is still
an escalation ask; the activity-anchor just makes the ask feel like
a continuation rather than a request.

Example illustrations (her loop, his time, one move):

- "you mentioned the natural wine bar in roma. thursday-ish?"
- "the bookstore-cafe you talked about. saturday morning, let's
  finally see if it lives up to the hype."
- "you owe me the verdict on that mezcal place. friday after work?"
- "the rooftop you said had the view. before the weather turns. when."

Each names what she opened, attaches a specific time, and stops —
no preamble explaining why he remembered, no padding.

## When it works

- She volunteered the activity / venue / curiosity earlier in the
  thread, ideally without prompting. Spontaneous mentions are
  stronger A15 loops than activities elicited via direct question.
- The activity is plausible for a first meet. Drinks > coffee >
  short walkable activity > short cultural thing > meal. A 4-hour
  hike or a private event collapses the autonomy-support — the
  commitment is too large to be casually picked up.
- He can name a specific time alongside the activity. Activity-
  anchor without a time-anchor degenerates into open-ended planning;
  the move requires both rails (her activity, his time) to land.
- The reference is fresh enough to be a live loop. Activities she
  mentioned 3 weeks ago that haven't been touched since may have
  closed on their own; pulling them up reads as forced rather than
  insinuating.

## When it fails

- **Misremembered activity → A6 inversion + A16 violation.** Naming
  something she didn't actually say (or attributing to her something
  someone else said) inverts the entire mechanism — instead of A6
  selective-noticing landing, it signals he is fabricating the
  shared territory. Verify against actual prior messages before
  deploying.
- **Activity wrong for first meet → A3 commitment inflation.**
  Anchoring on a 4-hour hike, an out-of-town day trip, or a private
  event commits her to far more than a first-meet rung warrants.
  A3 ladder math says the rung asked for has to fit prior
  investment; over-large rungs read as presumption.
- **Activity-anchor without time-anchor → A3 ambiguity.** "We
  should do the wine bar sometime" leaves the planning load on her
  and degrades to fishing. The mechanism requires him to convert her
  open loop into a specific coordinate; half-converting wastes the
  loop.
- **Imposed activity → A16 autonomy violation.** Anchoring on a
  venue or activity *he* picked while pretending it came from her
  territory ("you'd love this place I know") is the inverse of the
  intended mechanism. The move only fires when the activity is
  genuinely hers.
- **Stale loop → A15 mechanism failure.** Activities mentioned long
  ago that the thread has moved past don't carry live cognitive
  return. Pulling them back can read as effortful (the man who took
  notes), which inverts the easy-insinuation register the move
  requires.
- **Headlined as the rationale → H7 collapse.** "I noticed you
  mentioned wanting to try X" calls out the noticing. H7 dies on
  exposure; the noticing has to read as offhand for the insinuation
  mechanism to fire.
