## What it is

The ask, with one specific time, one specific place. Yes/no
question, no negotiation. Converts dramatically better than open-
ended ("we should hang sometime") because she only has to decide,
not coordinate.

If you don't have a specific venue, name a *category* and a clear
time — that still beats vague.

Examples:

- "thursday 8pm at café las flores. you in?"
- "drinks at hideout this friday after 7?"
- "saturday morning, the coffee place near parque central. 10am."
- "wednesday after work, drinks somewhere walkable. you pick the
  spot."

## When it works

- Rapport / qualifying is warm — she's reciprocating with substance,
  matching or raising your energy.
- You have at least *one* concrete (time + place, or time + you-
  pick-place). Vague kills the conversion.
- The invitation stands on its own — no preamble, no explanation,
  no apology for asking.
- Time is plausible (within a week, ideally a few days for quick-
  meet-window goals).

## When it fails

- Thread is still flat / cold — the ask shows up before she's
  warmed up, reads as presumptuous.
- Multiple hedges in the same message ("if you're free", "no
  pressure", "totally cool if not") — undermines the ask.
- Invitation is too vague ("wanna hang sometime") — back to fishing.
- Invitation is too elaborate (5pm at this place then dinner at this
  place then drinks) — too much commitment for a first meet.
