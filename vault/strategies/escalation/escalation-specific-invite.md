## What it is

**Targets:** escalation 60 → 100 / logistics 0 → 70. The hard close.
Requires rapport ≥ 60 and ideally one prior bridge or activity-anchor
landed. Without that substrate, the specific invite is the wrong move
— `escalation-time-bridge` is.

**Mechanisms:** A3 commitment ladder (the largest single rung short
of the meet itself) + A16 autonomy support via co-authored choice
architecture + A17 Voss "no as safety" / calibrated easy-out + A1
frame-confidence + Greene's seduction-arc terminal move (clarity *is*
the seduction at the close).

The invitation names one specific time, one specific place, asked as
a yes/no. This is the inversion-point of the whole arc: every prior
phase was indirection, mixed signals, insinuation — and at the close,
clarity becomes the seducer. Greene's principle: at the kill, the
indirect approach inverts. A3 commitment-ladder math says the larger
the rung asked for, the more frame-confidence is required; the
specific invite is the largest text-side rung available, and the only
thing that lets her clear it without friction is a felt sense that
she is choosing freely (A16 autonomy).

Choice architecture is the entire mechanism. "Drinks Thursday — wine
bar on Smith or the noisy one, your call" supports autonomy because
it frames her as co-author of the plan; she clears the rung as
someone making a small decision, which A11 self-perception then
encodes as her wanting to. "Can I take you out?" frames consent as
binary grant/deny — autonomy-violating, A16-killer. Same intent,
opposite mechanism activation.

A17 "no as safety" is the second invisible rail. Letting her say no
without consequence (no follow-up apology, no protest, no re-pitch)
is what makes saying yes feel safe. The easy-out should be implicit
in the framing ("if thursday's a no, no drama") not bolted on as
hedging — bolted-on hedges undermine A1 frame-confidence. The
asymmetry: the move that lets her say no easily is the same move
that makes her likely to say yes.

Example illustrations (one specific time, one place, no preamble):

- "thursday 8pm at café las flores. you in?"
- "drinks at hideout this friday after 7?"
- "saturday morning, the coffee place near parque central. 10am."
- "wednesday after work, drinks somewhere walkable. you pick the
  spot."

Each names a hard time, a real place (or hands her place-choice as
co-authorship), and stops. No softening, no second sentence, no
question after the question.

## When it works

- Rapport ≥ 60 and qualifying ≥ 50, ideally one bridge or activity-
  anchor already absorbed. The A3 ladder rung only clears when prior
  rungs have compounded; a hard close from cold is a skipped-rung
  violation.
- The plan is plausible — within the next ~7 days, real venue, normal
  time. A16 autonomy collapses if the plan is so elaborate (5pm here
  then dinner there then drinks elsewhere) that saying yes commits
  her to a 5-hour evening. First-meet asks should be one venue, ~90
  minutes implied.
- Frame stays confident through the ask. A1 + I7 frame control: the
  invitation arrives as a delivered proposal, not a request for
  permission. No "if you're free", no "totally cool if not" stacked
  in the message.
- The easy-out is structural, not bolted on. A17 "no as safety"
  works because the *frame* permits no — confident asks accept no
  cleanly without protest, and her brain reads that even before she
  decides.
- IOIs (I5) are present: she's been future-pacing, asking back,
  matching energy, picking up bridges.

## When it fails

- **Premature deployment → A3 skipped-rung collapse.** Asked from a
  thread that hasn't compounded rapport, the rung is too large for
  prior consistency to support. Her brain refuses moves that don't
  match prior investment; the answer is no or evasion regardless of
  underlying interest.
- **Stacked hedges → A1 frame collapse.** "Thursday 8pm, if you're
  free, totally fine if not, no pressure" telegraphs that he is
  asking her to bail him out from his own ask. The mechanism the
  hedges *try* to invoke (A17 no-as-safety) only fires when the
  frame is confident; bolted-on hedges signal the opposite.
- **Vague "let's hang sometime" → A3 ambiguity + A16 burden-shift.**
  Puts the planning load entirely on her, with no specific rung
  offered. She has to do the work of picking time, place, and
  framing — which reads as him not wanting it enough to plan, and
  the move dies as politeness.
- **Over-elaborate plan → A16 autonomy violation via commitment
  inflation.** A 4-stop evening laid out in advance commits her to
  more than the rung warrants. First-meet asks are one venue, simple
  time, easy exit available; bigger plans are a later-date rung.
- **Permission-framed ask → A16 violation.** "Can I take you out?"
  frames her as gatekeeper. The mechanism dies on contact — she's
  forced into evaluator-frame, and the asymmetric load on her
  signals he's not bringing equal investment.
- **Re-pitch after a soft no → A10 sociometer leak + A17
  inversion.** Pushing again ("come on it'll be fun") destroys the
  no-as-safety mechanism for the next ask and reveals sociometer
  dependence on her yes. One ask, one answer, accept what comes.
