## What it is

A direct, specific ask. "drinks Thursday at 8?" One line, no
softening. Forces a yes or no, which beats indefinite ambiguity
when the window is short.

## When it works

- `days_remaining < 4` — soft anchors are too vague.
- Conversation has clear mutual warmth and reciprocation.
- She's a planner type (signals: schedules, specific times in her
  prior messages, asks "when").

## When it fails

- Conversation is under 8 messages — premature.
- She's been giving short replies; ask reads as pressure.
- No prior signal of warmth or interest in meeting.
