## What it is

**Targets:** escalation 50 → 100 / logistics 0 → 80 in a single move.
Compresses two phases. Requires rapport ≥ 60 *and* `voice.md` permits
direct register *and* `days_remaining` is short enough that
indirection costs more than directness saves.

**Mechanisms:** I11 statement of intent (Tyler Durden / direct game)
+ A1 pre-suasion via boldness + A7 implied scarcity (efficiency itself
signals he doesn't have time for the indirect arc) + A19 mate-
preference for agency / frame-stability + A3 ladder-jump (skip
multiple rungs on the strength of one move).

The move bypasses indirect rapport-into-bridge-into-soft-anchor with
a single declarative ask. "Drinks Thursday at 8?" One line, no
softening, no preamble, no question after the question. The
mechanism is frame-confidence as substitute for warmth investment —
I11 in textbook form. He is not auditioning, not asking permission,
not building toward; he is naming the thing.

This works when it works because the boldness *is* the pre-suasive
frame (A1) — the message itself signals high MV via agency (A19) and
real scarcity (A7) implied by the efficiency. A man with options and
a clock doesn't run a 14-message rapport arc; the move's economy is
its own DHV (I6). When her sociometer (A10) reads the directness as
backed by real frame, the brain registers it as competence. When it
reads as effortful or compensatory, the same words register as
desperate. The signal margin is narrow.

This is the highest-variance escalation move in the catalog. I11's
own footnote: direct game from a man who can't back the frame
collapses fast. The move requires the user's actual voice to permit
it; on a `voice.md` that codes more reserved, the same words read as
out-of-character and trip authenticity-detectors. Voice-permitting
is a hard prerequisite, not a stylistic preference.

When the channel allows, voice notes amplify the warmth substrate
that text alone can't carry — a voice-note direct-ask carries the
paralinguistic signal that lets her sociometer read confidence
rather than terseness.

Example illustrations (single line, no padding):

- "drinks thursday at 8?"
- "you. me. friday. wine bar. yes or no."
- "let's do this. coffee saturday morning, you pick the place."
- "thursday after work. drinks. send."

Each is one clause, frame-confident, asks the question and stops.

## When it works

- Rapport ≥ 60 with strong IOIs (I5): she's future-pacing in her
  own messages, asking back substantively, matching or raising
  energy, picking up callbacks. The directness lands when it
  arrives at a thread already running hot.
- `days_remaining < 4` or hard scarcity (he's traveling, she's
  traveling). The implied A7 scarcity in the move's economy needs a
  real underlying scarcity; without one, the directness pattern-
  matches as random and breaks A1.
- She's a planner-coded archetype: prior messages cite specific
  times, she asks "when," her schedule shows up in her language.
  Planners read directness as competence; non-planners often read
  it as pressure.
- `voice.md` permits direct register without strain. I11 fails on
  performed boldness; the user's actual voice has to back the
  move. If `voice.md` codes reserved, this is the wrong card.
- Goal_type is quick-meet-window. The whole move is a wager that
  rapport-investment isn't worth the calendar cost; that wager
  only makes sense when the calendar is the binding constraint.

## When it fails

- **Premature deployment → I11 substrate collapse.** Asked from a
  thread that hasn't crossed rapport ≥ 60, the boldness has nothing
  to back it. A1 frame fails because there is no warmth substrate
  for the directness to read as confidence; same words register as
  pressure or as a guy mass-messaging.
- **Voice mismatch → A19 authenticity inversion.** A reserved voice
  shipping a clipped one-liner trips the authenticity-detector. The
  brain reads the register-jump as performance, which is the most
  reliable A19 inversion in the catalog — agency-claimed-without-
  backing reads as low MV.
- **Short replies preceding the ask → A2 inversion.** If her last 3
  messages have been one-liners and reply-lag is growing, the direct
  ask reads as pressure-on-a-cooling-thread. Direct asks compound
  IOIs; on IODs they accelerate the cool.
- **Hedge-stacked direct ask → A1 collapse.** "Drinks thursday at 8
  if you're free, no pressure?" is no longer a direct ask — it's a
  soft anchor in worse clothes. Pick the move; don't dilute it.
- **Re-ask after dodge → A10 sociometer leak.** If the direct ask
  doesn't land, doubling down or rephrasing exposes that the frame
  needed the yes. Drop one turn, return to rapport temperature, and
  if anything, graduate down to `escalation-soft-time-anchor` later.
- **Used outside quick-meet-window → A7 manufactured scarcity.**
  Direct game on an extended-window goal reads as urgency from
  nowhere. The implied scarcity has to map to real scarcity; faking
  it pattern-matches as theater (A13 inversion).
