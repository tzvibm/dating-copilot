## What it is

Pivot from text to a quick phone or voice call before the in-person
meet. Best for matches who explicitly prefer phone (their bio says
"phone caller", they hate text marathons), or when the thread is warm
but text feels limiting. A 5-minute call massively de-risks the meet
for both sides.

The ask is casual and short — it's not a "let's hop on a 30-minute
call" pitch, it's "give me your number, much faster than this."

Examples:

- "we should just call. easier than this. drop your number?"
- "this would be faster as a 5-minute call. send me your number."
- "ok we're texting in circles. what's your number, I'll call you
  tonight."

## When it works

- Her bio explicitly notes phone-caller / not-a-text-person.
- Thread has been substantive but is plateauing — she's warm but
  text isn't moving things forward.
- Voice notes don't push hard against phone calls.
- The ask is direct and casual — one sentence, no preamble.

## When it fails

- She hasn't engaged warmly yet — premature, reads pushy.
- Pitched as a "deeper conversation" — too heavy. Phone bridge
  works because it feels lighter than texting, not heavier.
- Stacking with the meet ask in the same message — pick one move.
- Multiple pushes after she dodges — if she sidesteps the phone
  ask, drop it and go straight to the meet ask instead.
