## What it is

**Targets:** escalation 50 → 90 via channel-jump. Substitutes for
several text-rungs of A3 commitment-ladder progress. Requires rapport
≥ 60 and ideally an explicit phone-coded signal in her bio or
behavior.

**Mechanisms:** Channel-jump per `_psychology.md` E section ("voice
note > text. phone call > voice note. video > phone. each upgrade is
a commitment-ladder rung worth several text-rungs") + A18 hyperpersonal
exit into a richer signal channel + A11 commitment ladder rung
compressed into one move + A14 otherness re-installation (voice
restores the paralinguistic cues text strips) + A12 misattribution
substrate (live voice generates arousal text can't).

The move pivots from text to a quick phone or voice call before the
in-person meet. The mechanism is the exit from text's hyperpersonal
distortion (A18) — once the thread is warm, text's compression and
ambiguity start working *against* the move. Voice restores tone,
cadence, hesitation, laughter — the paralinguistic vocabulary that
makes warmth metabolize into chemistry. A 5-minute call generates
more A12 misattribution-substrate than three days of texts.

The channel-jump is itself a large A3 ladder rung. Per the
psychology reference, each channel upgrade compresses several text-
rungs of commitment progress; her saying yes to a call is a stronger
self-perception (A11) lock-in than five more turns of substantive
text would be, because the action is qualitatively bigger. By the
time she's been on a call with him, the meet's perceived friction
has dropped sharply — the unknown of "what is this guy actually
like" is partially resolved, and the in-person ask faces less
ambiguity-aversion.

The ask has to read as casual and short — *not* "let's hop on a
30-minute call" (that pitch reads as a commitment in itself), but
"give me your number, much faster than this." The framing positions
the call as *less* effort than the texting it replaces, which it
genuinely is for many threads. That framing supports A16 autonomy
(the call is shorter, easier, less work) where a heavy framing
would invert it.

This is the right move when text is plateauing — the warmth is real
but text isn't compounding it further, and the meet ask hasn't
landed cleanly. The phone bridge is a smaller rung than the meet
itself but a much larger rung than another text-turn, and it
generates the substrate that makes the next meet ask easier.

Example illustrations (casual, short, no preamble):

- "we should just call. easier than this. drop your number?"
- "this would be faster as a 5-minute call. send me your number."
- "ok we're texting in circles. what's your number, i'll call you
  tonight."
- "voice note's better than this. or call me, your pick."

Each frames the call as the lighter option, asks for the number
directly, and stops.

## When it works

- Her bio or prior messages explicitly code phone-friendly: "phone
  caller", "i hate texting", voice notes from her side, references
  to calls. The A16 autonomy mechanism fires hardest when the
  channel-jump matches her stated preference.
- Thread has been substantive but is plateauing — warm enough that
  the bridge doesn't read as premature, stalled enough that the
  channel-jump genuinely solves the problem A18 is creating.
- Rapport ≥ 60 with strong IOIs (I5). Channel-jumps from cold
  threads invert; the move requires real warmth substrate for the
  larger A3 rung to clear.
- The user can carry voice register without strain. Voice restores
  paralinguistic signal — which means his actual voice has to back
  the text frame he's been running. If the gap is too wide, the
  call becomes a downgrade rather than an upgrade.
- The ask is one sentence. Length on this ask kills it — heavy
  framing inverts the "lighter than texting" mechanism the move
  depends on.

## When it fails

- **Pitched as a deeper conversation → A16 inversion via commitment
  inflation.** "Let's get on a call to really get to know each
  other" frames the call as more effort than texting, which is
  the opposite of the mechanism. The move dies because the framing
  contradicts the actual A3 efficiency claim.
- **Premature deployment → A3 skipped-rung collapse.** Asked from a
  thread that hasn't compounded warmth, the channel-jump is too
  large a rung for prior consistency to support. Same as specific-
  invite from cold — her brain refuses moves that don't match
  prior investment.
- **Stacked with the meet ask → A2 cognitive load + A1 frame
  ambiguity.** Asking for the number *and* the meet in one message
  doubles the rung-jump and confuses the mechanism. Pick one — if
  you're asking for the call, ask for the call; if you're asking
  for the meet, ask for the meet.
- **Re-pushed after dodge → A10 sociometer leak.** If she sidesteps
  the phone ask, doubling down ("seriously though, let's call")
  exposes that he wanted the rung confirmed. Drop the call ask
  cleanly and graduate to the meet ask if rapport supports it; the
  failed call ask doesn't pre-disqualify the meet.
- **Voice mismatch → A18 hyperpersonal inversion.** If the user's
  text-voice is dramatically calibrated (witty, edgy, performed)
  and his actual voice can't match, the call exposes the gap and
  the warmth metabolizes the wrong direction. A18 cuts both ways
  on channel-jump — when the actual signal is stronger than text
  promised, it compounds; when it's weaker, it collapses.
- **Heavy length on the ask itself → A2 reciprocity inversion.** A
  five-sentence pitch for the phone call signals the call is a big
  deal, which contradicts the "easier than this" mechanism. One
  sentence, no padding.
