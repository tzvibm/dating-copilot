## What it is

**Targets:** escalation 30 → 60 via assumed-future framing. Requires
rapport ≥ 60 — without warm reciprocation substrate, "we'll" reads
presumptuous and inverts.

**Mechanisms:** H7 art of insinuation + A15 Zeigarnik / open loops +
A11 self-perception (her non-objection = tacit consent) + A1 pre-
suasion of the meet-frame + A8 unity / co-conspirator pronoun.

The move drops offhand references to a future hang *before* any
explicit invitation, so the meet feels assumed rather than
negotiated. By the time the actual ask arrives, the question her
brain is processing is "when," not "if." This is H7 in textbook form
— suggest without stating, let the reader do the work of inferring,
because an inference she made is hers and self-perception (A11) makes
it stick.

The bridge installs an open loop (A15) at the level of the
relationship's future tense. "We'll have to argue about this over
drinks" creates an unresolved scene her mind keeps half-running in
the background; her attention returns to it for the same reason
unfinished tasks linger. Crucially, the pronoun does work: the casual
"we" (A8 unity, deployed lightly enough to be deniable) prefigures a
relational identity before one exists, and the pre-suasive frame
(A1) of *us already having a meet on the horizon* anchors how she
reads everything that follows.

The orchestrator's time-bridging / future-pacing doctrine: roll
forward casually, see if she rolls with you. Her pickup of the "we"
or her continuation of the bridged scene is a strong IOI (I5) and
the cue to graduate to a specific invite within 1-2 turns. Her
silence on the bridge isn't a no — it's signal that the rapport
substrate isn't quite warm enough yet.

Example illustrations (offhand, buried, no question attached):

- "you're going to have to show me the actually-good cafe in this
  city. the lo-fi places are killing me."
- "we'll have to settle the saturday-morning vs friday-night debate
  in person."
- "this is the kind of thing we'll argue about over drinks."
- "putting you on the calendar for the bookstore tour i'm clearly
  not qualified to lead."

Each is a sentence, buried mid-message, never headlined. The "we"
and the future tense do all the work; the topic is whatever loop
she already opened.

## When it works

- Rapport ≥ 60. She's reciprocating substantively, matching or
  raising energy, ideally already volunteering callbacks. The H7
  insinuation mechanism *requires* warmth substrate — without it the
  hyperpersonal effect (A18) reads the "we" cold and inverts to
  presumption.
- The bridge is buried, not headlined. A15 open loops work when the
  hook is felt rather than announced. A standalone "btw we should
  hang" sentence pointed at directly is asking, not bridging.
- The reference is *specific* — anchored on a venue, an activity, or
  a debate she opened. Specificity is what gives the imagined scene
  enough resolution for A15 to run; vague "we should hang" generates
  no scene to be incomplete.
- Voice register permits "we" lightly. A8 unity needs plausible
  deniability — the moment it reads heavy ("babe", "love"), A14
  otherness collapses and the move dies.

## When it fails

- **Premature deployment → A8 unity inversion.** The "we" deployed
  before warmth exists is felt as imposition; her brain registers
  the pronoun as a claim she hasn't licensed. Wait for one warm
  reciprocation minimum before bridging.
- **Bridge stacked with a direct ask → A1 frame contradiction.**
  "We'll have to settle this over drinks — thursday 8pm?" combines
  insinuation and statement. The H7 mechanism dies the moment the
  invitation is explicit; pick one or the other, never both in the
  same message.
- **Bridge imposes his frame on her territory → A16 autonomy
  violation.** Bridging to a wine bar when she said she's a coffee
  person, or to a club when she said she hates loud places, reads as
  not-listening. A15 open loops need *her* loops to attach to;
  hijacking the activity inverts to tone-deaf.
- **Headlined bridge → A15 mechanism failure.** Pointing at the
  bridge ("not to get ahead of myself but…") collapses the
  insinuation. The bridge has to read as offhand for her imagination
  to do the work; called-out bridges become asks, badly framed.
- **Ignored bridge re-bridged immediately → A10 sociometer leak.**
  If she doesn't pick up the "we," doubling down ("seriously though
  we should") signals he needs the scene confirmed. Drop it for one
  turn, raise rapport temperature, then bridge again differently or
  graduate to a specific invite once temperature recovers.
