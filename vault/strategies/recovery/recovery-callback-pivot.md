## What it is

Re-open by referencing something specific she said earlier and
pivoting to a small new angle. Different from `rapport-callback`
because here the conversation is broken, not warm. The callback
proves you remember; the pivot gives her a new way in that doesn't
require her to re-engage with the old thread.

## When it works

- The earlier detail was specific and felt important to her.
- The pivot creates a fresh question, not a re-litigation of where
  things went quiet.
- Tone is light — you're not making a case for why she should reply.

## When it fails

- The callback feels surveillance-y after a long gap.
- You overplay it ("I've been thinking about what you said about…").
- Pivot is the same kind of question that died last time.
