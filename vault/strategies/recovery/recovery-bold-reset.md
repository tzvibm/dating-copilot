## What it is

Thread went cold. The recovery is **new energy from your world, with
zero acknowledgment of the silence**. No "hey sorry been busy", no
"hope you're doing well", no "wasn't sure if you saw my last
message". Apologizing for the gap or fishing for a response just
re-summons the awkwardness.

Just walk back in like nothing happened. The frame is "I'm reaching
out because I have something good", not "I'm reaching out because I
need a response".

Examples:

- "back in town this weekend. thursday's looking like a drinks night."
- "just got told the new wine bar by parque central is the move. test
  run friday?"
- "settled the hike vs brunch debate. it's brunch. saturday?"
- "decided I'm allergic to lo-fi cafes. you owe me a recommendation."

## When it works

- 48-96h+ silent thread after at least one substantive exchange.
- New energy is real — you actually have something fresh to bring,
  not a manufactured pretext.
- Stays short. One sentence + one ask, max.
- Comes with an actual move (specific time, specific place, specific
  thing) — not just "hey".

## When it fails

- Acknowledging the silence — "hey sorry been busy", "hope you're
  doing well", "didn't want to bother you". Resurrects the gap.
- Coming back with a question — "how's your week" — fishing,
  reads weak.
- Coming back with the same energy that died last time. If polite
  questions stalled the thread, polite questions will stall it
  again.
- Multiple recoveries in a row. After one bold reset that doesn't
  land, mark the thread dead and move on.
