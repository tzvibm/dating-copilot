## What it is

**Targets:** re-engagement (off-axis). Recovery is not on the
linear phase-progress chain — it's a meta-state applied when active
phases have decayed. **Trigger condition:** 48-96h+ silent thread
after at least one substantive exchange, with the prior thread warm
enough that abandonment isn't yet the right read. The move targets
re-establishing presence, not advancing a numeric phase percent.

**Mechanisms:** H2 calculated absence (now closing) + A1 pre-suasive
reset + A10 sociometer maintenance + A14 otherness via own-energy +
I7 frame control.

The move is **new energy from his world, with zero acknowledgment
of the silence**. No "hey sorry been busy", no "hope you're doing
well", no "wasn't sure if you saw my last message". The
orchestrator's standing doctrine — *recovery = bold reset, not
apologetic double-text* — operationalised through the mechanism
stack below.

The mechanism stack: the silence created an absence (H2 calculated
absence, whether intentional or organic — the mechanism doesn't
care which, only how it's closed). Two ways to close it. The
apologetic close ("hey sorry been busy") references the silence,
which (a) flags A10 sociometer leak in real-time — high-MV men
don't account for their absences to people they're casually dating
— and (b) foregrounds the gap and forces *her* to evaluate it,
putting her in evaluator-frame and triggering the A16 autonomy
violation. The bold close treats the silence as if it doesn't
exist; A1 pre-suasive frame resets fresh — the new message *is*
the new frame, and her brain anchors on what's in front of her,
not on what wasn't there yesterday. A14 otherness fires because
the energy is from *his* world (a plan, a take, a small piece of
his day moving forward), which preserves the desire-gap the
silence had stretched. I7 frame control: the apologetic close
enters the frame *the silence created* (gap-as-problem); the bold
close ignores that frame and proceeds in his own (life-in-motion).

The single most reliable failure pattern in recovery is
self-sabotage at the threshold — the man who held a strong frame
through 30 messages then collapses it in the recovery turn by
flinching at the silence. The silence is only a problem if the
recovery message *makes* it a problem. Otherwise it's a gap she
filled with imagination (A18 hyperpersonal — direction-dependent
on prior frame). If the prior frame was strong, her imagination
fills the gap warmly; the bold reset confirms what her imagination
was already half-doing.

The recovery message must come with an actual move — specific
place, specific time, specific thing. Not just "hey." The move is
the proof of life-in-motion that activates A14 and A19 (agency
signal). A bold reset that's just a re-greeting fails because it
asks her to do all the re-engagement work.

Example illustrations:

- "back in town this weekend. thursday's looking like a drinks
  night."
- "just got told the new wine bar by parque central is the move.
  test run friday?"
- "settled the hike vs brunch debate. it's brunch. saturday?"
- "decided I'm allergic to lo-fi cafes. you owe me a recommendation."

Each one carries new texture, an implicit life that's been moving,
and exactly one open loop or move for her to pull on — without
any reference to the gap.

## When it works

- **48-96h+ of silence after at least one substantive exchange.**
  Below 48h is too soon — reads as chasing, no real absence
  accumulated. Past ~7-10 days the thread has metabolised into
  cold-zone and `recovery-clean-reopen` (lighter touch) is the
  better tool. The window matters because the absence has to be
  *real* enough that returning means something but not so long
  that she's forgotten the prior frame.
- **New energy is real.** The man actually has a fresh plan, take,
  or specific thing — not a manufactured pretext. Pretexts pattern-
  match (A13 failure mode) and read as engineered, which collapses
  the entire frame.
- **Stays short.** One sentence + one ask, max. A18 compression
  applies harder in recovery because length without warmth substrate
  reads as overcompensation for the silence.
- **Comes with an actual move.** Specific time, specific place,
  specific thing. The move is the proof that A14 otherness is real
  (he's been doing things) and A19 agency is intact.
- **Prior frame was strong.** Bold reset succeeds in proportion to
  prior frame strength — if the opener and rapport had compounded,
  her imagination filled the silence warmly (A18) and the reset
  lands on a primed substrate. Bold reset on a thread that was
  already weak just confirms the weakness.

## When it fails

- **Acknowledging the silence → A10 sociometer leak + A1 frame
  collapse.** "Hey sorry been busy", "hope you're doing well",
  "didn't want to bother you", "I know it's been a while" — each
  one flags his sociometer reading low and forces her into
  evaluator-frame on the gap. The frame the silence created
  (gap-as-problem) gets confirmed instead of ignored. This is the
  single highest-leverage failure mode in recovery.
- **Coming back with a question → A16 autonomy violation + I6
  DLV.** "How's your week?" "How have you been?" — fishing-frame.
  No take, no move, no own-energy. The message asks her to fill
  the void with content, which is precisely the dynamic that let
  the silence happen in the first place.
- **Same energy that died last time → I5 IOD reinforcement.** If
  polite questions stalled the thread, polite questions will stall
  it again. The recovery has to *change something* — heat, register,
  specificity, or move-type. Same input gets same output.
- **Multiple recoveries in a row → anxious-attachment activation
  (Bowlby/Hazan-Shaver) + I6 DLV compounding.** After one bold
  reset that doesn't land, mark the thread dead. A second recovery
  attempt within the same dead window inverts every mechanism
  this card relies on — it converts H2 calculated absence into
  obvious pursuit pattern, A14 otherness into desperation, and
  A10 sociometer into a flashing low-MV signal.
- **Bold reset that names the boldness → A1 self-undermining.**
  "Anyway, ignoring the gap, here's the thing…" — meta-commentary
  on the move collapses it. The frame is held by *acting* in the
  frame, not by narrating the frame.
- **Long recovery message → A2 reciprocity inversion.**
  Multi-paragraph re-opener after silence is the worst possible
  combination — the gap heightens her sensitivity to investment-
  asymmetry, and a heavy message at zero engagement triggers the
  obligation alarm at maximum amplitude. She declines to match
  the energy and the thread closes.
