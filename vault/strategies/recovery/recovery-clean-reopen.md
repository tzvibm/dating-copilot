## What it is

A clean re-open after silence: short, fresh thread, zero reference
to the gap. "saw a place tonight that reminded me of [thing she
said]" — gives her something to react to without forcing her to
account for the silence.

## When it works

- 48-96h of silence after a substantive turn (not too soon, not
  past-saving).
- You have a real, specific hook (something you saw / did /
  noticed that connects to her).
- The hook is one sentence — no preamble, no apology.

## When it fails

- "Hey, sorry I disappeared" framing — anchors the silence as a
  problem, makes you the one who needs to explain.
- Hook is generic ("hope your week is going well") — gives her
  nothing to react to.
- Re-open within 24h — you're chasing.
