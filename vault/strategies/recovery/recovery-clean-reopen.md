## What it is

**Targets:** re-engagement (off-axis). Recovery is not on the
linear phase-progress chain. **Trigger condition:** 48-96h of
silence after a substantive turn — long enough that absence is
real, short enough the thread isn't fully cold; or longer gaps
on `reconnect-later` goal-types where this card is the
canonical reopener.

**Mechanisms:** A1 pre-suasive reset (cleanest version) + A14
otherness via present-tense observation + A10 sociometer-positive
posture + A2 reciprocity gradient (small, not heavy).

A lighter version of `recovery-bold-reset`. The move is short,
fresh, and zero reference to the gap. "saw a place tonight that
reminded me of [thing she said]" — gives her something to react
to without forcing her to account for the silence. Where bold
reset comes with an explicit move (specific time, specific
ask), clean reopen comes with an observation that opens the
door without pushing through it.

The mechanism stack: A1 pre-suasive reset operates the same way
as in bold reset — the new message *is* the new frame, and her
brain anchors on what's present, not on what was missing. A14
otherness fires because the observation is from *his* present-
tense day (he was somewhere, saw something, noticed a thing); his
life is moving and she's getting a small window into a moment of
it. A10 sociometer-positive: no validation requested, no "hey
stranger" or "long time no talk", no reading-of-the-gap. A2
reciprocity gradient is the calibration — clean reopen runs *under*
bold reset's investment level, which is appropriate when the
relationship's built reciprocity has decayed; investment slightly
under hers (and her current state is silence, so very low) is
where the gradient lives. This is why clean reopen is the right
tool when a move-with-ask would be too heavy.

The doctrine: **no "hey stranger." no "long time no talk." no
"sorry I disappeared."** All of these are A10 sociometer leaks —
they put her in evaluator-frame on the gap and reset her gauge
read on him low. They feel like polite acknowledgment but they're
the most reliable way to make the silence permanent. The
present-tense anchor is the entire point: it's not a re-greeting,
it's a man saying something because it occurred to him, in the
flow of his day, with her in mind only as the recipient who
might enjoy it.

This card is the canonical first move on `reconnect-later`
goal-types — the orchestrator's playbook treats clean reopen as
the default "she's leaving / I'm leaving" reactivation. It also
covers the lighter half of recovery on warmer goal-types when a
specific ask would be too forward for the temperature she's at.

Example illustrations:

- "just walked past that bar you mentioned. can confirm it's still
  hostile to humans."
- "found the bookstore. the cat is real."
- "the hike vs brunch question got settled by weather. brunch
  won."
- "tried the place. mezcal flight is an act of war."

Each one is one sentence, present-tense, references something she
said or implied, and is fully complete on its own — no hook
demanding a reply, but plenty of substrate for one if she wants
to give it.

## When it works

- **48-96h of silence** after a substantive turn. Below 48h reads
  as chasing; the absence hasn't accumulated enough for return to
  carry weight. Above ~10 days for active goal-types, the thread
  has metabolised; clean reopen still works for `reconnect-later`
  but not for active pursuit goals.
- **Real, specific present-tense hook.** Something he saw, did,
  or noticed *now* that genuinely connects to something she said
  or implied. The connective tissue is the proof — without it,
  it's just a "hey." With it, it's an A14 window into his moving
  life.
- **Hook is one sentence.** A18 compression applies hardest in
  recovery — length amplifies the gap rather than bridging it.
  No preamble, no apology, no setup.
- **`reconnect-later` goal-type especially.** The canonical
  reopener for this archetype. The fresh-first-impression posture
  is exactly what the goal-type calls for — A1 reset is the
  whole job.
- **No demand for reply embedded.** The hook can stand alone if
  she doesn't reply; her brain reads it as not-fishing, which is
  what makes it reply-able. Hooks framed as questions or asks
  flip the gradient back to needy.

## When it fails

- **"Hey, sorry I disappeared" framing → A10 sociometer leak +
  A1 frame collapse.** Anchors the silence as a problem, makes
  him the one who needs to explain. "Hey stranger" / "long time
  no talk" / "remember me?" — all variants of the same
  sociometer-collapse. The gauge reads low in real-time and her
  read on his MV drops accordingly.
- **Generic hook → A14 otherness collapse + A2 reciprocity
  inversion.** "Hope your week is going well" / "how's life" —
  gives her nothing concrete to react to and asks her to do the
  re-engagement work. The mechanism only fires when the hook is
  *specific* and *present-tense*. Generic warmth is recovery
  poison.
- **Re-open within 24h → I5 IOD inversion + chasing-frame.**
  Reads as not-letting-the-thread-breathe, which is the inverse
  of the H2 calculated-absence dynamic the move is built on. The
  silence has to be real for the return to carry weight.
- **Stacked questions → A18 cognitive load + A2 inversion.**
  Multi-question reopen reads as interrogation under the cover
  of catch-up. Recovery is *especially* sensitive to question-
  stacking because the gap heightens her sensitivity to anything
  that feels like investment-mismatch.
- **Hook that retroactively re-litigates the gap → A1 frame
  self-undermining.** "I was going to message earlier but…" —
  any version of explaining-himself-into-the-message converts
  clean reopen into apologetic reopen, which is the failure mode
  this card is specifically built to avoid.
- **Wrong tool for the gap-state → strategy mismatch.** If the
  thread genuinely needs an ask (escalation was high before
  silence, she's a planner-type, the goal-type is
  quick-meet-window with a closing window),
  `recovery-bold-reset` is the right tool — clean reopen
  under-asks and the door swings open without anyone walking
  through. If the prior thread had a specific volunteered detail
  worth retrieving, `recovery-callback-pivot` retrieves more
  Zeigarnik tension than the present-tense observation does.
