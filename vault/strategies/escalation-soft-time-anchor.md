## What it is

Suggest a meet by anchoring to a window without demanding a specific
commitment. "I'm around Thursday-ish if you want to grab a drink"
rather than "Thursday at 8?".

The softness is the point: it lets her counter-propose without feeling
like she's rejecting a hard ask.

## When it works

- Phase is rapport leaning into escalation.
- She has reciprocated at least one question with warmth.
- `days_remaining > 4` (so a soft anchor still leaves room).
- Conversation has at least 8 messages of substance.

## When it fails

- She's a planner type — soft asks read as flaky.
- `days_remaining < 3` — too soft, needs a real ask.
- Conversation is under 8 messages — premature.
- She's already proposed a specific time; mirror her instead.
