## What it is

Open with a specific reference to a photo that has a non-obvious
detail — not the obvious thing (her dog, the beach), the second-most-obvious
thing (the book on the side table, the city skyline behind her).

## When it works

- Photos have at least one detail to riff on.
- The detail is something she actually picked, not incidental.
- The riff is a take, not a question.

## When it fails

- Photos are generic / model-shot / nothing to grab.
- The "detail" is something embarrassing or sensitive.
- The opener turns into an interrogation ("what book is that").
