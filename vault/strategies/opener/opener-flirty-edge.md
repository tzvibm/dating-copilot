## What it is

**Targets:** opener 0 → 60+ via temperature installation; raises the
pre-suasive ceiling on warmth/sexual-undercurrent register from turn
one.

**Mechanisms:** A12 misattribution priming + H3 stir-up-the-
transgressive + A1 pre-suasive frame (high ceiling) + I1 cocky-funny
+ F1 Rake register flavour.

The move opens with a single declarative line that has *temperature*
— playful confidence, a hint of challenge, a mock-judgment, an
implication of something she shouldn't quite want. Not a pickup
line, not a question, not a callback. A statement that establishes
the room is already warm.

Mechanically this is the strongest pre-suasive ceiling-raise (A1)
available at the opener. Hyperpersonal compounding (A18) means the
ceiling installed in message one becomes the ceiling for the whole
thread — flirty-edge sets a high one and her imagination fills
ambiguous downstream messages in that register. The line carries
mild transgression (H3) — implication of attraction, mock-suspicion,
playful presumption — which generates the small undifferentiated
arousal that misattribution (A12) labels as *attraction to him*
when the proximate cause is him. The cocky-funny scaffold (I1)
defuses the arrogance by making the wink visible, which is what
prevents the move from collapsing into either sleaze or performance.
The register sits squarely in F1 Rake territory: hungry, irreverent,
not safe and not sorry, which reads as appetite for life — and that
reads as scarcity (A7) without claim.

The temperature has to feel earned by the user's actual voice, not
constructed. F1 Rake collapses on canned material — a line that
took ten edits to land reads as performance (I6 DLV) and the
mechanism inverts.

Example illustrations:

- "you look like trouble. confirm or deny."
- "ok this is the part where I'm supposed to be charming."
- "trying to figure out if you're the planner or the chaos. lean
  one way."
- "fair warning. I have opinions on weekend plans."
- "betting you're more fun in person than on hinge. bold of me."

Each is one short line, declarative, with one implied open loop
(A15) for her to volley against. None reference her bio explicitly.
None ask permission to be flirty.

## When it works

- The user's voice notes / `voice.md` permit flirty-edgy register.
  F1 Rake on top of a reserved voice substrate reads as costume.
- Her photos and prompts show some pleasure-coded register herself
  — playful captions, evident humour, a willingness to flirt in the
  bio. The pre-suasive ceiling (A1) only sticks if she can plausibly
  reach it; flirty-edge on a buttoned-up voice creates a ceiling she
  declines to meet.
- One line, max two short sentences. Length kills the temperature
  — the mechanism is in word choice, not word count.
- Goal is quick-meet-window or flexible-short-window where compressed
  arc benefits from early temperature.
- No stronger specific-noticing hook exists. If a photo gives a real
  detail, callback (A2) is a more reliable opener-progress lever.

## When it fails

- **Line targets her body or innate trait → A9 reactance miscalibrated
  + A14 otherness collapse.** The brain doesn't misattribute pain as
  attraction; it just registers the incoming hit as a reason to leave.
  Flirt about implication, never anatomy.
- **Reads as snark, not warmth → A12 fails to fire.** Tension without
  embedded warmth registers as hostility. The move requires the wink
  to be visible — if the line stings without the playful undercut,
  misattribution can't label the arousal as attraction.
- **Generic edge ("you seem cool 😈") → A10 sociometer leak + I6 DLV.**
  Performed edge that doesn't have specific texture reads as low-
  discrimination signal. Same downward sociometer move as generic
  flattery, just in a different costume.
- **Voice-substrate mismatch → A1 sustainability failure.** A flirty-
  edge opener from a user whose follow-up voice is reserved creates
  spike-and-fade — the opener over-promises the ceiling and the
  thread collapses inward as the actual register shows up.
- **Length creep → A18 compression failure.** The medium rewards
  density; a flirty-edge line that needs three sentences to set up
  has already lost. The energy lives in the cut.
- **Deployed on a cold/distance-coded match → ceiling she won't
  reach.** If her bio reads professional or guarded, the high pre-
  suasive ceiling (A1) goes unmet and she drops the thread because
  the register doesn't fit her.
