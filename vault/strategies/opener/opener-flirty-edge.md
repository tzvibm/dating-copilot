## What it is

Open flirty and slightly edgy — confident, playful, with a hint of
challenge or tease. Not a pickup line. Not a question. A statement
that has temperature. Sets the room from message one.

The energy: you're already amused, already interested, but not
overeager. She has to meet your level.

Examples (short — that's the point):

- "you look like trouble. confirm or deny."
- "ok this is the part where I'm supposed to be charming."
- "trying to figure out if you're the planner or the chaos in your
  group. lean one way."
- "fair warning, I have opinions on weekend plans."
- "betting you're more fun in person than on hinge. bold of me."

## When it works

- The user's voice notes allow flirty / edgy register (check them).
- One sentence, max two. Em-dash and compound-clause free.
- Has *temperature* — playful confidence, not sleaze, not interview.
- Stands alone without referencing her bio explicitly.

## When it fails

- The line is about her body / appearance — burns it.
- Reads as snark / mean — kills warmth.
- Tries to be edgy but ends up generic ("you seem cool 😈").
- Longer than 2 short sentences. Length kills the energy.
- The user's voice notes say "no flirt opener" — pick a different
  strategy.
