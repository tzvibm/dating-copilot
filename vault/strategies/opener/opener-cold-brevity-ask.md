## What it is

**Targets:** opener 0 → 60+ AND escalation signal in a single move
by eliminating all framing and sending the ask alone. The absence
of justification IS the move.

**Mechanisms:** A10 sociometer-positive (high-MV men don't justify
invitations) + A7 scarcity-implied (asking for tonight implies a
window exists, without stating it) + A16 autonomy (yes/no is the
lowest-friction ask possible) + I11 direct game at maximum
compression.

The pattern is two or three words, nothing else. No scarcity
explanation, no profile reference, no small talk, no framing. The
bet: brevity at a cold moment signals the sender has options and
isn't performing. The message asks exactly one question and stops.
The sociometer read (A10) fires because men who need to justify an
invitation implicitly reveal they aren't confident the invitation
stands on its own. Not justifying says the same thing a frame does
— without a frame.

Distinct from opener-scarcity-direct-ask: that card's mechanism is
A7 (explicit scarcity front-loaded as the frame). This card's
mechanism is A10 (implied sufficiency via brevity). Both are
escalation-compressed openers for cold threads; the difference is
*what generates the pull* — the constraint vs. the confidence.

Example illustrations:

- "Free tonight?"
- "Tonight?"
- "Plans tonight?"

Each is one question. The time is today. No justification appended.

## Prerequisites

phase_progress: no numeric prerequisites.

State conditions (HARD):
- Thread is cold/DOA — polite acks or total silence. After any
  warm or substantive exchange, brevity reads as withdrawal, not
  confidence. Use only where there's nothing warm to reference.
- goal_type is casual-sex or quick-meet-window. The brevity frame
  assumes compression is the right move; extended-window goals
  contradict this.
- User's voice substrate (voice.md) supports direct register.
  Brevity on a tentative voice collapses — the message needs to
  feel intentional, not like he ran out of ideas.
- A real plan or window exists today. The message implies "I'm
  doing something tonight" without stating it; if that's false,
  the implicit frame is hollow.

## When it works

- **Thread is cold and has no warmth to reference.** There's nothing
  to riff on, so leading with content from her profile reads as
  reaching. Brevity sidesteps that entirely — the message is about
  now, not about her profile.
- **User can hold the frame.** If she replies with "to what?", the
  response needs to be equally clean and confident — a specific
  place or fork, no backpedaling. The card only works if the follow-
  up matches the opener's register.
- **Goal is today.** The three-word ask implies today-or-nothing
  without saying it; the implication is part of the confidence
  signal. This doesn't work for extended or multi-day arcs.
- **Her profile reads as social and direct.** Active, foodie, beach
  bars, phone caller — archetypes that prefer getting to the point.
  Works less well on earnest/introspective profiles where the
  brevity reads as indifferent.

## When it fails

- **No real move behind it.** If she says yes and he has nothing
  planned, the brevity collapses into "he just asked everyone and
  is seeing who bites." The card requires a real plan to back it up.
- **Warm thread — brevity reads as withdrawal.** After substantive
  exchange, a two-word message reads as the sender pulling back or
  losing interest. The mechanism only fires on cold threads where
  brevity can't be read as a downgrade.
- **Follow-up is anxious.** She replies "to what?" and he over-
  explains or hedges — the entire frame is gone. If the user can't
  back the card with clean follow-through, a more framed opener
  is safer.
- **Extended-window goal → brevity signals low investment.** On a
  longer timeline where rapport is being built, a three-word message
  early reads as disinterest, not confidence.
