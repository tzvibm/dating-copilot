## What it is

**Targets:** opener 0 → 60+ AND escalation/logistics 0 → 30+ in a
single move; phase compression — the opener that bypasses rapport
on the bet that frame confidence and real time-pressure substitute
for warmth investment.

**Mechanisms:** I11 statement of intent + A7 scarcity (real options)
+ A1 pre-suasive frame at maximum confidence + A19 mate-preference
signal for agency + I9 time distortion.

The pattern is two clauses, no padding: one line establishing real
time-bounded scarcity, one direct invitation or open-fork ask.
Nothing in between. The thread skips the rapport loop entirely on
the bet that the timeline forces a decision and frame-confidence
makes the ask land.

The mechanism is direct-game (I11) leaning hard on real scarcity
(A7). When the time-pressure is genuine — he's actually leaving
Sunday, she's actually local for the week — the compressed frame
inherits the credibility of the constraint itself. Pre-suasion (A1)
runs at maximum: the move installs *I'm a man with a window, this
is what I'm here for* as the entire frame. That ceiling is the
highest of any opener but the most fragile — there's no recovery
if she balks. A19 reads activate: agency, frame-stability under no-
warmth-substrate conditions, decisiveness. I9 time distortion is
implicit: a single message that opens, escalates, and asks compresses
days of phase progression into one turn. Sociometer-wise (A10) the
move is sociometer-positive *only when the scarcity is real* — if
the constraint is manufactured, the bottom drops out and the same
line reads as desperate.

This card is the opener equivalent of betting the whole stack on
one move. The win condition is high — meet set in one turn — and
the failure mode is hard — she ghosts, no rapport substrate to
recover on.

Example illustrations:

- "Only here a few more days. Worth grabbing a drink."
- "Leaving sunday. what does your week look like."
- "Moving thursday. should meet before then."
- "Three nights left in this city. wine bar on smith or somewhere
  noisier."

Each names the constraint plainly, asks plainly, hands her a
specific or a fork, and ends.

## When it works

- The scarcity is *real*. He genuinely leaves Sunday, she's
  genuinely local, the constraint exists in the world independent
  of the message. Manufactured scarcity (A7 failure mode) collapses
  the entire frame; the move depends on the constraint being
  externally verifiable.
- Goal is `quick-meet-window`. The move encodes that goal in its
  structure — extended-window goals make the urgency frame
  contradict the timeline and undercut both.
- User's voice substrate (`voice.md`) supports direct register. I11
  fails without warmth conviction backing the frame; if the voice
  reads tentative, the bold ask exposes the gap.
- The thread is dead-on-arrival or this is the first message. The
  move skips rapport by design; deploying it after a warm exchange
  wastes the rapport that already accumulated.
- Logistics on her end are trivial — she's local, there's no
  coordination cost beyond picking a night.

## When it fails

- **Manufactured scarcity → A7 inversion + A10 sociometer crater.**
  The moment the timeline pattern-matches as fake (vague city, no
  specific dates, repeats across matches), the entire mechanism
  inverts. Real-options scarcity requires real options. Faked, it
  reads as desperation theatre.
- **No warmth substrate + tentative voice → A1 frame collapse.** I11
  direct game requires the sender to be able to *back the frame*
  under pressure. A direct ask from a user whose follow-up register
  is anxious creates the worst spike-and-fade in the catalog —
  she replies once at the level he set, and the moment he can't
  hold it, the thread implodes.
- **Extended-window goal → frame-goal contradiction.** If he's
  staying, the urgency reads as fake immediately; A7 fails because
  the constraint isn't real. Use a longer arc.
- **Skipping over a warm reply to push the ask → A2 inversion + A11
  ladder skip.** If she'd already given a substantive reply, the
  scarcity push throws away the rapport accumulated. The commitment
  ladder (A3) still applies — direct game compresses it, doesn't
  abolish it. After a warm reply, escalate via the rapport rung,
  not the cold-direct rung.
- **Both are passing through → mutual scarcity reads as no-point.**
  When neither party is anchored, the urgency frame loses asymmetry
  and the move reads as "this is going nowhere anyway."
- **Ask isn't specific → A16 autonomy + A3 logistics ambiguity.**
  "What does your week look like" is a softer fork than "drinks
  thursday, wine bar on smith or the noisy one"; on a cold thread
  the softer fork is fine, but vague "we should hang sometime"
  collapses the whole move into wishful framing.
