## What it is

Signal your limited time in the city, then make a direct meeting invitation.
No warm-up, no rapport buffer. The scarcity ("only here a few days") does
the qualifying work without asking for it — she either bites or she doesn't.

The pattern: one line about your timeline, one direct ask or statement.
Nothing in between.

Examples (user's voice register):
- "Only here a few more days. Worth grabbing a drink."
- "Leaving [city] Sunday. What does your week look like?"
- "Moving on Thursday. Should meet before then."

## When it works

- quick-meet-window with a phone-caller or low-text-preference archetype.
- She's local and logistics are trivial (close distance, nothing to coordinate).
- Thread is stalled or flat — skips rapport-rebuild, just advances the ball.
- User voice is confident and direct, not warm/tentative.

## When it fails

- She hasn't replied at all yet — too cold for a scarcity push.
- She's also passing through — mutual scarcity can signal "no point."
- Extended-window goal — urgency frame undercuts the longer investment.
- She gave a warm, engaged reply and you skip over it to push logistics.
