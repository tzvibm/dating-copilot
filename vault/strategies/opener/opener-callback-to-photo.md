## What it is

**Targets:** opener 0 → 60+ via specific-noticing reciprocity at low
investment.

**Mechanisms:** A2 reciprocity gradient (specific noticing) + A18
hyperpersonal compression + A1 pre-suasive curious-stranger frame +
A10 sociometer-positive signal.

The move picks one non-obvious detail from her photos — not the dog,
not the beach, not the obvious centrepiece, but the second-thing-in-
frame: the book on the side table, the skyline behind her, the half-
visible tattoo, the bar in the background. Then it asks a single 5-
second-answerable question or hands her a one-line take on that
detail. The opener is short by design.

The mechanism stack runs through reciprocity (A2) at exactly the right
gradient. Specific noticing transmits "I see you, but at manageable
size" — small enough that returning the energy doesn't feel like
work, large enough that the message is unmistakably *for her*, not
copy-paste. The investment-just-under-hers rule (A2) is satisfied
because her cost to reply is also small. The non-obvious choice
encodes attention-as-discrimination (A6 selective expression
adjacent): noticing the obvious thing carries no signal because
everyone notices it; noticing the second thing signals he reads
texture, which lifts the sociometer (A10) reading without claim. The
short form leverages compression (A18) — a single well-chosen line
in text outperforms a paragraph because the medium rewards density.
Pre-suasively (A1) the frame installed is *interesting-stranger-
who-paid-attention*, which caps higher than interview and lower than
flirty-edge — sustainable across arbitrary downstream registers.

This is the opener-progress lever to reach for whenever a real
specific hook exists. Lead-with-take wins when there isn't one.

Example illustrations:

- "the bookstore in your 3rd photo. where is that."
- "that café in your 2nd photo is on my list. need a verdict."
- "the tattoo on your arm. story or aesthetic."
- "is that the rooftop bar in la condesa or am I making that up."

Each names one concrete thing, asks something answerable in a single
short reply, and hands her exactly one thing to pull on.

## When it works

- At least one photo carries a non-obvious nameable detail. Generic
  beach pics or model shots offer no specific-noticing surface; the
  A2 mechanism can't fire on absent detail.
- The reference is *one* thing, not a tour of her profile. Stacked
  references invert the gradient (A2) into obligation alarm.
- The phrasing is answerable in five seconds. Cognitive ease (A18)
  is half the move — a question that requires thought reads as
  interview-frame and caps low (A1).
- The register is curious, not flattering. Curiosity is sociometer-
  neutral; flattery on a stranger is a sociometer leak (A10).

## When it fails

- **Detail is the obvious thing → A2 collapses to zero.** "Cute dog"
  carries no discrimination signal because everyone says it. The
  reciprocity mechanism only fires on noticing she registers as
  *for her specifically*.
- **Vague reference → A1 small-talk frame ceiling.** "Nice tattoo"
  fails the specificity test and drops back into generic-compliment
  territory, which sets the pre-suasive ceiling at small-talk.
- **Detail is something she didn't deliberately stage → A16 autonomy
  violation.** Calling out her body, a barely-visible bystander, or
  anything she didn't mean to surface puts her in performance-frame
  ("did he process me correctly?") and triggers withdrawal.
- **Stacked questions → A2 inversion + cognitive load (A18).** "what
  book is that, also where are you from" reads as interrogation and
  collapses the manageable-size advantage. One move per opener.
- **Forced thoughtful answer → cognitive-ease loss.** "What does that
  bookshop mean to you?" weights the reply too heavily for an
  opener; she leaves the message un-replied because the cost feels
  wrong for the phase.
- **Effortful cleverness on the noticing → A10 leak + I6 DLV.** A
  callback that performs how-clever-of-me-to-spot-this signals
  optimisation-for-impressing, which the brain reads as low-MV
  evidence. Specificity carries the move; the writing should
  disappear.
