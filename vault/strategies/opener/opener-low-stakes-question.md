## What it is

Ask one specific, low-effort question that's interesting to answer
because it's about a small choice — not "what do you do" or "where
are you from". Pulls a small piece of personality without demanding
much energy from her.

Examples: "where do you go when you actually want to read", "what's
the move on a sunday with no plan", "wine or coffee for a first
read on someone".

## When it works

- Profile has zero hooks for a callback or prompt-flip.
- Your question implies you have a take (so it's not just an
  interview).
- The question is answerable in one sentence — low friction.

## When it fails

- Question is "what do you do" tier — you sound bored.
- Stack of two questions in one message — interrogation.
- Question is about her looks or attractiveness — burns the gambit.
