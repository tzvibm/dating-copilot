## What it is

**Targets:** opener 0 → 60+ via frame installation.

**Mechanisms:** A14 otherness installation + A1 pre-suasive frame +
I1 cocky-funny + A10 sociometer-positive signal.

The move opens the thread with a confident statement, observation,
or take from *his own world* — a take on his week, an opinion he
holds, a small piece of his day. Her bio calibrates the *register*
(creative-bookish gets a culture take, planner-type gets a logistics
tease, outdoorsy gets a weekend-frame); her bio is never the
*subject*.

The mechanism stack: leading with his own energy preserves the
desire-gap (A14) from message one — she's drawn in by curiosity
about him, not invited to evaluate herself. Choosing a frame with a
point of view installs a high pre-suasive ceiling (A1) that
hyperpersonal compounding (A18) will then amplify in his favour
through the rest of the thread. The cocky-funny register (I1)
encodes authority by leak (A5) and humour-as-competence (A19) in a
single move that doesn't read as performance, because the self-
awareness defuses the arrogance. The sociometer (A10) registers
positively because no validation is being requested — the message
*delivers* a position rather than *asks* for engagement.

The first thing she should learn from the opener is one specific
thing about *him*, not the other way around. The opener is an
invitation into his frame, not a question about hers.

Example illustrations (from his world, with calibrated register):

- (Creative-bookish register) "ok hot take: tuesday night > friday
  night. you can fight me on this."
- (Planner register) "wednesday's empty on my calendar. that's a
  problem someone needs to solve."
- (Outdoorsy register) "monday i'm flying out and trying to make
  this week count. consequences pending."
- (Foodie register) "found a place last week that does the city's
  best mezcal flight. dangerous information to be in possession of."

Each example carries a take (a position, not a question), implies
his life is in motion, and leaves exactly one open loop (A15) for
her to pull on — without ever quoting her bio.

## Prerequisites

phase_progress: no numeric prerequisites — this is an opener entry
move.

State conditions:
- her bio gives any register hook to calibrate the take's flavour
  against (even thin signal is enough).
- user has a real take to draw on (cocky-funny collapses on canned
  material).

## When it works

- Her bio gives *any* register hook to calibrate against — even
  thin signal is enough. The take's topic flavour comes from her
  vibe; the take's substance comes from his world.
- He has a real take to draw on. Cocky-funny (I1) collapses on
  canned material; the move requires actual conviction or specific
  recent experience to source from.
- `voice.md` permits direct/playful register. If the user's voice is
  more reserved, soften the cocky element — the take can stand
  without it.
- Photos don't give a stronger specific hook (otherwise
  `opener-callback-to-photo` is the better pull, since A2 specific-
  noticing is a more reliable opener-progress lever when available).

## When it fails

- **Take is too generic → A14 collapse.** "The weather sure is
  something" carries no point of view, no otherness signal, no
  frame. The mechanism requires a take with substance; vagueness
  defaults the message back into supplicant-frame.
- **Take is about her → A16 autonomy violation + A2 inversion.**
  "Your bio is interesting" / "your photos suggest you're…" — same
  reactive trap as bio-quoting. The mechanism only fires when the
  message is genuinely about him.
- **Stacked with a question → cognitive load + A1 frame
  ambiguity.** Adding "what about you?" or any second move dilutes
  the take and reverts to interview-frame. One move per opener.
- **Performed cleverness → A10 sociometer leak + I6 DLV.** A take
  that needs a runway, multiple subordinate clauses, or a wink at
  itself signals optimisation-for-impressing. The brain reads
  effort-to-impress as low-mate-value evidence. The take must read
  effortless.
- **Frame ceiling overshoots her register → A1 sustainability
  failure.** A flirty-edgy take landed on a match whose voice cues
  read more reserved creates a ceiling she can't sustainably reach
  — she replies once at the level he set and then collapses
  inward. Calibrate the take's heat to her likely top-end, not his.
