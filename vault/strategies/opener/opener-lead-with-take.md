## What it is

Open with a confident take, observation, or frame from **your own
world** — your day, your week, your weekend, a tiny opinion you have
about something. Her bio sets the *register* (tone, topic flavour),
not the subject. The message reads like you're inviting her in, not
auditioning by referencing her profile back at her.

The point: she should learn one thing about *you* from your opener.
Not the other way around.

Examples (note the brevity — one or two short sentences, no em
dashes, no compound clauses):

- (Profile reads "creative / cafe-hopper") "on a quest for a cafe
  here that isn't lo-fi only. accepting recommendations 😈"
- (Profile reads "outdoorsy") "friday night or saturday morning.
  one of those is clearly the right answer."
- (Profile reads "planner type") "thursday calendar's looking too
  empty. need to fix that."
- (Profile reads "bookish") "the bookstore on this block is
  dangerous. someone needs to slow me down."

## When it works

- You have *any* hook from her vibe to calibrate the take's topic
  register (drinks/food/weekend/work — pick what fits her bio).
- Your take is one sentence with a clear point of view.
- The opener is an *invitation* into your move, not a question about
  hers.

## When it fails

- Take is too generic ("the weather sure is something"). No hook.
- Take is *about her* ("your bio is interesting") — same reactive
  trap by another name.
- Stacking with a question — pick one move per opener.
- Trying-too-hard wit. If you'd never say it out loud, scrap it.
