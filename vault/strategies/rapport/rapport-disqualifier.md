## What it is

**Targets:** rapport 40 → 70 via audition inversion; raises
qualifying-axis substrate as a side effect by installing the
*she's-clearing-his-bar* frame.

**Mechanisms:** I3 the neg (durable form) + A9 reactance + A10
sociometer signal (non-supplication) + F1 Rake / F5 Coquette
register flavour + A11 self-perception (her defending becomes
investment).

The move flips the audition. Standard texting puts him in the
position of demonstrating he meets her bar; this card inverts the
question to *she has to convince him she's not a category he's
already amused-skeptical of*. Done playfully, it reads as confident
non-supplication — he's not auditioning, which means he must have
real options, which is the cleanest possible sociometer signal.

The mechanism stack runs through I3 in its operationally durable
form. Mystery's original framing was destabilisation; what survives
in text is the version that targets *categories she opted into* —
her self-described archetype, a stated preference, an opinion she
authored — never her body or innate traits. Reactance (A9) generates
the pull: he installs a freedom (his approval, the right to clear
his bar) she now has slight motivation to claim. The freedom has to
be game-able for the mechanism to fire — if the bar is rigged or
attacks something innate, the brain registers injury rather than
playful constraint. Sociometer-wise (A10) the move is the highest-
leverage non-supplication signal in the rapport phase: a man not
auditioning is registered as a man with options, full stop. The
register sits in F1 Rake (irreverent appetite) blended with F5
Coquette (you-almost-have-me, you-almost-don't) — both pleasure-
coded, both pulling against propriety just enough that misattribution
(A12) labels the resulting tension as attraction. Self-perception
(A11) compounds when she defends, expands, or counter-disqualifies
— now she's invested in clearing the bar, which Bem-mechanically
upgrades her felt interest.

The card carries an edge by design and shouldn't be sanded off.
The audition-flip is the move. The targets are categorical (her
self-described archetype, her stated takes, her chosen labels) —
the rule is tactical, not moral: targets she chose let her play
the game, targets she didn't choose let the move land as injury,
and injury collapses the mechanism. It's about what works, not
what's permitted.

Example illustrations:

- "you'd have to convince me you're not just another spotify-list
  foodie."
- "fair warning, I'm tough on cafe recs. you're either bringing
  your A game or we're not friends."
- "I'll be honest, I have low expectations of anyone who claims
  they 'love to travel' on hinge. defend yourself."
- "ok if you're a sunday-brunch person we have a structural
  problem. talk me out of it."

Each names a category she opted into, sets a game-able bar, and
hands her room to defend, escalate, or counter-disqualify.

## When it works

- Rapport substrate is warm. The reactance mechanism (A9) requires
  a freedom she's already mildly invested in; cold matches don't
  experience the takeaway as loss because there's nothing to take
  away. Below rapport 30, the move reads as hostility.
- The bar targets a *chosen category* — her self-described
  archetype, her stated take, an opinion she authored. The
  mechanism fires on opt-in surfaces; it injures on innate ones.
- Voice register (`voice.md`) supports playful-arrogant. F1 Rake
  flavor on a reserved voice substrate reads costume; the move
  collapses on the gap.
- One disqualifier per turn, max. Stacking two challenges in one
  message is too much push and no pull (I2 push-pull requires the
  pull half on the next turn or in the same beat). Reactance
  miscalibrates upward when the freedom-removal stacks.
- She can carry it. Some matches are reciprocating-warm but
  literal-reading; the move requires she pattern-match the
  playfulness. If her replies are short and earnest, the pull
  doesn't fire — switch to share-then-volley.

## When it fails

- **Bar targets innate trait → A9 miscalibrated, registers as
  injury not arousal.** Body, looks, weight, intelligence-as-
  innate, anything she didn't choose: the brain doesn't relabel
  the hit as attraction. Pain just registers as a reason to leave.
  The original Mystery-form neg (destabilization on appearance) is
  the template for failure, not success.
- **Deployed cold → A9 mechanism dormant + cruelty register.**
  Reactance requires a freedom she's already invested in. With no
  rapport substrate the disqualification reads as he's-decided-
  not-to-like-her; she leaves because there's nothing to claim.
- **Bar isn't game-able → A11 ladder blocked.** "You'd have to be
  smarter than my last one" — she can't defend the bar without
  punching a non-existent target. The freedom installed has to be
  one she can plausibly clear; otherwise self-perception doesn't
  compound because there's no path to invest.
- **Tone reads stern not playful → F1 Rake collapses to judge.** The
  defining quality of the durable I3 form is *amused* skepticism.
  Stern skepticism inverts the move — she's not auditioning for a
  judge, she's leaving. Read aloud; if it lands without a wink, it
  doesn't ship.
- **Stacked with another challenge → A12 tension without warmth.**
  Misattribution (A12) requires warmth substrate to relabel arousal
  as attraction. Two pushes in one message starves the substrate
  and the tension reads as hostile.
- **Lands on something she's actually sensitive about → A14 collapse
  + cold withdrawal.** A category she opted into in her bio may
  still be a real-insecurity surface for *her*; the move requires
  the topic be load-bearing-but-light. Read register before deploy.
