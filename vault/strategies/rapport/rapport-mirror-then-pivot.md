## What it is

First sentence: match her last reply's energy (one short volley on
her topic). Second sentence: introduce one new angle that's adjacent
to what she said — not a hard pivot. Keeps the thread alive while
giving her something fresh to respond to.

Example: she says "obsessed with the food here". You: "the X taco
on Y street is the best I've had this trip. what's something you'd
travel for that isn't food."

## When it works

- The conversation is warm but circling the same topic.
- The new angle is genuinely related, not a tonal jump.
- You can land both halves in 2 short sentences.

## When it fails

- Pivot feels like a non-sequitur or interview question.
- Mirror half is too long — you're padding.
- New angle is a deeper question she's not ready for.
