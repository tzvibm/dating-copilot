## What it is

**Targets:** rapport 40 → 65 via meeting-then-leading; the move that
keeps a circling thread alive without forcing a tonal jump.

**Mechanisms:** H6 enter-their-spirit (calibrated mirroring) + A17
mirror/label (Voss) + A2 reciprocity at matched gradient + A6 liking-
via-similarity + A11 self-perception via volunteered angle.

The two-beat structure: *first sentence* matches her last reply's
energy with one short volley on her topic. *Second sentence*
introduces one new angle that's adjacent — not a hard pivot, not a
non-sequitur, a half-step into territory she can plausibly see from
where she just was. Both halves in two short sentences total.

The mechanism is Greene's H6 in compressed form. Enter her spirit
first, lead her after — meet her where she is before nudging her
where you want to be. The mirror half does A17 work: echoing her
last topic at her register signals tracking, validates the move
she just made, and discharges any latent reciprocity-anxiety. Voss's
label/mirror mechanism dampens the small evaluative load every
reply carries. Once the mirror has landed, the pivot half can
introduce something fresh without the move reading as
not-listening — because the listening was just *demonstrated*. A2
reciprocity stays at matched gradient: he's volleyed at her size,
not above it, so the obligation alarm doesn't fire. A6 liking-via-
similarity activates on the mirror half; A11 self-perception
activates on the pivot, because the new angle hands her something
fresh to volunteer on — and what she volunteers retroactively
becomes evidence to her that she's interested.

The card's flavour is *unobtrusive thread maintenance*. Not the
flashy tension moves (push-pull, disqualifier); this one keeps the
warm thread warm and converts circling into progress. The skill is
in the adjacency of the new angle — too close and it's redundant,
too far and it's interview-pivot.

Example illustrations:

- (she: "obsessed with the food here") "the X taco on Y street is
  the best I've had this trip. what's something you'd travel for
  that isn't food."
- (she: "monday burnout is real") "tuesday morning energy on a
  monday is a niche skill. what are you not doing this week."
- (she: "I just got back from oaxaca") "oaxaca's the only mezcal
  argument I've ever lost. what's the place you keep going back to."
- (she: "obsessed with this book right now") "I read that one in a
  hotel lobby in 4 hours. what's the last thing you read that
  ruined a weekend."

Each opens with one short volley acknowledging her topic, then
hands her one adjacent fork to step onto.

## When it works

- The thread is warm but circling — she's reciprocating, but the
  topic has stopped accumulating new information. The move is
  designed to break the loop without breaking the warmth.
- The new angle is genuinely adjacent, not a tonal jump. H6
  calibrated mirroring requires the lead to come from inside her
  register; pivoting from "obsessed with this book" to "what's
  your relationship with your father" inverts the move into
  interview-jump.
- Both halves fit in two short sentences. The mirror has to be
  short enough not to pad; the pivot has to be short enough not
  to read as setup-for-something. Length kills both halves' work.
- Mid-rapport (rapport 30-65). Below 30 the mirror has nothing to
  build on; above 65 the move is too gentle — she's ready for
  push-pull or share-then-volley.
- Voice register supports relaxed warmth. The card is non-
  performative by design; it doesn't carry F1 Rake or I3 neg
  flavor.

## When it fails

- **Pivot is non-sequitur → H6 mechanism inverts to interview-
  jump.** A new angle that doesn't land inside her current
  register reads as not-listening — the mirror half then registers
  as transactional rather than genuine. Same A6 cost as ignoring
  her topic outright.
- **Mirror is too long → A2 inversion + padding.** A multi-sentence
  echo signals he's filling space until the pivot. The mirror's
  job is fast tracking-acknowledgment; one short volley is enough.
  Length signals optimisation.
- **New angle is a deeper question she's not ready for → A11 ladder
  skips a rung.** "What's your relationship with your mother" off
  a casual food thread is a four-rung jump and her brain refuses
  the inconsistency. Adjacency is the operative constraint.
- **Pivot lands as another generic question → A1 ceiling collapse
  + A10 sociometer leak.** "what's something you'd travel for"
  works because of the qualifier "that isn't food" — it's a fork
  with a position implied. Without that, the pivot is biography-
  interview in costume.
- **Used when the thread is hot → opportunity cost.** When she's
  reciprocating substantively and the topic is generating real
  texture, mirror-then-pivot is too gentle; push-pull or share-
  then-volley would advance further. The card is for circling
  threads, not running ones.
- **Stacked: mirror + pivot + question + take → cognitive load
  (A18).** The move is two beats. Adding a third (a tease, a
  callback, an additional ask) overruns the message and dilutes
  the H6 elegance.
