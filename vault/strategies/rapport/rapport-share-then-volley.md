## What it is

**Targets:** rapport 30 → 70 via reciprocity-gradient calibration;
converts a transactional Q&A thread into mutual disclosure.

**Mechanisms:** A2 reciprocity gradient (calibrated vulnerability
disclosure) + F6 Charmer register + A11 self-perception ladder + A6
liking via selective expression + A11/A3 commitment ladder via
disclosure rung.

The two-beat move: share one concrete, slightly-vulnerable thing
about himself first — not résumé, not curated highlight, something
real she didn't already know. Then ask one question that maps to
what he shared so the volley returns balanced. The share lowers the
cost of her returning at-level. The question handed back is sized
to match.

The mechanism is the cleanest A2 application in the rapport phase.
Reciprocity is gradient: per-message investment slightly under hers
compounds, significantly above triggers obligation alarm. The card
calibrates the gradient *deliberately* by making the share small,
specific, and concrete — not a paragraph of self-disclosure (which
would invert the gradient), and not a fact (which carries no
disclosure cost and doesn't lower her bar to share back). The
specific size of the share sets the size of the return, which she
matches by self-perception (A11) — sharing back at his level
becomes evidence to her that the thread is the kind where they
share like this. The Bem mechanism does the rest: the disclosure
she volunteers becomes retroactive evidence she was already that
invested.

F6 Charmer is the register flavour. The Charmer makes her feel
like the centre of attention without overt seduction — listens,
returns focus to her in flattering precision. Share-then-volley
inhabits exactly that frame: he discloses just enough to model the
disclosure depth, then hands the floor back. Crucially the
Charmer's risk is interviewer-frame collapse if no take of his own
ever surfaces. This card hedges that risk by structuring the share
as a take-with-texture — he's offering substance, not just asking.
A6 liking-via-selective-expression activates because the share
signals real-not-canned: she registers that he's showing her
something specific, which compounds reciprocal-liking when she
shares back.

The card's flavour is *calibrated mutual disclosure*. Not the
tension moves; this one builds the warmth substrate other moves
operate on top of. Use early or mid-rapport when the thread is
warm but flat.

Example illustrations:

- "honestly the reason I came to mexico city was to get out of NYC
  after a brutal year. what's the last move you made just to get
  out of somewhere."
- "I picked up running because I was sleeping like garbage and
  needed something to make it worse. what's something you started
  for the wrong reason that stuck."
- "my best friend in this city is someone I met arguing about a
  taco place. what's the most ridiculous way you've made a real
  friend."
- "I read three books on a flight last month because I'd
  pre-loaded too many. embarrassing. what's the last thing you
  over-prepared and didn't need."

Each shares one concrete real thing, sizes the disclosure to fit
two short sentences, and asks a question that mirrors the share's
shape — not its content.

## When it works

- The thread is warm but transactional / Q&A-shaped. The mechanism
  is designed to break the interview pattern by modeling a
  different rhythm; it doesn't add value on a thread that's
  already running mutual disclosure.
- The share is real but light. A2 gradient requires the disclosure
  size to stay calibrated; trauma-dump or heavy-emotional content
  inverts the gradient and triggers A14 otherness collapse.
- The question maps to the share's *shape* (a similar-sized step,
  a parallel kind of disclosure), not its content (don't ask her
  the exact same question back at her — that's not volleying,
  that's deflection).
- The share carries texture / point of view, not biography. F6
  Charmer fails on canned material; the share has to read as
  specific-this-particular-thing, not generic.
- Both halves fit in 4 sentences total, max. Above that, A2 gradient
  inverts and the move reads as wall-of-text.

## When it fails

- **Share is too heavy → A2 inversion + A14 otherness collapse.**
  Multi-paragraph emotional disclosure on day two closes the
  desire-gap and triggers the same delayed cold-withdrawal Perel
  catalogues. She enjoys it in the moment and goes cold within 48
  hours. The calibration is the move; over-sharing isn't more
  share, it's a different (failing) move.
- **Share is performed self-aware → A10 sociometer leak.** "I'm
  such a hopeless romantic" / "I'm bad at this" — meta-share that
  signals optimisation-for-being-relatable rather than actual
  disclosure. The mechanism only fires on specific-real-thing; the
  meta-frame inverts to DLV.
- **Question doesn't mirror the share → A2 mechanism breaks.** A
  share about loneliness followed by a question about her favorite
  food reads as bait-and-switch. The volley has to maintain the
  disclosure register the share opened — otherwise the share just
  hangs without payoff and she can't tell what she's being
  invited to do.
- **No question, just the share → F6 inverts to monologue.** Without
  the volley half, the move is one-sided disclosure, which closes
  her out. The question is what makes it a volley rather than a
  reveal.
- **Share is résumé / curated highlight → A2 carries no disclosure
  cost.** "I lived in tokyo for two years" is biography; the
  mechanism requires actual mild vulnerability to lower the cost-
  to-share for her. Facts don't lower bars.
- **Stacked: share + take + tease + question → cognitive load
  (A18).** The move is two beats. Adding more dilutes the
  reciprocity-gradient calibration that does the work.
