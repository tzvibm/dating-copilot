## What it is

Share one concrete, slightly-vulnerable thing about yourself first,
then ask one question that invites her to match it. Not a résumé
fact — something she didn't know. Lowers the cost of her sharing
back.

Example: "honestly the reason I came to mexico city was to escape
NYC after a brutal year. what's the last move you made just to get
out of somewhere."

## When it works

- Conversation is warm but transactional / Q&A-shaped.
- Your share is real but not heavy (no trauma dumps).
- The question maps to what you shared so the volley is balanced.

## When it fails

- Share is too heavy or self-aware ("I'm such a hopeless romantic").
- Share + question is more than 4 sentences — wall of text.
- Question doesn't mirror what you shared; feels like a bait-switch.
