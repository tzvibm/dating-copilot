## What it is

Tease her about a *choice* she made — her take, her vibe, her opinion,
something she explicitly opted into. The point is to engage with what
she said as though you're equals trading shots, not auditioning. Done
right, it raises temperature without grinding.

The line you don't cross: never tease her appearance, body, looks,
weight. Tease the things *she chose*, not the things she didn't.

Examples:

- (She said she only does cafes.) "this is the part where I find out
  you've never had a beer in your life."
- (She bragged about her playlist.) "you definitely run the music in
  your friend group. I'm sorry to your friends."
- (She named a niche TV show.) "ok so you're one of *those*."
- (She said she likes hiking.) "outdoorsy people scare me. you're
  going to make me walk somewhere."

## When it works

- She just shared something with personality / a clear take.
- Voice register has flirty / playful permission (most defaults).
- The tease is one short sentence; the punch is in word choice, not
  word count.
- It opens for her to volley — she can defend, escalate, or join in.

## When it fails

- Tease lands as judgement, not play. Re-read it as if a stranger
  said it to you. If it stings without warmth, scrap it.
- Tease about her body, weight, appearance, or anything she didn't
  choose — burns the entire thread.
- Tease lands when she's actually being vulnerable about something
  hard. Read the room.
- Stacking: tease + question in one message. Pick one.
