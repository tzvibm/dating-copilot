## What it is

**Targets:** rapport 40 → 70 via tension-with-warmth; the
temperature lift that converts agreeable rapport into pull.

**Mechanisms:** I2 push-pull + A12 misattribution of arousal + H4
mix pleasure with pain + A9 light reactance + F5 Coquette register
flavour.

The move teases her about a *choice* she made — her take, her
stated vibe, an opinion or label she explicitly opted into. The
tone is equals trading shots, not auditioning. Done right, it
raises temperature without grinding. The line lands as warmth-with-
edge: she doesn't lose status, she gains a beat she can volley
against.

The mechanism is I2 in operation. Push-pull at the message level
generates the small undifferentiated arousal that misattribution
(A12) labels as attraction when the proximate cause is him. The
key constraint is the *embedded warmth* — A12 only fires when the
tension has positive context; without warmth substrate, the same
line registers as hostility and the brain labels the arousal
correctly as annoyance. H4 is the texture: pure pleasure habituates
fast, pure pain extinguishes engagement, but alternation locks
attention. A9 light reactance is part of the lift — the tease
implies a small freedom (his interest, his approval) she now has
slight motivation to claim or contest. F5 Coquette register frames
the rhythm: you-almost-have-me, you-almost-don't, on a single beat.

The targeting rule is mechanical, not moral: tease *what she
chose* — her take, her vibe, her stated preference, a label she
authored — never her body, weight, looks, or anything innate. The
rule isn't about etiquette; it's about whether the misattribution
mechanism fires. Choices she opted into are game-able surfaces;
the brain experiences play on them as pleasurable conflict. Innate
or unchosen surfaces produce real injury; the brain doesn't
relabel injury as attraction, and the message becomes a reason to
leave.

The card's flavour is *equals trading shots*. F5 Coquette in its
operationally durable form: the pendulum is the move, but the
pendulum's amplitude stays small. One push per turn, max.

Example illustrations:

- (she said she only does cafes) "this is the part where I find
  out you've never had a beer in your life."
- (she bragged about her playlist) "you definitely run the music
  in your friend group. I'm sorry to your friends."
- (she named a niche TV show) "ok so you're one of *those*."
- (she said she likes hiking) "outdoorsy people scare me. you're
  going to make me walk somewhere."

Each targets a chosen category, lands in one short sentence, and
opens for her to defend, escalate, or join in.

## When it works

- Rapport is warm. A12 misattribution requires warmth substrate to
  relabel arousal as attraction; below rapport 30, the tease lands
  without the cushion and registers as cold.
- The tease targets a *choice* she made — a stated take, an opinion
  she authored, a category she opted into. The mechanism fires on
  game-able surfaces; it injures on innate ones.
- Voice register (`voice.md`) supports flirty/playful. Most defaults
  permit this; reserved voice substrates can carry a softer form.
- One short sentence. The punch lives in word choice and rhythm,
  not word count. Length collapses the move into commentary.
- The tease leaves room to volley — she can defend, counter-tease,
  or join in. A11 self-perception ladders when she steps into the
  game; if the line closes off return, the mechanism doesn't
  compound.

## When it fails

- **Tease lands as judgment → A12 inversion + A6 liking collapse.**
  The line that stings without warmth converts the misattribution
  mechanism into hostility-detection. The cushion has to be
  visible; if reading aloud sounds like critique, the move
  doesn't ship.
- **Targets innate trait → A9 miscalibrated, registers as injury.**
  Body, looks, weight, anything she didn't choose: the brain
  doesn't relabel pain as attraction. This is the I3 / I2 failure
  mode, identical mechanism. Choices, not innate.
- **Vulnerability misread → A14 cold register + A6 collapse.** If
  she just shared something hard or earnest, even a well-aimed
  tease lands as not-tracking-the-room. The move requires register-
  read; the same line that lands at her playful baseline lands
  cold on a tender turn.
- **Stacked: tease + question + callback → I2 dilution.** Push-pull
  is one push per turn. Stacking a tease with a follow-up question
  overruns the move and the misattribution beat doesn't have room
  to fire — the brain processes the whole message as interview-
  with-edge.
- **Generic edge with no specific trigger → A10 sociometer leak.**
  A tease that doesn't land on something specific she just said
  reads as performed-edge — the same DLV signature as generic
  flirty-edge openers. The specificity is what carries the move.
- **Used too often → habituation (A13 inverted).** Variable-ratio
  reinforcement requires variation; a thread of nothing-but-
  teasing flattens the schedule and the mechanism extinguishes.
  One push per turn at most, and not every turn.
