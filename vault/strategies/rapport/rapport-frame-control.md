## What it is

She threw a test — a curveball question, a mild tease at you, a
"trying-to-throw-you-off" line. The move is **brief acknowledge +
redirect to your direction**. Don't fold (over-explain, apologize),
don't stonewall (terse "ok"), don't escalate the test into an
argument. Stay confident, keep your move.

Examples:

- *Her*: "you say that to all the girls?"
  → "only the ones I'd actually want to grab drinks with. thursday?"
- *Her*: "you sound like you're reading from a script lol"
  → "if it was a script it'd be working better. drinks?"
- *Her*: "what makes you so sure?"
  → "instinct. and I'm right enough of the time."

## When it works

- She just tested / teased / curveballed. The move keeps your
  direction alive without folding or fighting.
- The acknowledgment is one short clause; the redirect is your
  actual move.
- Tone is playful + secure, not defensive.

## When it fails

- She said something serious / vulnerable, not a test. Reading a
  real concern as a test reads as cold.
- The redirect is to the same move you'd already pushed and she
  side-stepped — that's grinding, not redirecting.
- Acknowledgment becomes a paragraph of justification ("haha no
  it's just that I…"). Folding.
