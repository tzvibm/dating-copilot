## What it is

**Targets:** rapport hold (no regression) AND escalation 0 → 30+ in
a single move; the reactive card that converts a shit-test into
forward motion.

**Mechanisms:** I7 frame control + I4 AMOG handling (translated to
text shit-tests) + A1 pre-suasion sustained turn-over-turn + A9
reactance-survival + A10 sociometer signal under pressure.

She threw a test. A shit-test ("you say that to all the girls?"),
a comparison frame ("you sound like every guy on hinge"), a
curveball ("what makes you so sure?"), a mild tease at his frame.
The move is **brief acknowledge + redirect to his original
direction**. Don't fold (over-explain, apologize, retreat). Don't
stonewall (terse "ok"). Don't enter her frame to refute it. Stay in
his and keep moving.

The mechanism is the cleanest demonstration of I7 in the catalog.
Whoever's frame the conversation operates in, controls it. A shit-
test is an invitation into her frame — usually defensive-comparison
("prove you're not like X") or evaluator-frame ("justify yourself").
The instinct to defend or explain is the failure: *engaging the
frame* loses it, regardless of what the words then say. The move
is to acknowledge briefly enough that she registers being heard,
and immediately return to his prior direction as though the test
didn't disturb it. That's I4 AMOG handling translated to text:
acknowledge the existence of the frame, refuse the frame, proceed.

A1 pre-suasion is the deeper layer. The opener installed a frame;
every subsequent turn either holds or yields it. A shit-test is the
first real pressure test on the frame; passing it sustains the
ceiling, which the rest of the rapport phase compounds against
(A18). A9 reactance is involved on her side — she pushed against
his frame to see if it gives; finding that it doesn't generates
the small reactance that misattribution (A12) labels as attraction.
Sociometer-wise (A10) the move is the highest-leverage moment in
rapport: how he handles a test reads diagnostic. A man who folds
or fights is a man whose gauge reads low; a man who stays warm and
keeps moving reads as having-options-and-knowing-it.

The card's flavour is *unbothered redirect*. Not banter, not
pushback. The energy is "I heard you, that's not the conversation
we're in, here's where we were."

Example illustrations:

- *Her*: "you say that to all the girls?"
  → "only the ones I'd actually want drinks with. thursday?"
- *Her*: "you sound like you're reading from a script lol"
  → "if it was a script it'd be working better. wine bar on smith
  or the noisy one."
- *Her*: "what makes you so sure?"
  → "instinct. and I'm right enough of the time."
- *Her*: "every guy on this app says that"
  → "and yet. saturday's the question."

Each acknowledges in one short clause and returns to the actual
move that was already in motion.

State conditions:

## When it works

- She threw an actual test, tease, or curveball — a frame-pressure
  signal, not real concern. The move depends on correctly classifying
  the move; reading vulnerability as test reads as cold (and
  collapses A6 liking).
- His original direction has somewhere to go. The redirect's value
  is in the *to* — there's a logistics anchor, a take, an open loop
  he was already moving on. A redirect with no destination is just
  brushed-off, which isn't frame-control, it's stonewalling.
- The acknowledgment is one short clause. Length signals the test
  rattled him; the brevity is the signal that it didn't.
- Tone is playful + secure, not stiff. F1 Rake flavor compatible —
  unbothered amusement, not corporate composure.
- Voice register supports brief confident lines. A user whose voice
  defaults to long warm explanations will have to actively cut for
  this move to read.

## When it fails

- **She wasn't testing → A6 inversion + A14 cold register.** Reading
  a serious or vulnerable line as a test reads as dismissive. The
  move requires correct classification; if she's actually sharing,
  the rapport mechanism that fires is share-then-volley, not frame-
  control.
- **Acknowledgment becomes justification → A10 sociometer leak.**
  "Haha no it's just that I — " is folding in costume. Any clause
  that explains, defends, or apologizes enters her frame and loses
  it. The mechanism breaks the moment the message exceeds one
  short acknowledgment clause.
- **Redirect is to the move she just side-stepped → grinding, not
  redirecting.** If her test was a pivot away from a meet-ask he'd
  already pushed, returning straight to the same ask reads as not-
  hearing-her. The redirect should be to *his direction* (the
  ongoing thread, a fresh adjacent move), not to the specific
  request she just declined.
- **No-acknowledgment stonewall → A14 cold-register collapse.**
  Skipping the acknowledgment entirely and just continuing reads as
  not-tracking, which is its own sociometer leak. The brief-
  acknowledge half is doing real work; cutting it doesn't make the
  move stronger.
- **Escalation into argument → A9 reactance-trap inverted.** Treating
  the test as a real claim to refute drops into her frame and
  burns the whole substrate. "I'm not like other guys" is the
  worst possible reply for the same reason it loses every time.
- **Mechanical deployment → A17 mechanism reads as therapy-speak.**
  The redirect should feel like the natural next thing he was going
  to say anyway, not a learned move. If it reads as choreographed,
  her brain pattern-matches it as game and the frame collapses by
  detection.
