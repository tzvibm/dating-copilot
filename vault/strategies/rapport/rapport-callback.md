## What it is

Bring back something she said earlier in the conversation as a
casual reference, then push the thread forward. Signals that you
were paying attention without making a thing of it.

Example: she mentioned hiking on day 1. Two days later: "ok the
hiking thing is on me — there's a 4am trail near here that's
supposedly worth it. how serious about it are you actually."

## When it works

- The earlier detail was specific, not a throwaway.
- The callback is low-key — no "remember when you said…" framing.
- It moves toward a possible activity or meet, even faintly.

## When it fails

- The detail wasn't actually important to her — feels surveillance-y.
- You overplay the callback ("I've been thinking about what you said
  about…").
- The current conversation has clearly moved on; callback feels
  forced.
