## What it is

**Targets:** rapport 30 → 70 via re-opening a closed loop she'd
half-forgotten; secondary lift on escalation when the callback
seeds a meet-anchor.

**Mechanisms:** A15 Zeigarnik (re-opening a stale loop) + A11 self-
perception ladder + A2 specific-noticing reciprocity at high
resolution + A18 hyperpersonal compounding.

The move surfaces one specific thing she said earlier in the thread
— a preference, a passing detail, an offhand take — and uses it as
the frame for forward motion. Not "remember when you said," not
"as you mentioned." A clean return to the detail that treats it as
shared ground, then pushes the thread an inch closer to a real
plan or a higher-resolution exchange.

The mechanism is the Zeigarnik (A15) lift. A detail she dropped
two turns or two days ago is a half-closed loop in her memory —
neither resolved nor purposefully held open. The callback re-opens
it, and her brain registers a small flash of recognition: *he was
listening, and the thing I said came back at me as something*. That
recognition is high-resolution A2 reciprocity — specific-noticing
at a level no one else does because no one else heard it. Self-
perception (A11) compounds in the same beat: the detail she
volunteered becomes, retroactively, a stake she was invested in;
his returning to it confirms (to her, internally) that the topic
mattered, which means *she* mattered to it. Bem's mechanism does
the rest. Hyperpersonal asymmetry (A18) amplifies the move —
specific recall in text reads stronger than the same recall in
speech because the medium strips so many other signals; precise
memory becomes loud.

The card's flavour is *attentive payoff*. Not the punchline-driven
moves; this one operates by quiet re-injection of her own material.
The forward push embedded in the callback is what keeps it from
collapsing into surveillance — the move is *callback then move*,
not callback alone.

Example illustrations:

- "the hiking thing is on me. there's a 4am trail near here that's
  supposedly worth it. how serious about it are you actually."
- "you said the mezcal place near roma. I went. you owe yourself an
  apology, that bar is a problem."
- "still thinking about your take on slow sundays. doing one
  saturday. interested in field-testing the theory."
- "the playlist thing is back in my head. send one. I'll send the
  worst song I love and we're even."

Each names her specific detail, lands a take or move on it, and
points the thread an inch forward.

## When it works

- The detail being recalled was specific, volunteered, and carried
  some genuine point of view — not a throwaway "lol same." The
  Zeigarnik (A15) mechanism only fires on loops that actually
  registered.
- The callback is offhand, never announced. "Remember when you
  said" externalises the memory and reads as performance. The
  natural form is to *use* the detail as the premise of a new
  move — the recall is implicit in the structure.
- The callback advances something — toward a meet, toward a higher-
  resolution disclosure, toward a small play. A11 only ladders if
  the move asks her to step. Pure recall without forward push is
  scrapbook-energy.
- Mid-rapport thread (rapport 30-70). Too early and the detail
  hasn't accumulated meaning; too late and the loop is closed and
  re-opening it reads as scrolling-back.

## When it fails

- **Detail wasn't important to her → A2 high-resolution noticing
  inverts to surveillance signal.** Specific-recall on something
  she said in passing reads as monitoring, not attention. The
  reciprocity mechanism fires on noticing she registers as for-
  her; it backfires on noticing she registers as creepy.
- **Announced callback → A14 otherness collapse + A11 mechanism
  blocked.** "I've been thinking about what you said about..."
  externalises the recall and re-frames the thread as *him
  processing her*, which puts her in observed-frame and triggers
  withdrawal. The self-perception ladder requires the move to feel
  like organic continuation, not retrospective.
- **Thread has clearly moved on → forced loop re-injection reads
  as evasion.** If the conversation has settled into a different
  topic with momentum, dragging back to the old thread reads as
  not-tracking-the-room. The Zeigarnik (A15) lift requires the
  loop to still be ambient; once she's mentally closed it, recall
  resurrects nothing.
- **Callback without forward motion → A11 fails to ladder + A15
  closes the loop instead of widening it.** Pure recall ("you
  mentioned hiking") closes the loop he could have used. The move
  has to spend the loop on something — a take, a fork, a soft
  meet-anchor — or it's a sociometer leak (he remembered, she now
  has to figure out what to do with that).
- **Stacked callbacks → I6 DLV + creep register.** Multiple recalls
  in one message reads as cataloging. One per message; the
  specificity is what carries it.
