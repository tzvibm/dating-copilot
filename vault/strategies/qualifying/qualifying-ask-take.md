## What it is

**Targets:** qualifying 30 → 70 via fork-with-take. Requires rapport
≥ 50 substrate; without warmth, forks read as interview, not play.

**Mechanisms:** A11 self-perception ladder + A2 reciprocity gradient
+ A9 light reactance + I12 five-questions territory + A16 competence-
support.

The move asks for *her take* on a small, concrete fork — not "tell me
about yourself", not "what do you do for fun", but a real opinion
question with a binary or ternary fork in it. The fork is the
mechanism: a question with options forces her to pick a side, and the
moment she picks she has acted in a way consistent with being someone
with a position. Self-perception (A11) does the rest — she infers, to
herself, that she is engaged here, because why else would she be
defending wine bars over hotel bars at this length. The internal
attribution is what makes the ladder rung stick.

The take-back-asymmetry is what differentiates this from interview-
frame. He has a position too (or implies he does), and the fork is
loaded — wine bars vs hotel bars isn't neutral; brunch vs breakfast
tacos isn't neutral. She is not being interviewed; she is being
invited into peer-banter where the cost of admission is a position.
That activates A16 competence-support (the question makes her feel
sharp, not auditioned) and a light A9 reactance pull (the framing
mock-implies a wrong answer, which she can game). The cumulative
effect is investment density per turn — I12 five-questions territory,
compressed.

Example illustrations (light forks with implied takes):

- "ok serious question. wine bars or hotel bars."
- "settle a debate i'm having. brunch or breakfast tacos."
- "give me your hottest take on this city. one shot, no hedging."
- "worst date idea you've heard with a straight face. go."

Each carries a fork (forced pick, not open-ended fish), implies he
has a take of his own ready to volley back, and is short enough that
A2 reciprocity stays in gradient — she answers in a sentence, not a
paragraph.

## When it works

- Rapport ≥ 50: she's reciprocating substantively, matching or
  raising energy. Without that substrate, forks read as interrogation
  (A2 inversion) and the A11 ladder rung doesn't lock in — her
  attribution becomes "going along," not "engaged."
- The question carries a real fork. Binary or ternary, gameable, with
  one side faintly defensible-against. Open-ended ("what kind of
  music do you like") is fishing, not qualifying — no A11 lock-in
  from naming a vague preference.
- He has a take of his own ready. The fork-with-take only works if he
  volleys back with a position when she picks; otherwise it slides
  back into interview and the I1 cocky-funny register collapses.
- The fork is light. She can answer in a sentence. Heavy forks
  ("what's your love language") collapse A14 otherness via premature
  disclosure and break the qualifying register.

## When it fails

- **Premature deployment → A11 misfire.** Used before rapport ≥ 50
  substrate, the self-perception mechanism doesn't fire — she
  attributes her answer to "being polite" not "being interested,"
  and the ladder rung is wasted.
- **Fork is too heavy → A14 otherness collapse.** "What's the worst
  thing an ex did to you" is technically a fork but lands as
  premature emotional disclosure. The qualifying register is light
  by design; weight here closes the desire-gap before proximity.
- **Question is googleable → A1 frame collapse + I6 DLV.** "What's
  your favorite restaurant in the city" is fishing dressed as a
  fork. She can answer it for any man on the app; her answer carries
  no A11 self-perception load because the action wasn't
  discriminating. The thread reverts to interview-ceiling.
- **Stacked fork → A2 cognitive load + A18 dilution.** Two questions
  in the same message reads as interrogation, splits her attention,
  and dilutes the A11 lock-in across answers. One fork per turn.
- **Fork without a take of his own → A19 agency leak + I7 frame
  loss.** If he asks "wine bars or hotel bars" and she replies "wine
  bars" and he says "cool, me too" the move evaporates. The fork is
  pre-suasion (A1) for a volley; without the volley the rung
  doesn't compound and the thread slides back into rapport-flat.
