## What it is

Ask for her *take* on something concrete — not "tell me about
yourself" (interview), not "what do you do for fun" (boring), but a
real opinion question that has a fork in it. Her answer reveals
something genuine about her, gives you texture to play with, and
moves the thread out of small-talk.

Examples:

- "ok serious question. wine bars or hotel bars."
- "tell me your hottest take on this city."
- "settle a debate I'm having: brunch or breakfast tacos."
- "what's the worst date idea you've heard with a straight face."

## When it works

- Thread is warm — she's reciprocating substantively, not just
  acknowledging.
- The question has a *fork* — she has to pick or take a stand. Not
  "what kinds of music do you like" (open-ended fish).
- The question is light — she can answer in a sentence or two.
- Her answer will tell you something usable for the next move.

## When it fails

- Used too early — premature in opener phase.
- Question is heavy ("what's your love language") — too earnest for
  qualifying.
- Stack of two questions in one message — interrogation again.
- Question is googleable / generic — back to interview voice.
